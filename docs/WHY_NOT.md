# Why Not

This document records what Playbook intentionally chooses not to become.

## We are not building

- Another addictive social network.
- A platform that sells student data.
- A tool that replaces educators, counselors, coaches, mentors, or families.
- A product optimized only for screen time.
- A disconnected résumé builder.
- A generic AI chatbot.

## Why

Playbook exists to create opportunity through verified growth, trusted relationships, and lifelong Scholar ownership.
