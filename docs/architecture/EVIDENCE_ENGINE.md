# Purpose

The Evidence Engine is responsible for transforming meaningful human experiences into trusted, verifiable, immutable evidence.

Evidence is the universal currency of Playbook.

Every Domain contributes Evidence.

Every Opportunity consumes Evidence.

Every Participant Record is built from Evidence.

Without Evidence, nothing meaningful exists inside the platform.

---

# Mission

Capture truth.

Verify truth.

Preserve truth.

Publish truth.

Never lose truth.

---

# Philosophy

People should not repeatedly prove themselves.

Evidence should travel with the Participant.

Evidence belongs to the Participant.

Organizations contribute Evidence.

Organizations never own Evidence.

Evidence survives schools.

Evidence survives employers.

Evidence survives teams.

Evidence survives ventures.

Evidence survives platforms.

Evidence belongs to a lifetime.

# Canonical Definition

Evidence is a verified representation of a meaningful participant experience that demonstrates knowledge, competency, achievement, contribution, performance, growth, or impact.

Evidence exists independently of the organization that helped produce it.
