# Purpose

This document defines how participants, mentors, advisors, coaches, organizations, administrators, and AI interact with the Playbook Human Development Operating System through Compass.

Compass is the universal interaction layer.

Every experience enters through Compass.

Every engine remains behind Compass.

---

# Interaction Philosophy

Participants should never think about engines.

Participants should never think about databases.

Participants should never think about architecture.

Participants should simply know

• where they are

• where they're going

• why

• what's next

Compass makes complex systems feel simple.

# Closing Principle

Compass is the human face of the Playbook Human Development Operating System.

Participants do not interact with engines, databases, or infrastructure.

They interact with a trusted guide that continuously helps them understand where they are, where they can go, and how to move forward.

Behind every interaction are deterministic engines, verified evidence, and explainable intelligence.

Compass turns platform intelligence into human progress.
