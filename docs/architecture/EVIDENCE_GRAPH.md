# Evidence Graph

Evidence does not exist in isolation.

Every Evidence item becomes a node within the Playbook Knowledge Graph.

Evidence may connect to multiple canonical entities simultaneously.

Examples include:

Participant

Competency

Organization

Relationship

Learning Outcome

Domain

Artifact

Goal

Opportunity

Plan

Event

Community

Venture

Team

Coach

Mentor

Certificate

Transcript

Badge

Evidence is never duplicated.

Connections are represented through graph relationships.

---

## Example

A single Pitch Competition may connect to:

Participant

↓

Evidence

↓

Entrepreneurship

Leadership

Communication

Financial Literacy

Innovation

Pitch Competition

Investor Feedback

Pitch Deck

Business Plan

Founder Competency

Opportunity

Planning Goal

One experience can strengthen many competencies.

---

## Competency Graph

Competencies evolve over time.

Multiple Evidence items strengthen the same competency.

Example

Public Speaking

├── Debate Competition

├── Founder Pitch

├── Classroom Presentation

├── Community Workshop

└── Conference Keynote

The competency becomes increasingly trustworthy through accumulated Evidence.

## Opportunity Traversal

The Opportunity Engine does not simply search for certificates.

Instead, it traverses the Knowledge Graph.

Example query:

Find Participants demonstrating:

Leadership

Communication

Innovation

Financial Literacy

Community Service

within the last three years.

This allows opportunities to be matched using demonstrated capability instead of isolated achievements.

## Compass Integration

Compass never reasons from isolated records.

Compass traverses the Knowledge Graph.

Typical traversal:

Participant

↓

Evidence

↓

Competencies

↓

Relationships

↓

Goals

↓

Plans

↓

Current Opportunities

↓

Recommended Next Steps

Compass explains graph relationships.

It never creates them.

---

## Graph Invariants

Evidence remains immutable.

Relationships remain versioned.

Nodes are never duplicated.

Connections are explainable.

Graph traversal never overrides platform Policies.

Permissions continue to be computed before traversal results are exposed.

Every graph edge is auditable.
