# Purpose

The Learning Domain defines how Participants acquire knowledge, develop competencies, demonstrate mastery, and generate verified evidence throughout their lifelong journey.

Learning is not limited to formal education.

Learning occurs anywhere meaningful growth happens.

Every learning experience contributes to the Participant Record through verified Evidence.

---

# Philosophy

Learning is lifelong.

Learning happens everywhere.

Schools are one provider.

Communities are providers.

Businesses are providers.

Mentors are providers.

Parents are providers.

Experience is a teacher.

Learning produces capability.

Capability produces opportunity.

Opportunity produces growth.

Growth produces new learning.

---

# Core Principles

Learning belongs to the Participant.

Organizations facilitate learning.

Evidence validates learning.

Competencies outlive courses.

Learning is measurable.

Learning is continuous.

Learning is contextual.

Learning generates opportunity.

---

# Canonical Definition

Learning is the structured process through which Participants acquire knowledge, skills, behaviors, competencies, and experiences that can be demonstrated through Evidence.

Learning is independent of institution.

Learning is participant-centered.
