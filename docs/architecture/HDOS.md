# Playbook Human Development Operating System

## Purpose

The Playbook HDOS powers every learner's journey from discovery through lifelong success.

Every engine has a single responsibility.

---

## Identity Engine

Owns authentication, roles, permissions, and verification.

---

## Participant Record Engine

Owns the Living Record for every participant.

---

## Evidence Engine

Owns transcripts, portfolios, certificates, resumes, and verified accomplishments.

---

## Academic Intelligence Engine

Transforms academic data into readiness insights.

---

## Athletics Engine

Owns athletic development and recruiting intelligence.

---

## Learning Engine

Owns courses, mastery, credentials, and skill progression.

---

## Opportunity Engine

Matches participants with scholarships, internships, colleges, careers, grants, and experiences.

---

## Relationship Engine

Owns mentors, coaches, counselors, parents, organizations, and community.

---

## Economy Engine

Owns rewards, coins, incentives, and marketplace systems.

---

## Planning Engine

Owns milestones, action plans, goals, and long-term planning.

---

## Compass Engine

Generates personalized recommendations and next best actions.

---

## Platform Intelligence

Owns Cartographer, Sentinel, Ledger, Archivist, Oracle, and system intelligence.

---

## Guiding Principles

- One source of truth
- One owner per responsibility
- AI assists, humans decide
- Every interaction creates value
- Evidence drives opportunity
- Privacy by design
