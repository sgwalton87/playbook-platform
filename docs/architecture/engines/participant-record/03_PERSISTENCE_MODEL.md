# Purpose

This document defines the persistence architecture for the Participant Record Engine.

The Participant Record Engine does not persist business truth.

Business truth remains within canonical Evidence.

Persistence exists to optimize retrieval, reporting, planning, and opportunity matching.

The Participant Record remains a projection.

---

# Philosophy

Persist projections.

Never duplicate truth.

Evidence remains canonical.

Participant Records remain reproducible.

Storage exists for performance—not authority.

---
