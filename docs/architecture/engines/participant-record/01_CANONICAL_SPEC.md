# Purpose

The Participant Record Engine is responsible for building and maintaining the Participant's lifelong, canonical development record.

The Participant Record is not a profile.

It is not a résumé.

It is not a transcript.

It is not a portfolio.

It is the complete, verified, longitudinal representation of a Participant's growth throughout their lifetime.

The Participant Record is built exclusively from verified Evidence.

---

# Mission

Aggregate truth.

Preserve history.

Represent growth.

Enable opportunity.

Never lose context.

---

# Philosophy

Participants evolve.

Records should evolve with them.

The Participant Record should tell the story of development, not merely store accomplishments.

It represents who the Participant has become.

Not merely what they have done.

---

# Why the Participant Record Exists

Traditional systems create fragmented records.

Schools maintain transcripts.

Employers maintain employment history.

Athletic organizations maintain statistics.

Businesses maintain founder history.

Governments maintain certifications.

The Participant Record unifies these into a single participant-owned record.
