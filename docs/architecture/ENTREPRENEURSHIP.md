# Purpose

The Entrepreneurship Domain defines how Participants discover opportunities, create ventures, build businesses, develop entrepreneurial competencies, secure resources, and generate lasting impact throughout their lifetime.

Entrepreneurship is a human development domain.

The objective is not simply to create companies.

The objective is to develop founders, innovators, builders, and leaders.

Entrepreneurship generates verified Evidence that contributes to the Participant Record.

---

# Philosophy

Ideas become ventures.

Ventures develop founders.

Founders develop leaders.

Leadership creates opportunity.

Success and failure both produce learning.

Entrepreneurship is lifelong.

Participants own their entrepreneurial journey.

Organizations support it.

# Canonical Definition

Entrepreneurship is the structured process through which Participants identify problems, develop solutions, build ventures, validate ideas, acquire resources, create value, and continuously develop entrepreneurial capability.

Entrepreneurship is participant-centered.

Ventures are vehicles for development.

# Closing Principle

Entrepreneurship is not measured by company valuation.

It is measured by the value Participants create, the problems they solve, the people they serve, and the leaders they become.

Every venture—whether it succeeds, pivots, or fails—contributes to the lifelong development of the Participant.

In Playbook, entrepreneurship is not merely the creation of businesses.

It is the continuous practice of turning ideas into impact.
