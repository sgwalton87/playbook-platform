# PBOS Governed Learning Intelligence Engine V1

## Purpose

Document the PBOS-ENGINE-LEARNING-001 implementation boundary for organizing learner-owned goals, competencies, skills, milestones, evidence, reflections, pathways, developmental mastery indicators, and advisory recommendations without replacing educators or learner agency.

## Ownership

Playbook OS Engineering, Learning Governance, and Constitutional Governance.

## Last Updated

July 26, 2026.

## Architecture

The learning domain validates constitutional Runtime Context, authorized learner identity, evidence provenance and permitted uses, and knowledge, strategy, and simulation inputs. Deterministic records preserve evidence ownership, source references, limitations, mentor relationships, and learner choice.

Skill progression distinguishes exposure, practice, demonstration, and mastery evidence. Completion is never equated with mastery. Reflections remain learner-authored and learner-owned. Pathways and recommendations remain advisory and create no educational or access decision.

The lifecycle is `OBSERVING → ASSESSING_CONTEXT → IDENTIFYING_GOALS → MAPPING_COMPETENCIES → BUILDING_PATHWAY → TRACKING_PROGRESS → REFLECTING → RECOMMENDING_NEXT_STEPS → MENTOR_REVIEW → ARCHIVED`. Stages cannot be skipped, and archival requires recorded human review.

## Governance and Safety Boundary

PBOS may organize learning data, identify evidence, suggest pathways, and support reflection. Authorized humans retain educational, evaluation, certification, disciplinary, admissions, career, and access decisions. The engine cannot determine potential or worth, rank learners, expose unauthorized data, claim unsupported mastery, manufacture personal meaning, or replace educators and mentors.
