## Architecture Implemented

The `pbos/athletics` domain defines athlete identity and profile records, performance evidence, verified athletic organizations, recruiting alignment, global mobility pathways, informational readiness, verified representation, Athletes Abroad programs, deterministic reporting, governance routing, and lifecycle enforcement. Inputs are bound to one verified Runtime Context and one authorized athlete owner.

## Evidence and Opportunity Boundary

Performance evidence remains distinct from athlete claims and retains event, statistic, source, classification, authority, timestamp, limitations, and provenance. Displayed statistics require matching evidence. Recruiting opportunities require verified organization identity and source provenance; alignments are advisory and cannot select athletes, make roster decisions, or guarantee outcomes.

## Global Mobility and Athletes Abroad

Mobility records preserve origin, destination, pathway, eligibility considerations, documentation, preparation needs, resources, evidence, and uncertainty. Athletes Abroad programs require verified partners, international scope, and consent evidence. PBOS creates neither eligibility decisions nor automatic connections.

## Governance and Safety Boundary

PBOS may organize athletic journeys, preserve verified evidence, explain alignment, support international preparation, and surface verified resources. It cannot rank athletes, calculate athlete value, fabricate statistics, expose private information, infer protected characteristics, replace coaches or scouts, authorize agents, select teams, decide eligibility, admission, contracts, or employment, or guarantee scholarships, rosters, mobility, or professional outcomes.

## Representation and Consent

Agents, advisors, recruiters, and athlete advocates require verified identity, verified organization, explicit `REPRESENT` permission, a consented ecosystem relationship, and matching relationship evidence. Representation records remain non-decisional.

## Lifecycle

The enforced lifecycle is `CREATED`, `DEVELOPING`, `SHOWCASE_READY`, `OPPORTUNITY_SEARCH`, `CONNECTED`, `TRANSITIONING`, `ACTIVE`, `POST_ATHLETIC`, and `ARCHIVED`. Skipped transitions fail closed. Showcase, connection, transition, active, post-athletic, and archival stages require identified human authority and evidence.
