# PBOS Governed Opportunity Intelligence Engine V1

## Purpose

Document the PBOS-ENGINE-OPPORTUNITY-001 implementation boundary for organizing verified opportunities, requirements, eligibility evidence, alignment signals, deadlines, and application pathways without selecting recipients or guaranteeing outcomes.

## Ownership

Playbook OS Engineering, Opportunity Governance, and Constitutional Governance.

## Last Updated

July 26, 2026.

## Architecture

The opportunity domain validates constitutional Runtime Context, authorized person identity, credential, mastery, learning, and discovery intelligence, verified organizations, visible criteria, source provenance, and deadlines. Deterministic opportunity, eligibility, alignment, recommendation, pathway, deadline, and report records disclose evidence and limitations.

Eligibility distinguishes meets requirements, may be eligible, and unknown without assumptions. Alignment explains every factor and states that relevance does not guarantee success. Recommendations remain advisory, submit no application, and confer no entitlement.

The lifecycle is `DISCOVERING → VALIDATING_SOURCE → ANALYZING_REQUIREMENTS → ASSESSING_ALIGNMENT → PRESENTING_OPTIONS → HUMAN_SELECTION → APPLICATION_SUPPORT → OUTCOME_RECORDING → ARCHIVED`. Human-controlled stages require recorded authority and evidence.

## Governance and Safety Boundary

PBOS may find opportunities, explain requirements, organize preparation, and surface pathways. Human authorities retain admissions, hiring, scholarship selection, program acceptance, and employment decisions. The engine cannot select winners, deny access, rank people, fabricate opportunities, hide criteria, expose private records, discriminate, submit applications, or guarantee acceptance.
