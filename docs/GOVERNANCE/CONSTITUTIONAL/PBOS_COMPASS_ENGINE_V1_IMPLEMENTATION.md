## Architecture Implemented
The `pbos/compass` domain defines person-owned goals, typed journey insights, evidence-bound resources, advisory next actions, consented support connections, deterministic reports, governance routing, and lifecycle enforcement across academic, athletic, career, financial-organization, and personal-development guidance.

## Agency and Safety Boundary
Every next action is a `POSSIBLE_NEXT_STEP`, never a required action, and supports accept, dismiss, modify, reflect, and feedback choices. Compass cannot invent goals, infer aspirations, rank, predict, decide, guarantee outcomes, provide regulated financial advice, or replace counselors, mentors, coaches, families, and advisors.

## Evidence and Support Governance
Recommendations retain source engine, evidence, confidence, limitations, resources, and support options. Support access requires verified identity, explicit permissions, active purpose-specific consent, and a consented evidence-backed relationship.

## Lifecycle
The lifecycle is `CREATED`, `UNDERSTANDING`, `MAPPING`, `IDENTIFYING_OPTIONS`, `PRESENTING_GUIDANCE`, `USER_REVIEW`, `ACTION_SELECTED`, `REFLECTION`, and `ARCHIVED`. User stages require person authority and evidence.
