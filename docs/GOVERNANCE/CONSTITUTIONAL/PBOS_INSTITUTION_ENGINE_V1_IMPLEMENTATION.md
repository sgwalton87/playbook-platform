## Architecture Implemented
The `pbos/institution` domain defines verified institutions, human approvals, representatives, programs, consented participant relationships, cohorts, role-based dashboards, impact records, deterministic reporting, and governed lifecycle transitions.

## Governance and Safety
Institution identity requires verified Ecosystem evidence and human approval. Representatives require active organization-bound roles and access approval. Participant relationships require verified Identity, purpose, permissions, consent, and evidence. External sharing and administration remain human-controlled. Institutions never own participant data, sell data, bypass consent, rank people, make admissions, employment, or recruiting decisions, or claim unsupported causal impact.

## Lifecycle
The lifecycle is `CREATED`, `VERIFYING`, `VERIFIED`, `ACTIVE`, `PROGRAM_OPERATING`, `REVIEWING`, `SUSPENDED`, and `ARCHIVED`; transitions require identified human authority and evidence.
