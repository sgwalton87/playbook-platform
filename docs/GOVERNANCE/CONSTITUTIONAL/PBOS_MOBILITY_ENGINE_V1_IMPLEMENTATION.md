## Architecture Implemented

The `pbos/mobility` domain defines person-owned journeys, classified transition statements, verified origins and destinations, requirements, readiness, documents, support authorization, deterministic reports, governance routing, and lifecycle enforcement. Inputs preserve Runtime Context, Identity, Academic, Athletic, Portfolio, Opportunity, Ecosystem, and Communication provenance.

## Mobility Journey and Readiness

Journeys retain category, origin, destination, goal, timeline, evidence, requirements, support, opportunities, documents, limitations, and provenance. Transition items distinguish `FACT`, `REQUIREMENT`, `INFORMATION_GAP`, `POSSIBLE_NEXT_STEP`, and `RECOMMENDATION`. Readiness is limited to `PREPARING`, `IN_PROGRESS`, `INFORMATION_NEEDED`, and `READY_FOR_REVIEW`; it is informational and never a success guarantee.

## Athletic, Academic, Career, and International Mobility

Athletic pathways support domestic and international preparation without team selection, recruiting decisions, agency, contract negotiation, or roster guarantees. Academic pathways organize verified requirements without admissions decisions or acceptance guarantees. Career and entrepreneurship pathways organize evidence and opportunities without hiring decisions or candidate ranking. International records preserve country, region, cultural and documentation awareness, source authority, verification, and limitations without providing immigration or legal advice.

## Support and Communication Boundary

Mentors, coaches, counselors, organizations, and advisors require an active consented Ecosystem relationship, explicit `CONNECT` permission, evidence, and an authorized Communication record. PBOS cannot infer or silently create support relationships.

## Governance and Safety Boundary

The engine rejects unsupported transitions, fabricated destinations, missing evidence, unauthorized access, outcome guarantees, recruiting, admissions, or employment decisions, legal determinations, ranking, privacy bypasses, and invalid support. Human authority remains required for every consequential outcome.

## Lifecycle

The lifecycle is `CREATED`, `EXPLORING`, `PLANNING`, `PREPARING`, `CONNECTING`, `TRANSITIONING`, `REFLECTING`, and `ARCHIVED`. Each transition requires an identified human actor, evidence, and a valid timestamp.
