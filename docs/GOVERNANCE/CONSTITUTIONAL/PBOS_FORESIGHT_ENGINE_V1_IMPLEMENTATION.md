# PBOS Governed Foresight Intelligence Engine V1

## Purpose

Document the PBOS-ENGINE-FORESIGHT-001 implementation boundary for deterministic, evidence-based identification of long-term patterns, emerging signals, possible future conditions, and preparation questions. Foresight is advisory exploration, not prediction or strategic authority.

## Ownership

Playbook OS Engineering, Executive Leadership, and Constitutional Governance.

## Last Updated

July 26, 2026.

## Architecture

The foresight domain validates constitutional Runtime Context and evidence from discovery, institutional knowledge, simulation, and strategy intelligence. It produces content-derived trends, near-, medium-, and long-term horizon analysis, emerging signals, possible future conditions, preparedness opportunities, provenance, and reports.

Trends are explicitly non-predictive. Horizons do not express probability. Recurrence does not establish causation. Future conditions disclose assumptions, limitations, and uncertainty. Preparedness records ask questions and identify resources to evaluate without creating plans or commitments.

The lifecycle is `OBSERVING → COLLECTING_SIGNALS → ANALYZING_PATTERNS → ASSESSING_HORIZONS → MODELING_FUTURE_CONDITIONS → IDENTIFYING_PREPARATION_AREAS → REPORTING → GOVERNANCE_REVIEW → ARCHIVED`. Stages cannot be skipped, and archival requires recorded human approval.

## Governance and Safety Boundary

PBOS may identify trends, organize evidence, describe possible futures, and recommend preparation questions. Human approval is required for strategic direction changes, investments, organizational commitments, policy changes, and constitutional implications. The engine cannot select direction, allocate resources, declare certainty, invent signals, or execute strategy.
