## Architecture Implemented

The `pbos/ecosystem` domain defines typed entities, consent-bound relationships, mentorships, support networks, verified resources, advisory alignments, governance routing, deterministic reports, and a fail-closed lifecycle. Inputs are bound to one verified Runtime Context and an authorized user-controlled profile.

## Governance and Safety Boundary

PBOS may organize verified ecosystem evidence, explain potential support relevance, and surface navigation options. It cannot accept relationships, require mentorship participation, represent organizations, establish partnerships, rank people, score influence, expose private networks, fabricate connections, or guarantee outcomes. Connections remain visible to authorized participants and require evidence, explicit permission, and consent.

## Determinism and Provenance

Artifacts use canonical context digests and content-derived identifiers. Reports retain source report identifiers, evidence references, entity ownership, permissions, verification state, limitations, and the exact advisory statement that a potential support connection is not a guaranteed outcome.

## Lifecycle

The enforced lifecycle is `DISCOVERING`, `VERIFYING`, `MAPPING`, `CONNECTING`, `SUPPORTING`, `REVIEWING`, and `ARCHIVED`. Skipped transitions fail closed; relationship-affecting stages require identified human authority and evidence.
