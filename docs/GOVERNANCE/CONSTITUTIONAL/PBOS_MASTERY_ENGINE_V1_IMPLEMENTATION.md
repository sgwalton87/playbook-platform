# PBOS Governed Mastery Intelligence Engine V1

## Purpose

Document the PBOS-ENGINE-MASTERY-001 implementation boundary for organizing verified evidence of demonstrated capability, achievements, portfolios, credentials, and developmental competency progression without ranking people or determining worth or potential.

## Ownership

Playbook OS Engineering, Learning Governance, and Constitutional Governance.

## Last Updated

July 26, 2026.

## Architecture

The mastery domain validates constitutional Runtime Context, learner identity and authorization, evidence provenance and permissions, approved human reviewers, learning intelligence, and institutional knowledge. Deterministic demonstrations, achievements, portfolios, credentials, mastery records, and reports retain evidence sources, owners, timestamps, validation records, context, and limitations.

Progression is evidence-gated from introduced through recognized mastery. Demonstrated and higher progression requires approved human validation. Credential completion remains distinct from demonstration and mastery. PBOS never self-certifies capability.

The lifecycle is `OBSERVING → COLLECTING_EVIDENCE → VALIDATING_EVIDENCE → MAPPING_COMPETENCIES → ASSESSING_DEMONSTRATION → UPDATING_MASTERY → REPORTING → HUMAN_REVIEW → ARCHIVED`. Stages cannot be skipped, and archival requires recorded human approval.

## Governance and Safety Boundary

PBOS may organize evidence, identify competencies, summarize contextual accomplishments, and support learner-owned portfolios. Human authority is required for credentials, certifications, admissions, employment, and opportunity access. The engine cannot rank value, infer intelligence or destiny, fabricate achievement, expose unauthorized evidence, replace reviewers, or convert completion into mastery.
