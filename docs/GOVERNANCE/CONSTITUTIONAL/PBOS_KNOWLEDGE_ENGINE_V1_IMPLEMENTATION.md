# PBOS Governed Institutional Knowledge Engine V1

## Purpose

Document the PBOS-ENGINE-KNOWLEDGE-001 implementation boundary for preserving, organizing, connecting, retrieving, and explaining institutional knowledge from verified evidence. The engine does not invent memory, rewrite history, or create authority.

## Ownership

Playbook OS Engineering, Records Governance, and Constitutional Governance.

## Last Updated

July 26, 2026.

## Architecture

The knowledge domain validates constitutional Runtime Context and evidence provenance before creating deterministic entities, relationships, precedents, lessons, graphs, retrieval results, and reports. Relationships require explicit source and target entities, evidence, confidence, and limitations. Retrieval retains contradictory evidence and uncertainty.

Precedents describe what occurred without prescribing repetition. Lessons are knowledge artifacts rather than commands. Content-derived identifiers and stable ordering make equivalent inputs reproducible.

The lifecycle is `OBSERVING → INGESTING → VALIDATING → CONNECTING → INDEXING → RETRIEVING → REPORTING → GOVERNANCE_REVIEW → ARCHIVED`. Stages cannot be skipped, and archival requires recorded human approval.

## Governance and Safety Boundary

PBOS may organize, connect, summarize, and retrieve. Human approval is required for classification changes, historical interpretation changes, ownership changes, deletion or archival decisions, and authoritative lessons. The engine rejects fabricated knowledge, history modification, missing provenance, unsupported relationships, invalid context, and governance bypass.
