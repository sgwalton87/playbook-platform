# PBOS Governed Strategy Intelligence Engine V1

## Purpose

Document the PBOS-ENGINE-STRATEGY-001 implementation boundary for deterministic, evidence-based, advisory strategic analysis. The engine organizes verified mission authority, constitutional context, discovery intelligence, tradeoffs, scenarios, and governance requirements without choosing priorities or committing resources.

## Ownership

Playbook OS Engineering, Executive Leadership, and Constitutional Governance.

## Last Updated

July 26, 2026.

## Architecture

The strategy domain validates Runtime Context, verified and sourced mission context, discovery evidence, and option evidence before modeling. It produces content-derived strategic option and report identifiers, explicit mission alignment, disclosed advisory priority scores, uncertainty-preserving tradeoffs, and scenarios that separate known facts from possible outcomes, assumptions, and unknown factors.

The lifecycle is `OBSERVING → ANALYZING → ALIGNING → MODELING_OPTIONS → EVALUATING_TRADEOFFS → REPORTING → RECOMMENDING → GOVERNANCE_REVIEW → ARCHIVED`. Stages cannot be skipped. Archival after review requires recorded human approval and evidence.

## Governance and Safety Boundary

PBOS may analyze, compare, model, and recommend. Human authority is required for strategic priorities, resource commitments, organizational direction, constitutional interpretation, major investments, and external commitments. Alignment and scoring are not decisions. The engine cannot allocate resources, approve strategies, claim guaranteed outcomes, or convert recommendations into approvals.
