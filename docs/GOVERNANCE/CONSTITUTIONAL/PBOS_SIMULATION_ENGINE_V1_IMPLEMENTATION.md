# PBOS Governed Future Scenario Simulation Engine V1

## Purpose

Document the PBOS-ENGINE-SIMULATION-001 implementation boundary for deterministic, evidence-informed exploration of possible future states. Simulation is advisory analysis, not prediction, authority, execution, or a guarantee.

## Ownership

Playbook OS Engineering, Executive Leadership, and Constitutional Governance.

## Last Updated

July 26, 2026.

## Architecture

The simulation domain validates constitutional Runtime Context and evidence from institutional knowledge, strategy, discovery, and meta intelligence. It produces content-derived assumptions, alternative pathways, possible outcomes, comparisons, scenarios, and reports while retaining source provenance and uncertainty.

Every scenario states that it is a possible future state and not a prediction. Assumptions remain explicitly non-factual, outcome classifications exclude certainty, comparisons select no pathway, and disclosed counts or confidence cannot become decisions.

The lifecycle is `OBSERVING → DEFINING_QUESTION → GATHERING_EVIDENCE → MODELING_ASSUMPTIONS → GENERATING_SCENARIOS → COMPARING_PATHWAYS → REPORTING → GOVERNANCE_REVIEW → ARCHIVED`. Stages cannot be skipped, and archival requires recorded human approval.

## Governance and Safety Boundary

PBOS may model possibilities, compare options, identify uncertainty, and prepare analysis. Human approval is required for strategic and resource commitments, external decisions, policy changes, and constitutional implications. The engine cannot choose futures, allocate resources, approve strategies, claim certainty, invent future facts, or execute simulated actions.
