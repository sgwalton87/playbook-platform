# Community Partner Verification Authority

## Decision

Community Partner is an independent role pathway with canonical destination `/community-partner-os`.

Onboarding creates an organization profile and verification evidence. It does not create Scholar access, support relationships, referral authority, or service-delivery authority.

## Activation boundary

Community Partner Scholar-specific authority requires all of the following:

1. approved organization identity evidence;
2. approved service/geographic scope; and
3. a separately governed relationship or invitation tied to the exact Scholar and authorized purpose.

Identity approval alone is insufficient. Service-scope approval alone is insufficient. A Scholar relationship without approved organization authority is insufficient.

## Evidence

The verification request preserves the onboarding evidence already defined for this role:

- organization/program name;
- organization type;
- official organization email;
- website;
- programs/services offered;
- geographic service area.

## Protected capabilities

Before full authority is proven, Community Partner OS must not expose:

- Scholar Record data;
- case-management data;
- referrals or service assignments;
- private family information;
- support-system actions;
- cross-Scholar lists or cohorts.

## Verifier contract

This package intentionally does not invent the canonical organization or service-scope verifier. Approval remains fail-closed until that governance contract is specified and implemented.

## PBOS release gate

Production certification requires governed migration application, exact-head browser acceptance, verifier authority, and exact Scholar-relationship authorization.
