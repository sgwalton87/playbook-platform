# Athlete Abroad Global Readiness Authority

## Decision

Athlete Abroad is a self-owned athlete pathway with canonical destination `/athlete-abroad-os`.

The athlete owns their Playbook/Scholar Record. Global-readiness review does not transfer ownership and does not create institutional authority over the athlete.

## Global capability boundary

Jurisdiction-sensitive global capabilities require:

1. authenticated self-owned Athlete Abroad identity;
2. reviewed global-readiness evidence; and
3. approved jurisdiction scope for the applicable destination/context.

## Evidence

The readiness review preserves the role-specific onboarding evidence already defined for Athlete Abroad:

- destination regions;
- passport readiness;
- eligibility context;
- international support needs.

## Protected capabilities

Until readiness and jurisdiction scope are approved, the OS must keep restricted any workflow that purports to determine or execute:

- visa or immigration eligibility;
- sport-governing-body eligibility;
- contract execution or legal advice;
- tax determinations;
- regulated agent representation;
- jurisdiction-specific recruiting or participation authority.

The athlete's own academic, athletic, and profile records remain self-owned and should not be hidden merely because a global readiness review is pending.

## Human agency

Playbook may organize evidence, explain requirements, and provide recommendations. Human and institutional authorities remain responsible for legal, eligibility, contract, tax, and participation decisions.

## PBOS release gate

Production certification requires governed migration application, exact-head browser acceptance, and a defined jurisdiction-review authority for any capability that moves beyond general readiness guidance.
