# Playbook Legacy

Playbook exists so every Scholar can own their story and use it to unlock opportunity.

## Non-Negotiables

- Scholars own their Portfolio.
- Student data must be protected.
- AI supports human relationships.
- Verified achievements matter.
- Opportunity is the outcome.
- Communities are partners, not customers alone.

## Legacy Goal

Playbook should help build a world where no Scholar loses an opportunity because they did not know how to tell, verify, or share their story.
