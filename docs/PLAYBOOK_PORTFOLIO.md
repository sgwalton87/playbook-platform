# Vision

The Playbook Portfolio™ is the verified digital identity of every Scholar.

It automatically documents a Scholar's academic, athletic, leadership, financial, professional, and personal growth throughout their educational journey.

The Portfolio is not a profile.

It is a living, AI-powered portfolio that grows with the Scholar from middle school through college and into their career.

Everything a Scholar accomplishes inside Playbook strengthens their Portfolio.

---

# North Star

> Every Scholar graduates with a verified digital portfolio that opens doors to college, careers, entrepreneurship, scholarships, internships, and lifelong opportunity.
