# Future History

This is a vision exercise written from the future.

## 2036 Vision

By 2036, Playbook has helped millions of Scholars build verified portfolios that unlock college, career, entrepreneurship, mentorship, and financial opportunity.

Scholars no longer start over every year. Their achievements, skills, service, recommendations, and milestones follow them throughout life.

Educators, families, mentors, employers, and community organizations contribute to a shared ecosystem of verified opportunity.

Playbook is remembered not as another app, but as infrastructure for upward mobility.
