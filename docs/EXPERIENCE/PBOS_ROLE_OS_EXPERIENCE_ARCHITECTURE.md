# PBOS Role OS Experience Architecture

**Purpose:** Define shared composition across Scholar, Parent, Mentor, Coach, Counselor, and Institution operating systems.  
**Owner:** Playbook OS Product and Experience Architecture  
**Last Updated:** July 30, 2026

| Role OS | Primary responsibility | Prohibited authority |
|---|---|---|
| Scholar | Own personal journey, evidence, and choices | Impersonate institutions or verifiers |
| Parent | Support consent-aware growth | Override scholar agency outside lawful scope |
| Mentor | Provide bounded guidance | Change records or approve credentials without authority |
| Coach | Support athletic development | Infer academic or medical authority |
| Counselor | Guide academic and career planning | Commit decisions for the scholar |
| Institution | Deliver governed services and verification | Use population access to bypass individual permissions |

All role systems inherit shared identity, evidence, recommendation, consent, accessibility, navigation, state, and audit contracts. Cross-role workflows use explicit invitations, scoped permissions, expiry, visibility, and revocation. No recommendation or relationship silently expands access.
