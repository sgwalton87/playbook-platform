# Role Experience Specifications

**Purpose:** Adapt one Playbook brand and component system to governed role responsibilities.  
**Owner:** Playbook OS Product and Experience Architecture  
**Last Updated:** July 30, 2026

| Role | Primary emphasis | Navigation adaptation | Authority boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Scholar | Identity, journey, action, opportunity, growth | Home, Journey, Record, Opportunities, Network | Own choices and scoped personal visibility |
| Scholar Athlete | Education, career, athletics, development | Academic status precedes athletic pathway | Cannot trade academic truth for recruiting presentation |
| Parent | Support, consent, celebration, safety | Scholar-scoped support views | Cannot silently replace scholar agency |
| Counselor | Academic and career guidance | Caseload, readiness, planning, referrals | Advice and authorized institutional updates only |
| Coach | Athletic development and support | Team, development, pathway, communication | No implied academic, medical, or guardian authority |
| Mentor | Goals, reflection, introductions, encouragement | Mentees, sessions, actions, resources | Consent-bound guidance; no canonical record mutation |
| Institution | Services, verification, population outcomes | Programs, verification, support operations | Tenant and role scope; no unrestricted individual access |

All roles inherit the brand constitution, semantic tokens, component IDs, complete states, accessibility, responsive behavior, evidence language, and audit rules. Adaptation changes workflow and information emphasis, not visual identity or constitutional authority.
