# MARKETPLACE-STORE

**Purpose:** Governed achievement and resource ecosystem.  
**Primary role:** Scholar  
**Secondary roles:** Parent, institution

**Hierarchy:** Earned balance and eligibility -> relevant resources and experiences -> rewards -> merchandise -> transaction evidence.  
**Components:** PB-NAV-001, PB-CARD-001, PB-BADGE-001, PB-MODULE-001.  
**Data:** Item identity, provider, price or reward requirement, availability, entitlement, fulfillment, refund policy.  
**Actions:** Review, save, request approval where required, redeem or purchase through governed commerce.  
**Permissions:** Age, organization, payment, entitlement, and guardian controls.  
**Navigation:** `/store`; item, checkout, order, and support exits.  
**APIs/Database:** Certified catalog, entitlement, payment boundary, fulfillment and audit.  
**States:** Loading, empty catalog, provider/payment error, success, purchase restricted, financial privacy.  
**Responsive/Accessibility:** Mobile preserves complete disclosure and confirmation; desktop supports catalog scanning; pricing and controls are screen-reader explicit.
