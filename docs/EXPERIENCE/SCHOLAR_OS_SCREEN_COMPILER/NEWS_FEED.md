# NEWS-FEED

**Purpose:** Personalized opportunity intelligence stream.  
**Primary role:** Scholar  
**Secondary roles:** Scholar Athlete, parent, counselor

**Hierarchy:** Time-sensitive verified opportunities -> programs and events -> career opportunities -> learning content -> community updates.  
**Components:** PB-NAV-001, PB-OPPORTUNITY-001, PB-CARD-001, PB-COMPASS-001.  
**Data:** Source, provenance, eligibility, expiration, recommendation reason, confidence, saved state.  
**Actions:** Review source, save, dismiss, provide feedback, open opportunity.  
**Permissions:** Role and age-aware feed visibility; no hidden sensitive targeting.  
**Navigation:** `/feed`; exits to opportunity, event, course, or source evidence.  
**APIs/Database:** Feed projection and opportunity sources; interaction audit.  
**States:** Loading, no trusted items, partial-source error, success, restricted content, personalization privacy.  
**Responsive/Accessibility:** Linear mobile stream; desktop filters remain secondary; headings and article semantics support assistive technology.
