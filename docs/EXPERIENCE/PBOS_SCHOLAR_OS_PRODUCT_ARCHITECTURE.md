# PBOS Scholar OS Product Architecture

**Purpose:** Define Scholar OS as Playbook's first governed human development operating system.  
**Owner:** Playbook OS Product and Experience Architecture  
**Last Updated:** July 30, 2026

## Product Decision

Scholar OS organizes identity, current reality, desired future, available paths, explainable actions, human support, and measured growth. It consumes Scholar Record and governed intelligence; it cannot create facts, permissions, authority, engine activation, or irreversible decisions.

## Operating Model

| Human question | Product surface | Truth source |
|---|---|---|
| Who am I? | Identity and mission | Scholar-confirmed identity and Scholar Record |
| Where am I? | Current reality | Evidence-backed academic, athletic, and growth state |
| Where am I going? | Goals and paths | Scholar-confirmed goals |
| What can help? | Opportunities | Sourced, eligible, expiring provider evidence |
| What should I do next? | Guided actions | Evidence, reasoning, confidence, human confirmation |
| Who supports me? | Human network | Consent-bound, scoped relationships |
| How am I growing? | Milestones and outcomes | Verified milestone and outcome evidence |

## Paths

Academic covers courses, credits, A-G and graduation requirements, college readiness, applications, and milestones. Athletic covers identity, history, achievement, development, recruiting readiness, and opportunities. Career, Opportunity, Financial Literacy, Mentorship, and Growth paths share the same evidence and agency contract.

## Intelligence Contract

Recommendations must expose source, evidence, reasoning, confidence, expected impact, alternatives, and confirmation. AI may summarize, organize, explain, and identify possibilities. It cannot rank human worth, decide a future, fabricate eligibility, or replace trusted people.

## Permissions And Privacy

The scholar owns personal goals and choices. Parent, mentor, coach, counselor, and institution visibility requires explicit scope, consent identity, expiry, and revocation. Sensitive domains default to private. Role access does not imply record ownership.

## Responsive And Accessible Experience

Mobile prioritizes current state, next action, evidence capture, opportunity deadlines, and support. Desktop adds path comparison and planning. Interfaces preserve semantic hierarchy, keyboard access, visible focus, assistive-technology labels, reduced-motion support, readable contrast, and non-color status meaning.
