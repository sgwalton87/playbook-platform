# PBOS Scholar OS User Journey Map

**Purpose:** Define the first end-to-end human development workflow.  
**Owner:** Playbook OS Product and Experience Architecture  
**Last Updated:** July 30, 2026

## Journey

```text
Current Reality
  -> Scholar reviews source evidence
  -> Scholar confirms identity and desired future
  -> PBOS presents available paths
  -> PBOS explains bounded recommendations
  -> Scholar accepts, modifies, or rejects
  -> Governed action records confirmation
  -> Progress captures milestone evidence
  -> Outcome Evaluation measures change
  -> Scholar and supporters reflect and adapt
```

## Stages

| Stage | Scholar need | PBOS responsibility | Human control |
|---|---|---|---|
| Orientation | Understand what the system knows | Separate verified, self-reported, stale, and missing truth | Correct, hide, or contest |
| Direction | Define a meaningful future | Structure goals without choosing them | Confirm, revise, or reject |
| Pathfinding | See credible options | Explain paths, constraints, evidence, and uncertainty | Compare and choose |
| Action | Know the next useful step | Recommend reversible actions with confidence | Accept, modify, defer, reject |
| Support | Engage trusted people | Enforce consent, role, visibility, and expiry | Invite, scope, revoke |
| Progress | Understand growth | Correlate milestones with evidence | Confirm interpretation |
| Outcome | Determine whether life improved | Measure intended and adverse outcomes | Decide what happens next |

## Recovery Paths

Missing evidence leads to evidence collection, not fabricated completion. Ineligible opportunities explain the criteria and appeal path. Revoked consent removes access immediately. Failed action preserves history and offers recovery. Conflicting evidence is marked disputed and excluded from consequential recommendations.

## Accessibility And Device Continuity

The journey works without pointer, color, animation, or large display. Mobile and desktop preserve the same decision and permission state. Interrupted workflows resume from the last trusted, human-confirmed step.
