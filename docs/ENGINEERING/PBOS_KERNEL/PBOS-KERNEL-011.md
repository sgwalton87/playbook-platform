# Constitutional Planning Engine

## Purpose

The Constitutional Planning Engine transforms mission into executable work.

It does not generate implementation.

It generates the constitutionally correct execution plan.

Every plan shall be deterministic, explainable, reproducible, governed, and evidence-backed.

---

# Mission

Convert goals into governed execution.

The Planning Engine continuously evaluates:

Mission

↓

Objectives

↓

Current Repository State

↓

Constitutional Authority

↓

Capabilities

↓

Dependencies

↓

Execution Context

↓

Evidence

↓

Risk

↓

Priority

↓

Execution Plan

---

# Guiding Principle

Planning is reasoning constrained by constitutional authority.

A valid plan is one that:

- Advances the mission.
- Respects constitutional authority.
- Satisfies dependencies.
- Preserves governance.
- Produces measurable outcomes.

---

# Planning Hierarchy

The engine plans at six levels:

Mission

↓

Objectives

↓

Strategies

↓

Projects

↓

Workflows

↓

Tasks

Every lower level inherits authority from the level above it.

# Planning Constraints

The engine shall never:

Skip dependencies

Ignore constitutional authority

Duplicate existing capabilities

Recommend contradictory work

Create orphaned tasks

Violate governance

---

# Priority Model

Priority is determined by:

Mission Impact

Constitutional Authority

Dependency Criticality

Repository Health

Risk Reduction

Capability Reuse

Implementation Cost

Expected Value

Priority is evidence-based.

---

# Dependency Resolution

Every task shall identify:

Required predecessor tasks

Required capabilities

Required artifacts

Required approvals

Required evidence

Required governance gates

Planning shall fail if dependencies cannot be resolved.

---

# Plan Scoring

Every candidate plan is evaluated for:

Mission Alignment

Governance Compliance

Architectural Consistency

Complexity

Reuse

Risk

Expected Impact

Time to Completion

Highest constitutional score wins.

---

# Explainability

Every plan shall answer:

Why this objective?

Why this order?

Why these dependencies?

Why these capabilities?

Why now?

What constitutional authority applies?
