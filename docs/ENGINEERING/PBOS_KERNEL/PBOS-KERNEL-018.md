# Knowledge Subsystem Constitution

## Purpose

The Knowledge Subsystem is the constitutional knowledge authority of PBOS.

Its responsibility is to organize, preserve, validate, relate, and expose every piece of institutional knowledge required by PBOS.

Knowledge is not merely stored.

Knowledge is governed.

Knowledge is versioned.

Knowledge is explainable.

Knowledge is constitutionally traceable.

---

# Mission

Maintain a complete, authoritative, explainable knowledge model of PBOS.

Every constitutional decision shall be supported by structured knowledge.

Every recommendation shall be explainable through connected knowledge.

Every artifact shall be discoverable through constitutional relationships.
