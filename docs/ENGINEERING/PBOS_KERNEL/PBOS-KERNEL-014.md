# Constitutional Evidence Engine

## Purpose

The Constitutional Evidence Engine verifies that constitutional work has actually been completed.

Planning alone does not create truth.

Execution alone does not create truth.

Evidence creates truth.

Every constitutional assertion shall be supported by verifiable evidence before PBOS recognizes it as complete.

---

# Mission

Transform claimed work into verified work.

The engine continuously evaluates whether constitutional requirements have been satisfied through observable, reproducible, and auditable evidence.

---

# Constitutional Principle

Verified Over Claimed.

No constitutional state transition may occur without sufficient evidence.

---

# Responsibilities

The engine shall:

Verify execution.

Validate evidence.

Measure completeness.

Detect unsupported claims.

Determine certification readiness.

Record provenance.

Preserve audit history.

Produce constitutional confidence scores.
