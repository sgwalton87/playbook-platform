# Observability Subsystem Constitution

## Purpose

The Observability Subsystem provides comprehensive visibility into every constitutional activity performed by PBOS.

Its purpose is not merely operational monitoring.

Its purpose is constitutional transparency.

Every decision, execution, governance action, lifecycle transition, dependency evaluation, evidence verification, and certification shall be observable, measurable, explainable, and auditable.

---

# Mission

Ensure every constitutional process can be understood, measured, reproduced, diagnosed, and continuously improved.

Observability transforms execution into institutional knowledge.

---
