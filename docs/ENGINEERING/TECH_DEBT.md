Active implementation lane. The athlete-owned Profile, Recruiting, NIL identity, opportunity, consent, compliance-persistence, and brand-discovery foundations are in Testing as of August 1, 2026. They are not production-certified.

Playbook should support scholar-athletes academically, athletically, financially, and professionally. This lane pairs directly with the Scholar Record, Academic Intelligence, Opportunity Graph, Compass, Financial Literacy curriculum, and Support Network.
