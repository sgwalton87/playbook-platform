# Purpose

The PBOS Objective Registry Operations Framework defines the operational model required to maintain, monitor, govern, and support the Objective Registry throughout its lifecycle.

The purpose of this document is to ensure the Objective Registry operates as an enterprise governance capability rather than an unmanaged collection of strategic requests.


---

# Operational Philosophy

The Objective Registry represents strategic intent entering the PBOS ecosystem.

Operational excellence requires:

- predictable governance,
- continuous validation,
- transparent ownership,
- durable history,
- reliable reporting,
- controlled evolution.


The Objective Registry must remain trusted as platform complexity increases.


---

# Operational Responsibilities

The Objective Registry Operations layer is responsible for:

- registry health,
- objective integrity,
- lifecycle monitoring,
- certification tracking,
- evidence validation,
- operational reporting,
- governance support.


The Operations layer does not:

- create unauthorized objectives,
- bypass certification,
- activate execution,
- modify lifecycle authority,
- replace PBOS planners.

# Incident Management

The Objective Registry must fail safely when operational issues occur.


Examples:


## Invalid Objective Data

Response:

- quarantine invalid record,
- preserve history,
- prevent downstream planning.


## Missing Evidence

Response:

- mark objective incomplete,
- block certification,
- require remediation.


## Context Drift

Response:

- invalidate stale evaluation,
- refresh context,
- require revalidation.


## Authority Conflict

Response:

- stop transition,
- preserve evidence,
- escalate governance review.
