# Purpose

The PBOS Objective Registry Certification Framework defines the requirements, evidence standards, authority model, and lifecycle controls required to certify strategic objectives before they influence governed platform evolution.

Certification ensures that objectives entering PBOS governance are:

- strategically aligned,
- properly authorized,
- evidence-backed,
- architecturally compatible,
- traceable throughout their lifecycle.


---

# Certification Philosophy

Objectives represent strategic commitments.

Certification establishes trust between strategic intent and governed execution.


The certification flow is:

Strategic Intent

↓

Registered Objective

↓

Evidence Evaluation

↓

Certification Review

↓

Governed Planning

↓

Authorized Execution


Certification does not guarantee implementation success.

Certification confirms governance readiness.


---

# Certification Scope

Certification evaluates:

- objective completeness,
- objective ownership,
- authority alignment,
- architectural impact,
- dependency integrity,
- evidence availability,
- lifecycle compliance,
- context lineage.


---

# Certification Principles

## Evidence Before Advancement

No objective may advance without sufficient evidence supporting its readiness.


## Authority Before Action

Certification confirms that appropriate authority exists before strategic objectives influence execution.


## Lineage Preservation

Every certification decision must preserve:

- objective identity,
- evidence identity,
- repository context,
- approval history,
- certification history.


## Fail Closed Governance

Certification must reject objectives when trust cannot be established.


---

# Certification Levels

## Level 0 — Uncertified

Objective exists but has not entered certification review.

Characteristics:

- no certification evidence,
- no governance approval,
- no planning eligibility.


---

## Level 1 — Registered

Requirements:

- objective identity assigned,
- objective owner assigned,
- objective purpose documented,
- lifecycle state valid.


---

## Level 2 — Governed

Requirements:

- authority ownership validated,
- dependencies identified,
- evidence requirements defined,
- architectural alignment reviewed.


---

## Level 3 — Certified

Requirements:

- evidence validated,
- lineage verified,
- dependencies confirmed,
- governance requirements satisfied,
- certification record generated.


---

## Level 4 — Enterprise Certified

Requirements:

- institutional impact evaluated,
- operational implications reviewed,
- security considerations evaluated,
- partner ecosystem requirements considered,
- enterprise readiness confirmed.


---

# Certification Authority

Certification authority belongs to the PBOS Certification Framework.

Responsibilities:

- evaluate certification criteria,
- validate evidence,
- record certification history,
- maintain certification status,
- prevent invalid certification transitions.


Certification authority does not:

- create objectives,
- select execution gates,
- execute platform changes,
- override lifecycle governance.


---

# Certification Evidence Requirements

Every certification record must include:

## Objective Identity

Required:

- objective identifier,
- version,
- ownership,
- creation history.


## Architectural Evidence

Required:

- affected systems,
- constitutional alignment,
- dependency relationships,
- platform impact.


## Validation Evidence

Required:

- validation requirements,
- verification results,
- review outcomes,
- certification decision.


## Lineage Evidence

Required:

- repository context identity,
- artifact identity,
- evidence digest,
- historical references.

PROPOSED

↓

SUBMITTED

↓

UNDER_REVIEW

↓

EVIDENCE_VALIDATED

↓

CERTIFIED

↓

EXPIRED

↓

REVOKED


Skipped lifecycle transitions are prohibited.


---

# Certification Failure Conditions

Certification must fail closed when:

- ownership is missing,
- evidence is incomplete,
- dependencies are unresolved,
- architectural alignment is unclear,
- lineage cannot be verified,
- context is stale,
- authority is ambiguous.


---

# Enterprise Certification Model

Enterprise partners require confidence that:

- strategic decisions are governed,
- platform evolution is traceable,
- changes are controlled,
- outcomes are measurable.


The Objective Registry Certification Framework provides the trust foundation required for ecosystem-scale platform governance.

# Success Criteria

The certification framework succeeds when PBOS can answer:


1. Why does this objective exist?

2. Who authorized it?

3. What evidence supports it?

4. What architecture does it affect?

5. Can the decision be audited later?
