# Purpose

The PBOS Enterprise Objective Governance Model defines how organizations, institutions, strategic partners, and ecosystem participants interact with the PBOS Objective Registry while preserving platform integrity, trust, accountability, and constitutional governance.

This framework enables Playbook to support enterprise-scale collaboration without allowing external influence to bypass PBOS authority boundaries.


---

# Enterprise Governance Principle

Enterprise participation expands platform capability.

Enterprise participation does not transfer platform authority.


Organizations may:

- propose objectives,
- define strategic needs,
- provide implementation requirements,
- contribute evidence,
- participate in validation.


Organizations may not:

- bypass PBOS governance,
- directly activate execution,
- override constitutional architecture,
- modify platform truth.

# Failure And Escalation Model


## Unauthorized Objective Advancement

Response:

- reject transition,
- preserve history,
- require governance review.


## Conflicting Enterprise Requirements

Response:

- evaluate strategic alignment,
- preserve competing evidence,
- require platform decision.


## Architectural Conflict

Response:

- block progression,
- require architecture review.


## Governance Violation

Response:

- suspend objective,
- preserve audit trail,
- initiate review.
