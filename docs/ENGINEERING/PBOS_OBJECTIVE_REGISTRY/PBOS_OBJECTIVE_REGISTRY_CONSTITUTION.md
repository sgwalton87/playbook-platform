---

# Purpose

The PBOS Objective Registry establishes the constitutional framework governing how strategic objectives enter, are evaluated, prioritized, and prepared for execution within the Playbook Operating System.

The Objective Registry exists to ensure that PBOS does not merely execute work, but governs the origin, legitimacy, alignment, and readiness of work before execution begins.

---

# Constitutional Principle

PBOS must never execute an objective that has not established:

- strategic purpose,
- authorized ownership,
- architectural alignment,
- dependency awareness,
- evidence requirements,
- governance readiness.

The Objective Registry is the authoritative source of approved strategic objectives.

---

# Scope

The Objective Registry governs:

- platform objectives,
- constitutional initiatives,
- architectural evolution,
- enterprise capabilities,
- ecosystem expansion opportunities,
- future platform investments.

The registry does not execute work.

The registry does not select implementation gates.

The registry does not replace the constitutional planner.

---

# Authority Boundary

The Objective Registry owns:

- objective identity,
- objective ownership,
- objective lifecycle state,
- objective evidence requirements,
- objective dependencies,
- objective strategic alignment.

The Objective Registry does not own:

- gate activation,
- code execution,
- deployment,
- lifecycle mutation,
- runtime artifact ownership.

---

# Governance Requirements

Every registered objective must define:

## Strategic Intent

Why does this objective exist?

## Business Capability

What capability does this enable?

## Technical Capability

What architectural capability does this introduce?

## Ownership

Who is accountable?

## Evidence

How will completion be proven?

## Dependencies

What must exist before execution?

---

# Forbidden Behaviors

The Objective Registry must never:

- invent objectives,
- bypass review,
- activate gates,
- create execution authority,
- modify runtime truth,
- fabricate completion,
- override constitutional governance.

---

# Enterprise Principle

The Objective Registry enables Playbook to operate as an enterprise platform ecosystem.

Future capabilities must enter through governed intent rather than uncontrolled feature accumulation.

---

# Definition Of Success

The Objective Registry is successful when PBOS can answer:

1. Why are we building this?
2. Who authorized this objective?
3. What capability does it create?
4. What architecture does it impact?
5. How will success be measured?
6. Is the organization ready to execute?

---
