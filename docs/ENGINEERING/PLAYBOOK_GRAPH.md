# Playbook Graph™

## Purpose

The Playbook Graph™ is the data architecture behind Playbook OS™.

It defines how users, records, achievements, evidence, verification, intelligence engines, opportunities, and AI guidance connect.

The goal is simple:

Every meaningful action in Playbook should strengthen the Playbook Record™.

---

## Core Principle

Nothing in Playbook should exist in only one place.

A certificate is not only a certificate.

It should:

- Enrich the Playbook Record™
- Create a timeline event
- Strengthen the Trust Score
- Improve Opportunity Engine recommendations
- Appear in the Portfolio
- Support the Resume Engine
- Become evidence in the Scholar Vault™
- Teach Compass AI™ something about the learner

This is compounding architecture.

---

## Layer Model

### Layer 1 — Playbook Record™

The canonical lifelong record.

### Layer 2 — Trust Layer™

Evidence, verification, confidence, and credibility.

### Layer 3 — Living Evidence™

Documents, photos, videos, links, reflections, outcomes, and media.

### Layer 4 — Intelligence Engines™

Academic, athletic, career, leadership, financial, service, transcript, FAFSA, NIL, and resume intelligence.

### Layer 5 — Opportunity Engine™

Scholarships, colleges, internships, jobs, mentors, research, competitions, grants, and NIL opportunities.

### Layer 6 — Compass AI™

Personalized guidance based on the Playbook Record™.

---

## Primary Entities

### profiles

The user-facing identity record.

### playbook_records

The canonical lifelong record for a person or organization.

### achievements

Meaningful milestones connected to the Playbook Record™.

### evidence

Artifacts that support achievements.

### evidence_packs

Bundles of achievement, evidence, verification, reflection, outcomes, and media.

### verifications

Trusted confirmations by people or organizations.

### reflections

Learner-authored meaning and growth statements.

### outcomes

Measurable results created by achievements.

### timeline_events

Chronological events generated from the Playbook Record™.

### opportunity_matches

Recommendations generated by the Opportunity Engine™.

### trust_reports

Trust summaries generated by the Trust Engine™.

### scholar_vault_items

Secure stored documents and artifacts referenced by the Playbook Record™.

## Ownership Rules

The learner owns their Playbook Record™.

Organizations may contribute evidence or verification.

No organization owns the learner's lifelong record.

Schools, nonprofits, coaches, employers, mentors, and institutions may verify parts of the record but should not control the whole record.

---

## Verification Flow

1. Scholar creates or imports an achievement.
2. Scholar attaches evidence.
3. Scholar requests verification.
4. Verifier reviews the evidence.
5. Verifier approves, rejects, or requests changes.
6. Verification updates the Trust Layer™.
7. Trust updates the Opportunity Engine™.
8. Compass AI™ explains what changed.

## Database Design Standard

Every table should answer at least one question:

1. Does it improve the Playbook Record™?
2. Does it consume the Playbook Record™?
3. Does it create evidence, trust, intelligence, opportunity, or guidance?

If the answer is no, it should not be part of the core graph.
