# Scholar Record Data Model

## Core Rule

Every new feature must improve the Scholar Record or consume the Scholar Record.

---

## Scholar Record

The Scholar Record is the source of truth for a Scholar's verified growth.

It contains identity, academics, athletics, career, leadership, service, achievements, readiness scores, timeline events, opportunities, and evidence-backed milestones.

---

## Achievement

An Achievement is any meaningful milestone in a Scholar's life.

Examples:

- Completed course
- Earned certificate
- Volunteer service
- Leadership role
- Athletic award
- Internship
- College acceptance
- Scholarship
- NIL milestone
- Public speaking engagement
- Community project

---

## Evidence

Evidence supports an Achievement.

Evidence can include:

- Documents
- Photos
- Videos
- Links
- Certificates
- Transcripts
- Recommendations
- Media coverage

---

## Verification

Verification records whether an Achievement has been reviewed and confirmed.

Statuses:

- unverified
- pending
- verified
- rejected

---

## Reflection

Reflection captures the Scholar's own voice.

This helps transform the Scholar Record from a static database into a living record of growth.

---

## Outcome

Outcomes describe what resulted from an Achievement.

Examples:

- Skill gained
- Award earned
- Scholarship received
- Admission secured
- Internship completed
- Service hours logged
- Leadership demonstrated

---

## Evidence Pack

An Evidence Pack bundles an Achievement with supporting evidence, reflections, verification, and outcomes.

This is the foundation for:

- Portfolio
- Resume
- Timeline
- Opportunity Engine
- Recommendation Workflow
- Athlete Intelligence
- NIL Preparation
- Compass AI
