# PBOS Experience Plane Architecture

**Purpose:** Translate governed intelligence into accessible human growth experiences.  
**Owner:** Playbook OS Product and Experience Architecture  
**Last Updated:** July 30, 2026

## Principle

The Experience Plane is not a dashboard layer. It composes role-specific operating systems around journeys, decisions, relationships, evidence, and progress. Intelligence assists people and always exposes source evidence, reasoning, confidence, and confirmation requirements.

## Role Systems

Scholar OS owns the learner's growth journey. Parent OS supports consent-aware encouragement. Mentor OS supports bounded guidance. Coach OS supports development and opportunity evidence. Counselor OS supports academic and career planning. Institution OS supports governed population services without overriding individual agency.

## Experience Contract

Every experience defines information architecture, navigation, screen inventory, workflow, component ownership, permissions, data relationships, loading, empty, error, success, offline and restricted states, accessibility, responsive behavior, analytics, audit events, and recovery.

## Authority And Safety

Role access never implies ownership of another person's record. Recommendations cannot become decisions silently. Sensitive paths require consent, purpose limitation, explanation, appeal, and human confirmation.
