# PBOS Trust Plane Architecture

**Purpose:** Define permanent evidence, identity, time, lineage, and historical-state truth.  
**Owner:** Playbook OS Engineering  
**Last Updated:** July 30, 2026

## Authority

Source owners own facts; identity authorities own actors and organizations; Validation verifies evidence; Certification issues scoped trust. The Trust Plane preserves and correlates truth but cannot manufacture authority or certify itself.

## Model

Every governed object binds identity, authority, organization scope, source, effective/observed/recorded time, content digest, validation, lineage, and supersession. Claim, Decision, Action, and Outcome form an immutable trace.

## Failure Behavior

Unknown origin, missing provenance, invalid temporal order, duplicate identity, digest mismatch, incomplete lineage, or fabricated validation fails closed. Corrections append new versions; historical records are never rewritten.

## Security

Access follows least privilege, tenant isolation, retention, legal hold, and audit policy. Trust evidence is immutable and may be superseded, disputed, or revoked without deletion.
