# PBOS Founder Operating Model

Owner: Playbook OS Engineering  
Last updated: July 30, 2026  
Related: [It Command Architecture](./PBOS_IT_COMMAND_ARCHITECTURE.md)

## Human Authority

The founder supplies mission, values, quality expectations, risk acceptance, and decisions reserved for human authority. PBOS supplies repository analysis, deterministic sequencing, package generation, validation requirements, evidence correlation, and guidance.

## Separation Of Duties

The founder command cannot amend the Constitution, certify itself, authorize its own package, activate production, or override a failed validator. A `GREEN` risk classification means policy-level eligibility only; it never substitutes for context, package, agent, authorization, validation, or evidence.

## Operating Practice

The founder invokes `npm run it`. PBOS either identifies the next governed action or reports why action is prohibited. Expert commands remain available for inspection, approval, execution, advancement, and history.

Every completed action must retain who acted, why it was eligible, which authority applied, what changed, which validations passed, and what evidence supports advancement.
