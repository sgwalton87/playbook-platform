# Playbook Record™

The Playbook Record™ is the canonical engine for lifelong achievement.

The Scholar Record™ is the learner-facing view of the Playbook Record.

## Rule

Internally, engines should be designed around the Playbook Record.

Role-specific experiences may present that record as:

- Scholar Record™
- Scholar-Athlete Record™
- Educator Record™
- Coach Record™
- Mentor Record™
- Organization Record™
- Alumni Record™
