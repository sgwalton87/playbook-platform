# Purpose

The Accessibility Standards establish the constitutional requirements ensuring every Playbook experience is usable by every person, regardless of ability, disability, language, technology, environment, or method of interaction.

Accessibility is not a feature.

Accessibility is a constitutional right within the Playbook ecosystem.

Every implementation shall begin with accessibility rather than adding accessibility after development.

# Constitutional Philosophy

Opportunity belongs to everyone.

Technology should remove barriers.

Technology should never create barriers.

Accessibility improves experiences for every user—not only users with disabilities.

Accessibility shall be considered during planning, design, engineering, testing, deployment, and future maintenance.

# Keyboard Accessibility

Every interactive experience shall support complete keyboard navigation.

Users shall never require a pointing device to complete a supported workflow.

Keyboard focus shall remain visible at all times.

Focus order shall remain logical.

Keyboard traps are prohibited.

---

# Screen Readers

Every interface shall provide semantic structure supporting modern screen readers.

Interactive controls shall expose accessible names.

Images conveying meaning shall include appropriate alternative text.

Decorative images shall be hidden from assistive technologies.

---

# Semantic Structure

Interfaces shall use meaningful document structure.

Examples include:

Headings

Lists

Regions

Navigation

Buttons

Forms

Tables

Dialogs

Semantic meaning shall never depend upon visual appearance.

---

# Color Accessibility

Color shall never be the only method of communicating:

Errors

Warnings

Selections

Status

Required fields

Progress

Validation

Meaning shall be reinforced through text, icons, patterns, or additional visual cues.

---

# Typography Accessibility

Typography shall support:

Browser zoom

Scalable interfaces

Readable line length

Logical hierarchy

Clear labels

Readable spacing

Typography shall remain legible under accessibility settings.

---

# Motion Accessibility

Interfaces shall respect reduced-motion preferences.

Animations shall never be required to understand content.

Critical information shall remain visible without animation.

---

# Touch Accessibility

Interactive controls shall provide touch targets large enough for reliable interaction.

Controls shall avoid accidental activation.

Spacing shall reduce unintended input.

---

# Forms

Forms shall:

Clearly identify required fields

Explain validation failures

Preserve entered information

Associate labels with controls

Provide recovery guidance

Validation shall occur respectfully.

Users should never feel punished for mistakes.

---

# Error Recovery

Every error shall communicate:

What happened

Why it happened when known

How to recover

Whether information has been preserved

Errors shall reduce frustration rather than increase it.

---

# Media Accessibility

Video should support:

Captions

Accessible controls

Keyboard interaction

Audio should support:

Playback controls

Transcripts where appropriate

Images shall include meaningful alternative descriptions when required.

---

# Tables

Tables shall:

Remain understandable by assistive technologies

Support logical navigation

Identify headers

Avoid unnecessary complexity

---

# Charts & Data Visualization

Charts shall:

Provide textual summaries

Avoid color-only communication

Support accessible interaction

Communicate trends clearly

---

# Artificial Intelligence

AI experiences shall remain accessible.

AI shall:

Explain recommendations

Identify uncertainty

Support keyboard interaction

Support screen readers

Avoid inaccessible visualizations

Preserve user agency

AI shall not reduce accessibility.

---

# Language

Interfaces shall:

Use plain language

Avoid unnecessary jargon

Explain specialized terminology

Support localization

Support future multilingual expansion

Users should understand information without specialized expertise whenever practical.

---

# Cognitive Accessibility

Experiences shall minimize unnecessary cognitive load.

Interfaces should:

Reduce distractions

Use familiar terminology

Present information progressively

Provide consistent navigation

Support memory through recognition rather than recall

---

# Authentication

Authentication experiences shall remain accessible.

Users shall be able to:

Create accounts

Sign in

Recover accounts

Verify identity

using supported assistive technologies.

---

# Notifications

Notifications shall remain accessible regardless of presentation method.

Critical information shall not disappear before users can access it.

---

# Offline Accessibility

Offline experiences shall preserve accessibility whenever supported functionality remains available.

---

# Future Technologies

These standards apply equally to:

Wearables

Augmented Reality

Virtual Reality

Spatial Computing

Brain-Computer Interfaces

---

# Testing

Accessibility testing shall include:

Automated testing

Manual testing

Keyboard testing

Screen reader testing

Color contrast validation

Responsive validation

User testing whenever practical

Accessibility shall not rely exclusively upon automated tools.

---

# PBOS Responsibilities

PBOS shall validate:

Semantic structure

Keyboard accessibility

Focus management

Alternative text

Accessible labels

Contrast compliance

Reduced motion

Responsive accessibility

Component accessibility

Navigation accessibility

Screen specification compliance

Accessibility certification status

Implementations failing constitutional accessibility requirements shall fail certification.

---

# Engineering Responsibilities

Engineering shall:

Implement governed accessible components

Preserve semantic HTML where applicable

Avoid inaccessible custom controls

Correct accessibility defects promptly

Accessibility regressions shall be treated as constitutional defects.

---

# Governance

Accessibility requirements may become more rigorous over time.

Accessibility requirements shall never become less protective without constitutional approval.

Accessibility defects affecting opportunity, education, employment, or safety receive the highest remediation priority.

---

# Success Criteria

Every person should be able to participate fully within the Playbook ecosystem regardless of ability, disability, language, technology, or environment.

Accessibility shall become an invisible characteristic of every experience rather than a visible accommodation.

The Accessibility Standards serve as the constitutional guarantee of inclusive access throughout the Playbook ecosystem.
