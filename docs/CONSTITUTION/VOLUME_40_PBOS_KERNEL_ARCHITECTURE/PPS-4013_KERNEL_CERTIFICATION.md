# Purpose

Establish the constitutional certification authority governing the PBOS Kernel.

Kernel Certification is the final constitutional safeguard protecting the integrity of the Playbook Operating System.

No Kernel execution, release, architectural evolution, or constitutional implementation shall be considered authoritative until it has successfully completed Kernel Certification.

Certification validates constitutional compliance.

Certification never creates constitutional authority.

Authority originates from the Constitution itself.

---

# Constitutional Philosophy

Certification is an independent constitutional function.

It shall never rely solely upon planner output, runtime output, implementation assumptions, developer assertions, or artificial intelligence recommendations.

Every certification decision shall be supported by independently verifiable constitutional evidence.

Certification protects long-term trust in PBOS.

---

# Certification Objectives

Kernel Certification shall verify:

- Constitutional integrity
- Repository integrity
- Runtime integrity
- Kernel integrity
- Service integrity
- State integrity
- Dependency integrity
- Scheduling integrity
- Execution integrity
- Validation integrity
- Security integrity
- Observability integrity
- Recovery integrity
- Performance integrity
- Historical integrity

Certification shall fail closed whenever sufficient evidence cannot be established.

# Certification Outcomes

Certification shall produce one of the following outcomes.

CERTIFIED

The Kernel satisfies every applicable constitutional requirement.

---

CERTIFIED WITH OBSERVATIONS

The Kernel satisfies constitutional requirements but includes non-blocking recommendations for future improvement.

---

CERTIFICATION WITHHELD

Required constitutional evidence is incomplete, ambiguous, or unavailable.

Execution shall not be considered constitutionally complete.

---

CERTIFICATION DENIED

One or more constitutional requirements have been violated.

Execution shall terminate.

Corrective action is required before recertification.

---

Every certification decision shall include:

- Certification Identifier
- Timestamp
- Repository Identifier
- Execution Identifier
- Constitutional Authority
- Applicable Constitutional Documents
- Validation Summary
- Evidence Summary
- Findings
- Risks
- Recommendations
- Certification Outcome
- Certifying Authority

Certification records become immutable constitutional artifacts.

---

# Constitutional Guarantees

Kernel Certification guarantees that every certified execution is:

- constitutionally governed;
- independently validated;
- fully traceable;
- reproducible;
- observable;
- historically preserved.

Certification guarantees process integrity rather than business correctness.

---

# Separation of Responsibilities

Planning recommends execution.

Scheduling coordinates execution.

Validation verifies execution prerequisites.

State Writers perform state transitions.

Kernel Services coordinate execution.

Observability records execution.

History preserves execution.

Certification independently verifies the constitutional integrity of the entire process.

No subsystem may certify itself.

---

# Constitutional Invariants

Kernel Certification shall preserve the following permanent invariants.

1. Constitutional authority shall never be bypassed.

2. Every certification decision shall be evidence-based.

3. Historical certification records are immutable.

4. Certification is reproducible.

5. Certification is deterministic.

6. Certification is independently verifiable.

7. Certification shall fail closed.

8. Every constitutional requirement shall be traceable to authoritative documentation.

9. No implementation technology may supersede constitutional authority.

10. Future Kernel evolution shall remain subject to constitutional certification.

These invariants are permanent unless modified through the Constitutional Amendment Process.
