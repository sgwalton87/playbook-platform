# Purpose

Establish the constitutional architecture governing how content is organized, composed, grouped, and presented throughout the Playbook Platform.

Content Composition defines how reusable interface elements combine to create coherent user experiences while preserving readability, accessibility, scalability, and consistency.

---

# Mission

Provide a unified architectural framework for composing interface content from reusable structural building blocks rather than one-off page implementations.

---

# Scope

This document governs:

- Content containers
- Sections
- Stacks
- Groups
- Cards
- Lists
- Reading layouts
- Information hierarchy
- Content density
- Visual grouping

---

# Constitutional Principles

## Composition Before Customization

Interfaces shall be assembled from reusable composition patterns before introducing custom layouts.

---

## Hierarchy

Content shall clearly communicate importance through constitutional composition rather than arbitrary placement.

---

## Reusability

Composition patterns shall be reusable across applications, operating systems, and workflows.

---

## Accessibility

Content composition shall preserve logical reading order and support assistive technologies.

---

## Consistency

Equivalent information shall use equivalent composition patterns throughout the platform.

---

# Constitutional Composition Elements

Playbook recognizes:

- Containers
- Sections
- Cards
- Lists
- Stacks
- Groups
- Dividers
- Reading Regions

---

# PBOS Responsibilities

PBOS shall validate:

- Composition hierarchy
- Accessibility
- Component inheritance
- Structural consistency
- Reuse opportunities

---

# Governance

Applications shall inherit constitutional Content Composition Architecture.

Independent composition systems are constitutionally prohibited.

# Future Evolution

Future constitutional amendments may introduce adaptive composition, AI-assisted information architecture, intelligent layouts, and emerging presentation paradigms while preserving constitutional consistency.
