# Purpose

Establish the constitutional governance governing the lifecycle, evolution, versioning, validation, and long-term stewardship of Layout Architecture throughout the Playbook Platform.

---

# Mission

Ensure every layout evolves in a controlled, predictable, and constitutionally governed manner while preserving compatibility, usability, accessibility, and architectural integrity.

---

# Scope

This document governs:

- Layout lifecycle
- Layout versioning
- Layout migration
- Layout deprecation
- Backward compatibility
- Constitutional validation
- PBOS enforcement
- Future layout evolution

---

# Constitutional Principles

## Stability

Layout Architecture shall evolve incrementally without introducing unnecessary disruption.

---

## Constitutional Compliance

Every layout shall inherit from constitutional Layout Architecture.

---

## Backward Compatibility

Existing layouts should remain compatible whenever reasonably achievable.

Breaking changes require constitutional review.

---

## Traceability

Every architectural change shall preserve traceability to the constitutional source governing the layout.

---

## Continuous Improvement

Layouts shall evolve based upon validated platform learning while preserving constitutional consistency.

---

# PBOS Responsibilities

PBOS shall validate:

- Constitutional inheritance
- Layout version history
- Migration compatibility
- Accessibility preservation
- Dependency integrity
- Cross-platform consistency

---

# Governance

Layout Architecture constitutes a constitutional subsystem.

Architectural modifications shall be reviewed, validated, documented, and versioned before adoption.

# Relationship to Other Documents

# Future Evolution

Future constitutional amendments may introduce autonomous layout optimization, AI-assisted governance, adaptive interface evolution, and emerging workspace paradigms while preserving constitutional integrity.
