# Purpose

Establish the constitutional architecture governing dashboard layouts across the Playbook Platform.

Dashboard Layouts organize actionable information, intelligence, progress, and navigation into consistent, user-centered workspaces.

---

# Mission

Provide every Playbook role with a dashboard architecture that surfaces the most relevant information, actions, and opportunities while minimizing cognitive load.

---

# Scope

This document governs:

- Dashboard structure
- Widget placement
- Summary panels
- Progress visualization
- Opportunity sections
- Activity feeds
- Quick actions
- Personalized workspaces

---

# Constitutional Principles

## Action-Oriented Design

Dashboards shall prioritize actionable information over passive reporting.

---

## Information Hierarchy

Critical information shall appear before secondary information.

---

## Personalization

Dashboards may adapt to user roles while preserving constitutional structure.

---

## Progressive Disclosure

Complex information shall be revealed progressively rather than overwhelming users.

---

## Consistency

Equivalent dashboard capabilities shall appear in equivalent locations whenever practical.

---

# Constitutional Dashboard Regions

Playbook Dashboards recognize:

- Welcome Area
- Primary Metrics
- Action Center
- Opportunities
- Progress Tracking
- Notifications
- Recent Activity
- Resource Panel

---

# PBOS Responsibilities

PBOS shall validate:

- Dashboard hierarchy
- Widget placement
- Accessibility
- Responsive behavior
- Structural consistency

---

# Governance

Dashboard implementations shall inherit constitutional dashboard architecture rather than creating independent organizational systems.

# Future Evolution

Future constitutional amendments may introduce adaptive dashboards, AI-generated workspace optimization, predictive prioritization, and emerging visualization capabilities while preserving constitutional consistency.
