# Purpose

Establish the constitutional architecture governing Panels and Interface Regions throughout the Playbook Platform.

Panels and Regions divide interfaces into reusable functional areas that organize information, actions, utilities, and contextual content.

---

# Mission

Provide a consistent architectural framework for reusable interface regions that improves usability, composability, scalability, and maintainability.

---

# Scope

This document governs:

- Side panels
- Context panels
- Utility panels
- Inspector panels
- Bottom panels
- Floating panels
- Docked regions
- Resizable regions
- Region visibility

---

# Constitutional Principles

## Functional Separation

Each region shall have a clearly defined responsibility.

---

## Composability

Panels shall compose into larger workspace architectures without duplication.

---

## Consistency

Equivalent interface regions shall behave consistently across applications.

---

## Accessibility

Panels shall support keyboard navigation, logical focus order, and assistive technologies.

---

## Flexibility

Regions may expand, collapse, resize, or reposition while preserving constitutional behavior.

---

# Constitutional Region Types

Playbook recognizes:

- Navigation Panel
- Content Region
- Utility Region
- Inspector Panel
- Context Panel
- Collaboration Panel
- Footer Region

---

# PBOS Responsibilities

PBOS shall validate:

- Region hierarchy
- Accessibility
- Component inheritance
- Responsive behavior
- Structural consistency

---

# Governance

Applications shall inherit constitutional Panel and Region Architecture.

Independent region systems are constitutionally prohibited.

---

# Relationship to Other Documents

This document supports:

- Workspace Layouts
- Components
- Navigation
- Responsive Layouts
- Experience Architecture

---

# Future Evolution

Future constitutional amendments may introduce intelligent regions, collaborative panels, adaptive layouts, and spatial interface regions while preserving constitutional consistency.
