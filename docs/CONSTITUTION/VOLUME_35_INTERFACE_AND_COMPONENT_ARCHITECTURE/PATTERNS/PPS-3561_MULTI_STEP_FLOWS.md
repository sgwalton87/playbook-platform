# Purpose

Establish the constitutional architecture governing multi-step workflows throughout the Playbook Platform.

---

# Mission

Provide a consistent framework for guiding users through complex workflows while minimizing cognitive load, reducing abandonment, and maintaining transparency.

---

# Scope

This document governs:

- Onboarding
- Applications
- Registrations
- Checklists
- Wizards
- Reviews
- Multi-page forms
- Guided setup
- Checkout workflows
- Progress persistence

---

# Constitutional Principles

## Sequential Progress

Users shall understand where they are, where they have been, and what remains.

---

## Progressive Disclosure

Information shall be introduced only when required.

---

## Recoverability

Users should be able to pause and resume workflows whenever practical.

---

## Accessibility

Every step shall satisfy constitutional accessibility requirements.

---

## Predictability

Equivalent workflows shall follow equivalent interaction patterns.

---

# Constitutional Flow Elements

Playbook recognizes:

- Step Indicator
- Progress Summary
- Primary Action
- Secondary Action
- Save Draft
- Review Screen
- Completion Screen
- Exit Flow

---

# PBOS Responsibilities

PBOS shall validate:

- Flow consistency
- Accessibility
- Navigation integrity
- Progress preservation
- Component inheritance

---

# Governance

Applications shall inherit constitutional Multi-Step Flow Architecture.

Independent workflow paradigms are constitutionally prohibited.

# Future Evolution

Future constitutional amendments may introduce adaptive workflows, AI-assisted guidance, personalized flow optimization, and collaborative completion while preserving constitutional consistency.
