# Purpose

Establish the constitutional architecture governing Search interaction patterns throughout the Playbook Platform.

---

# Mission

Provide consistent search behavior that enables users to discover information quickly, accurately, and predictably across every Playbook experience.

---

# Scope

This document governs:

- Global search
- Contextual search
- Instant search
- Autocomplete
- Suggestions
- Saved searches
- Search refinement
- Search history
- Zero-result handling
- Search recovery

---

# Constitutional Principles

## Discoverability

Search shall minimize effort required to locate information.

---

## Consistency

Equivalent search experiences shall behave consistently throughout the platform.

---

## Transparency

Search results shall clearly communicate relevance and available refinements.

---

## Accessibility

Search shall remain fully usable through keyboard navigation and assistive technologies.

---

## Performance

Search interactions shall communicate system responsiveness and progress.

---

# Constitutional Search Behaviors

Playbook recognizes:

- Instant Search
- Manual Search
- Suggestions
- Filters
- Search History
- Saved Searches
- Empty Results
- Recent Results

---

# PBOS Responsibilities

PBOS shall validate:

- Search consistency
- Accessibility
- Behavioral integrity
- Component inheritance
- Cross-platform compatibility

---

# Governance

Applications shall inherit constitutional Search Pattern Architecture.

Independent search behaviors are constitutionally prohibited.

# Future Evolution

Future constitutional amendments may introduce semantic search, conversational search, AI-assisted discovery, multimodal search, and intelligent ranking while preserving constitutional consistency.
