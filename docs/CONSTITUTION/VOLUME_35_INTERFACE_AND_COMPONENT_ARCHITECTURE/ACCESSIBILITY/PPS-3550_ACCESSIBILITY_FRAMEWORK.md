# Purpose

Establish the constitutional framework governing accessibility throughout the Playbook Platform.

Accessibility is a constitutional requirement ensuring every user, regardless of ability, can effectively access, understand, and interact with the platform.

---

# Mission

Provide a unified accessibility architecture that embeds inclusive design, equitable interaction, and assistive technology support into every constitutional interface component.

---

# Scope

This document governs:

- Accessible interaction
- Inclusive design
- Keyboard accessibility
- Screen reader compatibility
- Color accessibility
- Motion accessibility
- Responsive accessibility
- Semantic markup
- Focus management
- Accessibility governance

---

# Constitutional Principles

## Accessibility by Default

Accessibility shall be designed into every interface rather than added afterward.

---

## Equal Opportunity

Every user shall have equitable access to platform capabilities regardless of ability.

---

## Inclusive Design

Accessibility improvements shall benefit all users whenever practical.

---

## Constitutional Compliance

Accessibility requirements apply to every application, operating system, component, and workflow.

---

## Continuous Improvement

Accessibility shall evolve through ongoing validation, testing, and constitutional governance.

---

# PBOS Responsibilities

PBOS shall validate:

- Accessibility compliance
- Semantic structure
- Component inheritance
- Constitutional consistency
- Cross-platform implementation

---

# Governance

Accessibility requirements are constitutionally mandatory.

Applications failing constitutional accessibility requirements shall not achieve PBOS certification.

---

# Relationship to Other Documents

This document governs:

- Responsive Accessibility

# Future Evolution

Future constitutional amendments may incorporate emerging accessibility standards, adaptive interfaces, multimodal interaction, AI-assisted accessibility, and future assistive technologies while preserving constitutional protections.
