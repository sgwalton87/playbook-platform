# Purpose

Establish the constitutional architecture governing Screen Reader Support throughout the Playbook Platform.

Screen Reader Support ensures digital experiences communicate meaning effectively through assistive technologies, enabling equitable access to every constitutional capability.

---

# Mission

Provide a unified semantic interaction architecture that enables reliable, understandable, and efficient screen reader experiences across every Playbook interface.

---

# Scope

This document governs:

- Semantic HTML
- ARIA roles
- Accessible names
- Accessible descriptions
- Live regions
- Landmark regions
- Form accessibility
- Dialog accessibility
- Table accessibility
- Notification announcements

---

# Constitutional Principles

## Semantic First

Native semantic elements shall be preferred over custom accessibility implementations whenever practical.

---

## Meaningful Communication

Every interactive component shall expose meaningful names, roles, states, and descriptions.

---

## Consistency

Equivalent interface components shall produce equivalent screen reader experiences.

---

## Dynamic Announcements

Changes affecting users shall be announced appropriately through assistive technologies.

---

## Equal Access

Screen reader users shall have equivalent access to platform capabilities and workflows.

---

# Constitutional Accessibility Elements

Playbook recognizes:

- Landmark Regions
- Accessible Names
- Accessible Descriptions
- ARIA Roles
- Live Regions
- Status Messages
- Alerts
- Semantic Structure

---

# PBOS Responsibilities

PBOS shall validate:

- Semantic correctness
- ARIA usage
- Screen reader compatibility
- Component inheritance
- Accessibility compliance

---

# Governance

Applications shall inherit constitutional Screen Reader Architecture.

Interfaces failing semantic accessibility requirements shall not achieve PBOS certification.

# Future Evolution

Future constitutional amendments may incorporate emerging assistive technologies, AI-enhanced accessibility services, multimodal interaction systems, and future accessibility standards while preserving constitutional protections.
