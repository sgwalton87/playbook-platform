# Purpose

Establish the constitutional architecture governing Card components throughout the Playbook Platform.

Cards are reusable containers that organize related information, actions, and metadata into meaningful, modular interface units.

---

# Mission

Provide a unified Card architecture that enables scalable information presentation while preserving consistency, accessibility, composability, and maintainability.

---

# Scope

This document governs:

- Information cards
- Dashboard cards
- Opportunity cards
- Scholar cards
- Organization cards
- Course cards
- Analytics cards
- Interactive cards
- Expandable cards
- Summary cards

---

# Constitutional Principles

## Modular Composition

Cards shall encapsulate related information into reusable interface units.

---

## Clear Hierarchy

Cards shall communicate primary information before secondary details.

---

## Consistency

Equivalent information shall be presented using equivalent card structures.

---

## Accessibility

Cards shall support keyboard navigation, semantic markup, focus management, and assistive technologies.

---

## Reusability

Cards shall remain reusable constitutional components independent of application-specific implementations.

---

# Constitutional Card Elements

Playbook recognizes:

- Card Header
- Card Title
- Card Subtitle
- Card Content
- Card Media
- Card Metadata
- Card Actions
- Card Footer

---

# PBOS Responsibilities

PBOS shall validate:

- Component inheritance
- Accessibility compliance
- Structural consistency
- Design Token usage
- Cross-platform compatibility

---

# Governance

Applications shall inherit constitutional Card Architecture.

Independent card systems are constitutionally prohibited.

# Future Evolution

Future constitutional amendments may introduce adaptive cards, AI-generated summaries, interactive intelligence cards, and emerging presentation paradigms while preserving constitutional consistency.
