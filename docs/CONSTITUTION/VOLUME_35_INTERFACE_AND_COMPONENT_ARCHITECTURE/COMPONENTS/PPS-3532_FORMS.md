# Purpose

Establish the constitutional architecture governing forms throughout the Playbook Platform.

Forms organize related inputs into structured workflows that collect, validate, and submit information while providing consistent, accessible, and user-centered experiences.

---

# Mission

Provide a unified form architecture that enables reliable data collection, minimizes user effort, and supports scalable workflows across every Playbook experience.

---

# Scope

This document governs:

- Single-page forms
- Multi-step forms
- Registration forms
- Profile forms
- Application forms
- Validation
- Error presentation
- Submission workflows
- Autosave behavior
- Draft recovery

---

# Constitutional Principles

## User Guidance

Forms shall clearly communicate required information, expected formats, and next actions.

---

## Progressive Disclosure

Complex forms shall be divided into manageable sections whenever practical.

---

## Accessibility

Forms shall fully support keyboard navigation, screen readers, semantic markup, focus management, and assistive technologies.

---

## Validation

Validation shall occur consistently and communicate errors constructively.

---

## Completion

Forms shall provide clear confirmation of successful submission and guidance following completion.

---

# Constitutional Form Elements

Playbook recognizes:

- Form Container
- Section
- Input
- Label
- Helper Text
- Validation
- Submit Action
- Success State
- Error State

---

# PBOS Responsibilities

PBOS shall validate:

- Accessibility compliance
- Validation consistency
- Component inheritance
- Workflow integrity
- Cross-platform behavior

---

# Governance

Applications shall inherit constitutional Form Architecture.

Independent form systems are constitutionally prohibited.
