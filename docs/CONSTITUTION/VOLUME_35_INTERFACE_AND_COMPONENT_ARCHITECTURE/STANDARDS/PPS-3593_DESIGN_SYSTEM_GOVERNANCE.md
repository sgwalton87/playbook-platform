# Purpose

Establish the constitutional governance governing the Playbook Design System.

---

# Mission

Provide a single constitutional source of truth governing reusable interface components, design tokens, accessibility, interaction behavior, documentation, validation, and lifecycle management.

---

# Scope

This document governs:

- Design Tokens
- Components
- Patterns
- Accessibility
- Documentation
- Governance
- Validation
- Lifecycle
- Ownership
- Evolution

---

# Constitutional Principles

## Single Source of Truth

The constitutional design system shall govern every interface implementation.

---

## Consistency

Equivalent experiences shall utilize equivalent interface architecture.

---

## Accessibility

Accessibility remains mandatory throughout the design system.

---

## Governance

All design system modifications require constitutional approval.

---

## Continuous Improvement

The design system shall evolve while preserving compatibility.

---

# PBOS Responsibilities

PBOS shall validate:

- Design system compliance
- Token usage
- Accessibility
- Component reuse
- Cross-platform consistency

---

# Governance

No application may bypass the constitutional design system.

---

# Relationship to Other Documents

This document governs the entirety of Volume 35.

# Future Evolution

Future constitutional amendments may introduce autonomous governance, intelligent audits, adaptive design systems, and AI-assisted architectural stewardship.
