# Purpose

Establish the constitutional architecture governing review and approval of interface designs throughout the Playbook Platform.

---

# Mission

Ensure every interface undergoes structured constitutional review before becoming part of the canonical Playbook Design System.

---

# Scope

This document governs:

- Design proposals
- Interface reviews
- Accessibility reviews
- UX reviews
- Design approvals
- Constitutional compliance
- PBOS validation
- Review documentation
- Design audits
- Approval workflows

---

# Constitutional Principles

## Constitutional Compliance

Every interface shall be reviewed against constitutional requirements.

---

## Evidence-Based Decisions

Design decisions shall be supported by documented rationale.

---

## Accessibility

Accessibility review is constitutionally mandatory.

---

## Transparency

Design reviews shall preserve documentation and decision history.

---

## Continuous Improvement

Review outcomes shall strengthen future interface architecture.

---

# PBOS Responsibilities

PBOS shall validate:

- Review completion
- Accessibility verification
- Constitutional compliance
- Documentation quality
- Approval traceability

---

# Governance

No interface shall become canonical without constitutional design review.

Supports Component Governance, Component Testing, Interface Evolution, and Constitutional Amendment Process.

# Future Evolution

Future constitutional amendments may introduce AI-assisted design reviews, automated compliance audits, intelligent review workflows, and continuous interface certification.
