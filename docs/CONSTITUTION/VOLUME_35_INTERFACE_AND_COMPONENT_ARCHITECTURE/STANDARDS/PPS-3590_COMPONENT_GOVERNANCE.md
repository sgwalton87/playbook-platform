# Purpose

Establish the constitutional governance governing the lifecycle, ownership, approval, maintenance, and stewardship of interface components throughout the Playbook Platform.

---

# Mission

Provide a unified governance framework ensuring every interface component remains reusable, maintainable, versioned, validated, and constitutionally compliant throughout its lifecycle.

---

# Scope

This document governs:

- Component ownership
- Component approval
- Component lifecycle
- Component maintenance
- Component review
- Component retirement
- Architectural stewardship
- Constitutional compliance
- PBOS validation
- Governance workflows

---

# Constitutional Principles

## Constitutional Authority

All interface components derive authority from the constitutional architecture.

---

## Single Source of Truth

Each reusable component shall have one canonical constitutional definition.

---

## Stewardship

Every component shall have clearly defined ownership and governance responsibilities.

---

## Validation

Components shall satisfy constitutional validation before platform adoption.

---

## Evolution

Component evolution shall preserve stability, compatibility, and constitutional integrity.

---

# PBOS Responsibilities

PBOS shall validate:

- Ownership
- Constitutional inheritance
- Lifecycle compliance
- Version integrity
- Dependency consistency
- Governance approval

---

# Governance

No interface component may become canonical without constitutional approval and PBOS validation.

# Relationship to Other Documents

This document governs all Interface & Component Architecture documents within Volume 35.

# Future Evolution

Future constitutional amendments may introduce automated governance, AI-assisted review, intelligent lifecycle management, and continuous architectural certification while preserving constitutional authority.
