# Purpose

Establish the constitutional architecture governing reusable component composition.

---

# Mission

Ensure complex interfaces are constructed from reusable constitutional building blocks rather than duplicated implementations.

---

# Scope

This document governs:

- Component hierarchy
- Composition
- Nesting
- Reuse
- Shared behaviors
- Shared styling
- Shared interaction
- Containers
- Layout composition
- Component relationships

---

# Constitutional Principles

## Composition Before Duplication

Existing constitutional components shall be reused before creating new ones.

---

## Encapsulation

Components shall expose stable public interfaces.

---

## Separation of Responsibility

Each component shall have one clearly defined purpose.

---

## Reusability

Components shall remain application independent whenever practical.

---

## Maintainability

Composition shall simplify long-term maintenance.

---

# PBOS Responsibilities

PBOS shall validate:

- Composition integrity
- Dependency hierarchy
- Reuse opportunities
- Component inheritance
- Architectural consistency

---

# Governance

Composition standards are constitutionally mandatory.

# Relationship to Other Documents

Supports Component Governance, Content Composition Architecture, Component Testing, and Design System Governance.

# Future Evolution

Future constitutional amendments may introduce AI-assisted composition analysis, automated reuse recommendations, and intelligent component assembly.
