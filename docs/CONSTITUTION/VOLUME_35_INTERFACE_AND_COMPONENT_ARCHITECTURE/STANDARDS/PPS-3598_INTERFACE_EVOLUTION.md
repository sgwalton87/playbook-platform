# Constitutional Principles

## Stability with Innovation

Innovation shall strengthen rather than destabilize constitutional architecture.

---

## Preservation

Historical interface knowledge shall remain traceable.

---

## Compatibility

Evolution shall preserve compatibility whenever practical.

---

## Governance

Major interface changes require constitutional approval.

---

## Human-Centered Design

Interface evolution shall continue serving human needs above technological novelty.
