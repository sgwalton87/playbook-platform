# Purpose

Establish the constitutional architecture governing the Execution Registry.

The Execution Registry serves as the authoritative catalog of executable capabilities throughout the Playbook Platform ecosystem.

Every governed execution shall be discoverable through the registry.

---

# Scope

The registry governs:

- Commands
- Workflows
- Events
- Automations
- Scheduled jobs
- Background processes
- Human approval workflows
- Distributed execution definitions
- Future execution capabilities

---

# Constitutional Principles

The Execution Registry shall be:

- Authoritative
- Complete
- Discoverable
- Versioned
- Governed
- Auditable
- Traceable

---

# Registry Model

Each registry entry shall define:

- Constitutional identifier
- Name
- Purpose
- Execution category
- Owner
- Lifecycle state
- Required authorization
- Required context
- Dependencies
- Required certification types
- Governance authority
- Version history

---

# Registry Lifecycle

Registry entries progress through:

Draft

↓

Review

↓

Approved

↓

Active

↓

Deprecated

↓

Archived

Lifecycle transitions require constitutional governance.

---

# Registry Integrity

The registry shall ensure:

- Unique identifiers
- Immutable historical versions
- Dependency integrity
- Constitutional traceability
- Cross-volume references

---

# Registry Responsibilities

The registry enables:

- Discovery
- Governance
- Dependency analysis
- Eligibility, execution, outcome, and evidence certification discovery
- Audit
- Impact assessment
- Platform evolution

---

# Governance

The Execution Registry constitutes constitutional infrastructure.

No executable capability shall exist outside the registry.

Registry evolution shall follow the Constitutional Amendment Process.
