# Purpose

Define the constitutional architecture governing execution service levels.

Service levels establish constitutional expectations for execution quality without altering constitutional authority.

---

# Scope

Applies to:

- Runtime execution
- Enterprise workflows
- Administrative workflows
- AI execution
- Background execution
- Future execution systems

---

# Constitutional Principles

Service levels shall be:

- Observable
- Measurable
- Governed
- Explainable
- Continuously evaluated

---

# Service Level Model

Execution service levels may define:

- Availability
- Responsiveness
- Completion targets
- Reliability
- Recovery objectives
- Certification targets
- Governance thresholds

---

# Constitutional Guarantees

Service levels shall:

- Inform governance
- Support continuous improvement
- Never supersede constitutional authority
- Preserve execution truth

---

# Compliance

Execution performance shall remain observable against established service objectives.

---

# Governance

Service levels support operational excellence while remaining subordinate to constitutional governance.
