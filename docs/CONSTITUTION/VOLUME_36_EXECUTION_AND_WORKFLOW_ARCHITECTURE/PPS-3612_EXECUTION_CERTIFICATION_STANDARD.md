# Purpose

Establish the constitutional architecture governing execution certification.

Certification confirms that an execution satisfied constitutional requirements before being recognized as valid.

Certification establishes trust.

Execution alone does not.

---

# Scope

Applies to:

- Commands
- Workflows
- Automations
- Events
- Administrative execution
- Enterprise execution
- AI-assisted execution

---

# Constitutional Principles

Certification shall be:

- Independent
- Deterministic
- Verifiable
- Observable
- Immutable
- Governed

---

# Certification Model

Certification verifies:

- Constitutional identity
- Authorization
- Context integrity
- Validation completion
- Policy compliance
- Execution evidence
- Lifecycle completion

Only certified execution may become constitutionally recognized.

---

# Certification Outcomes

Execution may be:

- Certified
- Conditionally certified
- Deferred
- Rejected
- Revoked

Certification decisions shall become immutable constitutional evidence.

---

# Revocation

Certification may be revoked only through governed constitutional procedures.

Revocation shall preserve historical truth.

Revocation shall not erase execution evidence.

---

# Certification Evidence

Every certification shall record:

- Certifying authority
- Timestamp
- Supporting evidence
- Constitutional references
- Decision outcome
- Governance rationale

---

# Governance

Certification provides the constitutional trust boundary separating execution from recognized constitutional truth.
