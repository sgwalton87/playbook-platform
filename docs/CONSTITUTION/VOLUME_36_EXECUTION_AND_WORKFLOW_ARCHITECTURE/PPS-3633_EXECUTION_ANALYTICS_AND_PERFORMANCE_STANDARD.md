# Purpose

Establish the constitutional architecture governing execution analytics and performance measurement.

Analytics provide governed insight into execution health without altering constitutional truth.

---

# Scope

Applies to:

- Commands
- Workflows
- Events
- Automation
- Enterprise execution
- AI execution
- Runtime execution

---

# Constitutional Principles

Execution analytics shall be:

- Accurate
- Explainable
- Observable
- Auditable
- Privacy preserving
- Constitutionally governed

---

# Analytics Model

Execution analytics may measure:

- Throughput
- Latency
- Reliability
- Recovery success
- Failure frequency
- Workflow duration
- Resource consumption
- Service level compliance

---

# Performance Measurement

Performance indicators shall support:

- Governance review
- Capacity planning
- Operational improvement
- Risk identification
- Platform evolution

Performance metrics shall never replace constitutional governance.

---

# Constitutional Constraints

Analytics shall never:

- Rewrite execution evidence
- Conceal failures
- Override governance
- Alter constitutional history

---

# Governance

Execution analytics provide measurable insight while preserving constitutional integrity and historical truth.
