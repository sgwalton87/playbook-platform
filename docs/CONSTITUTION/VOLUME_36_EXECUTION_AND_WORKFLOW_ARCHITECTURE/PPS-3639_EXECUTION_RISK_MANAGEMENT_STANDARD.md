# Purpose

Establish the constitutional architecture governing execution risk management.

Execution risk shall be identified, evaluated, mitigated, monitored, and governed before, during, and after execution.

---

# Constitutional Principles

Execution risk management shall be:

- Proactive
- Observable
- Explainable
- Governed
- Auditable

---

# Governance

Risk informs execution.

Risk shall never override constitutional authority.
