# Purpose

Define the constitutional architecture governing execution policy evaluation.

The Policy Engine evaluates execution against constitutional and organizational policies before, during, and after execution.

---

# Constitutional Principles

Policy evaluation shall be:

- Deterministic
- Explainable
- Observable
- Auditable
- Governed

---

# Policy Responsibilities

The Policy Engine evaluates:

- Authorization
- Security
- Compliance
- Organizational policy
- Execution constraints
- Human approval requirements

---

# Constitutional Constraints

The Policy Engine shall never:

- Modify constitutional authority
- Rewrite execution evidence
- Bypass governance

---

# Governance

Policy engines enforce constitutional policy.

They do not define constitutional policy.
