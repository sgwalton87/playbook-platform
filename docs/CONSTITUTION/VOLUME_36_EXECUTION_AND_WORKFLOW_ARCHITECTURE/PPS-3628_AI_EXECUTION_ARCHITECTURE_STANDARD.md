# Purpose

Establish the constitutional architecture governing artificial intelligence execution throughout the Playbook Platform.

Artificial intelligence extends execution capability while remaining constitutionally subordinate to human authority.

AI shall execute only within governed constitutional boundaries.

---

# Scope

Applies to:

- AI assistants
- AI execution engines
- AI planning systems
- AI workflow execution
- AI recommendations
- AI automation
- Future artificial intelligence technologies

---

# Constitutional Principles

AI execution shall remain:

- Human-governed
- Explainable
- Observable
- Deterministic where required
- Recoverable
- Auditable
- Constitutionally constrained

---

# AI Responsibilities

Artificial intelligence may:

- Recommend
- Plan
- Analyze
- Coordinate
- Execute authorized tasks
- Detect anomalies
- Optimize workflows

Artificial intelligence shall never independently redefine constitutional authority.

---

# Constitutional Constraints

AI execution shall never:

- Self-authorize
- Self-certify
- Amend constitutional policy
- Conceal reasoning
- Rewrite historical evidence
- Circumvent governance

---

# Explainability

Every AI execution shall preserve:

- Inputs
- Reasoning references
- Decisions
- Outputs
- Confidence indicators
- Human oversight

---

# Governance

Artificial intelligence remains an execution participant.

Constitutional authority remains human.
