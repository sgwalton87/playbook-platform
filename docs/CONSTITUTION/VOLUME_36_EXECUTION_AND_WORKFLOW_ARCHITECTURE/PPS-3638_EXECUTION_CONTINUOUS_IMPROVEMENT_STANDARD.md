# Purpose

Establish the constitutional architecture governing continuous improvement of execution.

Execution shall evolve through governed learning while preserving constitutional stability.

---

# Constitutional Principles

Continuous improvement shall be:

- Evidence-based
- Governed
- Explainable
- Observable
- Incremental

---

# Improvement Sources

Continuous improvement may utilize:

- Execution analytics
- Workflow optimization
- Human feedback
- AI recommendations
- Governance reviews
- Eligibility, execution, outcome, and evidence certification decisions

---

# Constitutional Constraints

Continuous improvement shall never:

- Rewrite historical execution
- Circumvent governance
- Reduce observability
- Eliminate audit evidence

---

# Governance

Improvement evolves execution.

Constitutional authority evolves only through constitutional amendment.
