# Purpose

Define the constitutional architecture governing workflow optimization.

Optimization improves efficiency while preserving constitutional behavior.

Optimization shall never alter constitutional authority.

---

# Scope

Applies to:

- Workflow execution
- Automation
- Enterprise workflows
- AI-assisted workflows
- Distributed workflows

---

# Constitutional Principles

Workflow optimization shall remain:

- Deterministic
- Explainable
- Observable
- Governed
- Measurable

---

# Optimization Model

Workflow optimization may improve:

- Execution efficiency
- Resource utilization
- Response time
- Recovery effectiveness
- Human interaction
- Automation quality

---

# Constitutional Constraints

Optimization shall never:

- Bypass governance
- Remove approval requirements
- Alter constitutional truth
- Reduce observability
- Eliminate audit evidence

---

# Continuous Improvement

Optimization shall occur through governed analysis supported by constitutional evidence.

---

# Governance

Workflow optimization preserves constitutional behavior while improving operational performance.
