# Purpose

Establish the constitutional governance framework for autonomous task execution.

Autonomy increases execution efficiency.

Autonomy does not diminish constitutional accountability.

---

# Scope

Applies to:

- Autonomous workflows
- Autonomous planning
- Autonomous execution
- Autonomous optimization
- Future autonomous platform capabilities

---

# Constitutional Principles

Autonomous execution shall remain:

- Authorized
- Observable
- Explainable
- Governed
- Recoverable
- Human-supervised

---

# Autonomous Task Model

Every autonomous task shall define:

- Constitutional objective
- Authorized scope
- Execution boundaries
- Success criteria
- Escalation conditions
- Human intervention thresholds

---

# Human Intervention

Autonomous execution shall pause or escalate whenever:

- Constitutional ambiguity exists
- Confidence thresholds are exceeded
- Policy conflicts occur
- Authorization becomes invalid
- Human review is required

---

# Constitutional Guarantees

Autonomous execution shall never:

- Expand its own authority
- Rewrite governance
- Override human decisions
- Conceal reasoning
- Alter constitutional history

---

# Governance

Autonomous execution remains constitutionally subordinate to human authority and constitutional governance.
