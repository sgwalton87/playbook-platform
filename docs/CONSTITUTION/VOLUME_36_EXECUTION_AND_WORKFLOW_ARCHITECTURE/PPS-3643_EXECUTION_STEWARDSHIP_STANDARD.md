# Purpose

Define the constitutional responsibilities for stewardship of execution architecture.

Stewardship preserves long-term constitutional consistency, quality, and trust.

---

# Constitutional Principles

Stewardship shall be:

- Responsible
- Transparent
- Accountable
- Sustainable
- Governed

---

# Stewardship Responsibilities

Stewards shall preserve:

- Constitutional integrity
- Historical continuity
- Documentation quality
- Governance consistency
- Platform trust

---

# Governance

Execution stewardship ensures the execution architecture remains healthy across future constitutional generations.
