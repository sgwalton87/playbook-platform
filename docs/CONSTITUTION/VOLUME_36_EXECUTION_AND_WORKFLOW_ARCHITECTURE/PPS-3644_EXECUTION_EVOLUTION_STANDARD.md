# Purpose

Establish the constitutional architecture governing the long-term evolution of execution throughout the Playbook Platform.

Execution architecture shall evolve through constitutional amendment while preserving backward trust, historical continuity, and governance integrity.

---

# Constitutional Principles

Execution evolution shall be:

- Deliberate
- Governed
- Backward-aware
- Evidence-based
- Constitutionally authorized

---

# Evolution Model

Execution evolution shall preserve:

- Historical execution truth
- Constitutional compatibility
- Governance continuity
- Organizational trust
- Future adaptability

---

# Governance

Execution evolves.

The Constitution determines how that evolution occurs.
