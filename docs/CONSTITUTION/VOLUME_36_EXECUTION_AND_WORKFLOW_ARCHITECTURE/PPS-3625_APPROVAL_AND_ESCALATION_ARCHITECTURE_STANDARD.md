# Purpose

Establish the constitutional architecture governing approvals, escalations, delegated decision authority, and governance intervention throughout the Playbook Platform.

Approval is the constitutional mechanism by which human authority authorizes execution beyond automated decision boundaries.

---

# Scope

Applies to:

- Human approvals
- Administrative approvals
- Executive approvals
- Compliance approvals
- Multi-stage approval chains
- Escalation workflows
- Delegated approvals

---

# Constitutional Principles

Approvals shall be:

- Explicit
- Observable
- Auditable
- Traceable
- Governed
- Revocable only through constitutional authority

---

# Approval Model

Every approval shall define:

- Approving authority
- Approval scope
- Decision criteria
- Required evidence
- Expiration
- Escalation path
- Audit record

---

# Escalation

Escalations may occur due to:

- Timeout
- Risk threshold
- Missing authority
- Policy conflict
- Organizational hierarchy
- Constitutional exception

---

# Constitutional Guarantees

Approvals shall never:

- Be implied
- Be hidden
- Circumvent governance
- Replace constitutional authority

---

# Governance

Approvals preserve human authority while enabling governed platform execution.
