# Purpose

Establish the constitutional architecture governing human participation within execution.

Playbook exists to empower people.

Automation, intelligence, and workflow orchestration shall reinforce human decision-making rather than replace constitutional human authority.

---

# Scope

Applies to:

- Human approvals
- Administrative review
- Expert review
- AI-assisted recommendations
- Escalation workflows
- Governance decisions
- Exception handling
- Future human-supervised execution models

---

# Constitutional Principles

Execution involving human participation shall remain:

- Human-directed
- Transparent
- Explainable
- Observable
- Accountable
- Governed
- Auditable

---

# Human Authority

Humans retain constitutional authority over:

- Final approvals
- Policy decisions
- Governance actions
- Constitutional amendments
- Exceptional execution
- Override decisions

Artificial intelligence shall never independently acquire constitutional authority.

---

# Human Approval Gates

Approval gates shall define:

- Required reviewer
- Approval criteria
- Escalation path
- Timeout behavior
- Rejection behavior
- Audit evidence

Approval decisions shall become immutable execution evidence.

---

# AI Assistance

Artificial intelligence may:

- Recommend
- Analyze
- Prioritize
- Summarize
- Detect anomalies

Artificial intelligence shall not:

- Self-authorize
- Self-govern
- Override constitutional controls
- Conceal reasoning

---

# Exception Handling

Human review shall be mandatory whenever:

- Constitutional conflicts occur
- Policy ambiguity exists
- Exceptional authority is requested
- Safety thresholds are exceeded
- Automated confidence falls below governance standards

---

# Governance

Human participation remains the constitutional safeguard preserving accountability, trust, and platform legitimacy.
