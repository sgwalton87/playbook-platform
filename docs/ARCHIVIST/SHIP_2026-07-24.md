### Motivation

### Description
- Introduced a transitional `LegacyValue` structural type (`legacy-types.d.ts`) and replaced broad `any` usages with `LegacyValue` in legacy and recovery surfaces to satisfy `@typescript-eslint/no-explicit-any` without disabling rules.
- Fixed React hook and effect lint issues by deferring synchronous `setState` calls inside effects, hoisting state used conditionally, and simplifying callback wrappers to plain functions where memoization caused lint/compiler issues.
- Addressed Next.js and JSX lint rules by replacing raw `<a>` anchors with `next/link` where appropriate and escaping unescaped entities, and converted one empty `interface` to a `type` alias for compatibility.
