## Failure And Recovery

No operational failure occurred because execution never began. Mission Control stopped at preflight. Recovery requires canonical repository-context reconciliation and human activation; it does not permit manual runtime edits, invented approvals, partial advancement, or retrospective evidence.

## Certification Position

PBOS has a repeatable preflight and authority architecture, but it has not completed its first autonomous operational loop. Certification can be reconsidered only after one fully admitted execution produces passing validation, durable evidence, and a legitimate lifecycle transition.
