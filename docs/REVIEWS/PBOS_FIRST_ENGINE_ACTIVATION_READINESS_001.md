# PBOS First Engine Activation Readiness 001

**Purpose:** Assess whether the Scholar Record foundation engine may be activated.

**Owner:** Playbook OS Engineering

**Last Updated:** July 30, 2026

## Decision

**IMPLEMENTATION READY**

**PRODUCTION ACTIVATION WITHHELD**

The Scholar Record engine enforces Kernel activation, identity, authority, tenant isolation, human control, evidence, provenance, optimistic revision, and immutable history.

Production activation is prohibited because production certification is blocked. No activation artifact, lifecycle transition, or runtime engine registration was created.

## Remaining Conditions

- Certified production adapters
- Production readiness certification
- Durable Scholar Record persistence adapter
- Privacy, retention, and recovery evidence
- Capacity and performance evidence
- Governed activation evidence persistence
