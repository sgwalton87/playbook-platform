# PBOS Volume 35 Constitutional Reconciliation Package

## Purpose

The PBOS Volume 35 Constitutional Reconciliation Package establishes the governance process required to resolve authority conflicts, duplicate constitutional claims, incomplete metadata, and competing architectural identities identified during the PBOS Volume 35 Forensic Audit.

This package does not modify Volume 35 artifacts.

This package creates the decision framework required before any remediation, migration, amendment, or certification activity occurs.

---

# Reconciliation Objective

The purpose of this reconciliation process is to restore:

- singular constitutional authority,
- explicit inheritance,
- deterministic ownership,
- artifact lifecycle governance,
- precedence clarity,
- historical integrity.

The objective is not to reduce architectural ambition.

The objective is to ensure PBOS has one authoritative source of truth.

---

# Current Condition

The forensic audit identified:

- multiple Volume 35 identity claims,
- competing governance authorities,
- duplicate responsibility ownership,
- incomplete lifecycle metadata,
- unresolved legacy corpus relationships,
- missing extension governance,
- incomplete artifact certification requirements.

These findings prevent Volume 35 certification.

---

# Reconciliation Principles

## Preserve History

No historical artifact will be deleted.

Previous architectural decisions remain part of PBOS institutional history.

---

## Establish Singular Authority

One constitutional root must govern Volume 35.

Subordinate documents may define domain rules but may not compete with root authority.

---

## Separate Scope From Authority

A document may own a domain.

A document may not claim authority beyond its defined scope.

---

## Govern Before Modifying

No canonical artifact changes occur until:

- identity is resolved,
- authority is assigned,
- precedence is defined,
- lifecycle rules are established.

---

# Reconciliation Scope
