# Purpose

The PBOS Volume 35 Canonical Identity Decision establishes the authoritative identity, scope, and precedence model for Volume 35 following the discovery of competing architectural corpora.

This document records the governance decision required before remediation, migration, certification, or promotion activities occur.

---

# Executive Decision

## Decision Status

PENDING GOVERNED APPROVAL

---

# Decision Required

PBOS must establish one canonical Volume 35 identity.

The current conflict exists because multiple document collections describe overlapping platform experience responsibilities.

PBOS cannot maintain multiple competing constitutional authorities.

---

# Current Identity Conflict

The forensic audit identified two competing architectural identities:

## Identity A

Volume 35:

Platform Experience Architecture

Purpose:

Defines the architectural governance layer between product architecture and implementation specifications.

Primary concerns:

- interface realization,
- experience architecture,
- component systems,
- navigation realization,
- design standards.

---

## Identity B

Volume 35:

Playbook Design System / PDS Legacy Corpus

Purpose:

Historical design-system-oriented architecture.

Primary concerns:

- design tokens,
- components,
- patterns,
- interface consistency.

---

# Constitutional Requirement

Only one identity may hold canonical authority.

The alternate identity must become one of:

- historical predecessor,
- migrated architecture,
- superseded corpus,
- archived reference.

It may not remain a competing source of truth.

---

## Recommended Decision

Establish:

# Volume 35 — Platform Experience Architecture

# Architectural Boundary

Volume 35 should govern:

- interface architecture,
- experience realization,
- component relationships,
- interaction standards,
- platform experience consistency.

Volume 35 should not govern:

- strategic product decisions,
- application composition,
- runtime behavior,
- security policy,
- AI governance,
- execution authorization.
