# Purpose

The PBOS Volume 35 Constitutional Recertification Plan defines the requirements, evidence standards, governance controls, and validation process required to restore Volume 35 certification after forensic audit findings resulted in certification being withheld.

This document defines how PBOS proves that Volume 35 has transitioned from:

architecturally documented

to:

constitutionally governed.

# Recertification Objective

The objective of recertification is to demonstrate that Volume 35 satisfies enterprise architecture governance requirements.

Certification must prove:

- one canonical identity,
- one constitutional root,
- explicit inheritance,
- unique ownership,
- complete artifact metadata,
- deterministic validation,
- historical preservation.

# Recertification Gates

---

# Gate 1 — Canonical Identity Validation

Objective:

Confirm Volume 35 has one recognized identity.

Required Evidence:

- canonical identity decision,
- registry entry,
- precedence declaration,
- historical migration record.

Pass Criteria:

PBOS can answer:

"What is Volume 35?"

without ambiguity.

---

# Gate 2 — Authority Graph Validation

Objective:

Confirm governance ownership is singular.

Required Evidence:

- authority graph,
- parent relationships,
- inheritance paths,
- ownership matrix.

Pass Criteria:

PBOS can answer:

"Who owns this decision?"

without conflicting answers.

---

# Gate 3 — Artifact Governance Validation

Objective:

Confirm all canonical artifacts are governed objects.

Required Evidence:

Each artifact contains:

- immutable identifier,
- volume identity,
- artifact type,
- parent authority,
- owner,
- steward,
- validator,
- certifier,
- lifecycle state,
- dependencies.

Pass Criteria:

No canonical artifact exists without governance metadata.

---

# Gate 4 — Lifecycle Validation

Objective:

Confirm architectural change is controlled.

Required Evidence:

Lifecycle states:


PROPOSED

↓

REVIEWED

↓

CERTIFIED

↓

CANONICAL

↓

DEPRECATED

↓

RETIRED

↓

ARCHIVED


Pass Criteria:

Every lifecycle transition has:

- authority,
- evidence,
- timestamp,
- historical record.

---

# Gate 5 — Legacy Migration Validation

Objective:

Confirm historical PDS architecture has resolved authority relationships.

Required Evidence:

- migration matrix,
- supersession records,
- archived references.

Pass Criteria:

No legacy artifact can override canonical Volume 35 authority.

---

# Gate 6 — Enterprise Governance Validation

Objective:

Determine whether Volume 35 can operate within enterprise-scale governance.

Evaluate:

- extensibility,
- partner integration,
- auditability,
- controlled evolution,
- ownership clarity.

Pass Criteria:

External organizations can understand:

- what Volume 35 governs,
- what it does not govern,
- how extensions are approved.
