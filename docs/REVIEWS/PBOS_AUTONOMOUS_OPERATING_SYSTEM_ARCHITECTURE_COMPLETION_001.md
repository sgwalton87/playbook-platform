## Completion Decision

ARCHITECTURE COMPLETE; OPERATIONALIZATION WITHHELD.

## Domains Established

Mission Intelligence aligns authorized objectives and outcomes. Architectural Memory preserves reasoning. World Model represents evidence-bound ecosystem state. Risk Intelligence assesses exposure. Simulation evaluates bounded scenarios. Agent Governance controls non-authoritative AI agents. Outcome Evaluation connects action to mission evidence.

## Dependencies And Roadmap

Implementation order is shared identity and provenance, Architectural Memory, Mission Intelligence, World Model, Risk Intelligence, bounded Simulation, agent registration, then Outcome Evaluation. AI Governance, Organization Governance, Security, Validation, Certification, Observability, Resilience, and the Kernel remain cross-cutting authorities.

## Human Governance

Humans retain mission definition, strategy, risk acceptance, authorization, certification, production activation, and consequential decisions. Intelligence can recommend but cannot select outside the Planner or execute outside the Kernel.

## Remaining Risks

No domain has operational storage, validators, adapters, scale evidence, security certification, or production ownership. The architecture does not support autonomous production operation.

## Future Capabilities

Future PBOS may perform richer analysis, simulation, agent-assisted work, and outcome learning only after bounded implementation, shadow evaluation, adversarial testing, independent certification, and explicit human approval.
