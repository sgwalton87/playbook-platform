# Playbook Design System

Playbook should feel intelligent, warm, premium, youthful, trustworthy, alive, and calm.

## Philosophy

Playbook is not a school portal.

Playbook is a learner operating system.

Every screen should help the learner understand:

1. Who am I becoming?
2. What have I proven?
3. What opportunities are opening?
4. What should I do next?

## Signature Concepts

- Living Dashboard
- Academic DNA
- Opportunity Galaxy
- Compass Home
- Growth Journey
- Achievement Showcase
- Scholar Genome
