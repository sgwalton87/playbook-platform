# Playbook Color System

**Purpose:** Define governed brand and semantic color tokens.  
**Owner:** Playbook Design System Governance  
**Last Updated:** July 30, 2026

## Canonical Tokens

| Token | HEX | RGB | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|

| Playbook Orange | `#F97316` | 249, 115, 22 | Energy, action, opportunity |

| Surface | `#FFFFFF` | 255, 255, 255 | Primary content surface |

Blue, orange, Future Paper, Ink, Slate, Muted, Line, Surface, Gold, and Purple reconcile existing implementation and design documentation. Growth Teal is established here as the governed development token.

## Usage

Blue is the main interaction and navigation color. Orange marks a meaningful opportunity or action and must not dominate full surfaces. Gold is reserved for earned achievement, teal for growth, and purple for exploration. Status colors use text, icon, and labels in addition to color.

## Accessibility

Text and essential controls meet WCAG contrast requirements in every state. Light accent colors require dark foregrounds and cannot serve as body text on white. Focus indicators maintain at least 3:1 contrast against adjacent colors. High-contrast and forced-color modes preserve meaning.
