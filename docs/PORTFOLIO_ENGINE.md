# 🎓 Playbook Portfolio Engine™

Version: 1.0

Status: Core Platform Engine

Owner: Playbook Series

---

# Mission

The Portfolio Engine is responsible for maintaining the verified digital identity of every Scholar.

It receives verified events from every Playbook product and transforms those events into a living Portfolio that powers AI, documents, recommendations, opportunity matching, and lifelong achievement tracking.

The Portfolio is the single source of truth.
