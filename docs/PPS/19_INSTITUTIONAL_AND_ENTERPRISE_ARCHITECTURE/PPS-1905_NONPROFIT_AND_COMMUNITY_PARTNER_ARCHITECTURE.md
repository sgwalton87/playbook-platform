# Purpose

Establish the constitutional architecture governing partnerships with nonprofit organizations, community organizations, foundations, and civic partners throughout the Playbook ecosystem.

---

# Scope

Applies to:

- Nonprofits
- Foundations
- Community organizations
- Civic organizations
- Service organizations
- Community initiatives

---

# Authority

Community partnerships exist to expand resources, relationships, and opportunities while preserving trust and accountability.

---

# Constitutional Principles

## Community Knowledge Matters

Organizations closest to communities provide valuable insight and support.

---

## Shared Mission

Partnerships should advance meaningful outcomes.

---

## Trust Through Service

Community relationships are built through consistent action.

---

## Sustainable Impact

Partnerships should create lasting improvement.

---

# Community Partner Architecture

Includes:

- Organization profiles
- Programs
- Resources
- Events
- Volunteer opportunities
- Community initiatives
- Impact reporting

---

# Responsibilities

Community partners shall:

- Provide accurate information.
- Support members responsibly.
- Maintain ethical standards.
- Protect participant information.
- Collaborate effectively.

---

# Validation Rules

Systems shall:

- Verify organizations.
- Maintain partnership records.
- Track opportunities.
- Preserve community trust.

---

# Compliance Requirements

Integrates with:

- Community Architecture
- Opportunity Architecture
- Event Architecture
- Governance Architecture

---

# Implementation Guidance

Community partnerships should strengthen existing ecosystems rather than duplicate or replace trusted local organizations.

---

# Definition of Done

Nonprofit and community partnerships expand access, strengthen relationships, and create measurable community impact.

---

# Future Amendments

Future versions may include:

- Community impact measurement
- Regional partner networks
- Civic engagement systems
- Community resource exchanges
