# Purpose

Establish the constitutional architecture governing partnerships with schools, districts, charter networks, and educational institutions serving learners.

---

# Scope

Applies to:

- Elementary schools
- Middle schools
- High schools
- Charter schools
- District systems
- Educational networks

---

# Authority

School partnerships exist to strengthen student success, educator capacity, family engagement, and equitable opportunity access.

---

# Constitutional Principles

## Student Outcomes First

Every implementation decision should improve learner outcomes.

---

## Educator Partnership

Technology should support educators, not replace them.

---

## Family Engagement

Families are important contributors to student success.

---

## Equity Through Access

Systems should reduce opportunity gaps.

---

# School Architecture Components

Includes:

- Institutional dashboards
- Student support systems
- Educator tools
- Counselor workflows
- Family engagement
- Opportunity pathways
- Reporting systems

---

# Responsibilities

School systems shall:

- Support implementation.
- Protect student information.
- Maintain appropriate access.
- Encourage adoption.

---

# Validation Rules

Systems shall:

- Respect educational privacy.
- Maintain permissions.
- Protect student data.
- Preserve institutional accountability.

---

# Definition of Done

School and district implementations successfully support students, educators, families, and administrators while maintaining constitutional standards.
