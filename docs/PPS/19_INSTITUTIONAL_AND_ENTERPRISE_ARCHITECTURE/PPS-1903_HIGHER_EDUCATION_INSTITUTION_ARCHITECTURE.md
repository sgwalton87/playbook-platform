# Purpose

Establish the constitutional architecture governing partnerships with colleges, universities, technical institutions, and postsecondary organizations throughout the Playbook ecosystem.

The Higher Education Institution Architecture defines how institutions collaborate with Playbook to support access, persistence, completion, career readiness, and lifelong engagement.

---

# Scope

Applies to:

- Universities
- Community colleges
- Technical schools
- Professional programs
- Graduate programs
- Postsecondary organizations
- Alumni networks

---

# Authority

Higher education partnerships shall strengthen student pathways while preserving student ownership, privacy, and agency.

Institutions participate as ecosystem partners, not owners of student identity.

---

# Constitutional Principles

## Access Through Alignment

Higher education partnerships should expand meaningful pathways.

---

## Student Success Beyond Admission

Admission is only one milestone.

Institutions should support:

- Persistence
- Completion
- Career readiness
- Alumni engagement

---

## Institutional Transparency

Students should understand:

- Programs
- Requirements
- Opportunities
- Outcomes
- Resources

---

## Lifelong Connection

Higher education relationships continue beyond graduation.

---

# Higher Education Architecture

Includes:

- Institutional profiles
- Admissions pathways
- Academic programs
- Student services
- Career pathways
- Alumni relationships
- Opportunity connections
- Institutional reporting

---

# Responsibilities

Higher education partners shall:

- Maintain accurate information.
- Support student pathways.
- Protect student data.
- Participate responsibly.
- Provide meaningful opportunities.

---

# Validation Rules

Systems shall:

- Verify institutional information.
- Maintain permission boundaries.
- Protect educational records.
- Preserve student control.

---

# Compliance Requirements

Integrates with:

- Scholar Record
- Learning Architecture
- Opportunity Architecture
- Career Journey Architecture
- Identity Systems

---

# Implementation Guidance

Higher education integrations should focus on helping students make informed decisions rather than simply promoting institutions.

---

# Definition of Done

Higher education partnerships successfully connect learners with pathways, resources, and relationships while maintaining constitutional standards.

---
