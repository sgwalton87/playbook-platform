# Purpose

The Intelligence Runtime governs how every intelligence engine executes, collaborates, and produces explainable recommendations throughout PBOS.

---

# Scope

Applies to every intelligence engine including Compass, Resume Intelligence, Scholarship Intelligence, Career Intelligence, Financial Intelligence, Mentor Intelligence, Recommendation Intelligence, and future intelligence systems.

---

# Authority

All intelligence execution shall occur through the Intelligence Runtime.

No intelligence engine may bypass constitutional governance or directly modify canonical platform data.

---

# Definitions

## Intelligence Engine

A specialized reasoning system responsible for a defined domain.

## Recommendation

Evidence-supported guidance presented to a user.

## Evidence

Canonical platform information supporting an intelligence decision.

---

# Constitutional Principles

## Evidence First

All intelligence shall originate from canonical evidence.

---

## Explainability

Every recommendation shall identify why it was produced.

---

## Human Agency

Recommendations inform decisions but never replace them.

---

## Non-Destructive Intelligence

Intelligence engines may recommend changes but shall never directly alter canonical records.

---

## Composable Intelligence

Multiple intelligence engines may collaborate through the runtime.

---

# Architecture

The Intelligence Runtime coordinates:

- Compass Intelligence
- Resume Intelligence
- Scholarship Intelligence
- Career Intelligence
- Financial Literacy Intelligence
- Mentor Intelligence
- Recommendation Intelligence
- Future Intelligence Engines

---

# Runtime Lifecycle

1. Observe
2. Gather Evidence
3. Analyze
4. Reason
5. Generate Recommendation
6. Explain
7. Present
8. Await Human Decision
9. Record Outcome

---

# Responsibilities

The Intelligence Runtime shall:

- Coordinate intelligence execution.
- Resolve dependencies.
- Prevent duplicate analysis.
- Explain every recommendation.
- Preserve execution history.
- Support collaborative reasoning.

---

# Interfaces

Communicates with:

- Journey Runtime
- Experience Runtime
- Recommendation Runtime
- State Management Runtime
- Event Runtime

---

# Validation Rules

The runtime shall:

- Reject unsupported recommendations.
- Require evidence for every recommendation.
- Prevent hallucinated canonical facts.
- Preserve reasoning history.

---

# Compliance Requirements

Every intelligence execution shall remain explainable, auditable, evidence-based, and constitutionally governed.

---

# Implementation Guidance

Intelligence engines should remain independently deployable while sharing runtime services.

---

# Definition of Done

The Intelligence Runtime is complete when every intelligence engine executes consistently, transparently, and from canonical evidence.

---

# Future Amendments

Future versions may introduce collaborative reasoning, distributed inference, adaptive orchestration, and enterprise intelligence marketplaces.
