Purpose

The Coach Operating System enables coaches to develop scholar-athletes academically, athletically, and personally.

Objectives

Coordinate:

- Team management
- Recruiting
- Eligibility
- Leadership
- Communication
- Performance

Dashboard

- Team Roster
- Recruiting
- Eligibility
- Calendar
- Messaging
- Performance
- Highlights
- Recommendations

Required Intelligence Engines

- Recruiting Intelligence
- Compass
- Performance Intelligence

Primary Workflows

- Manage roster.
- Track eligibility.
- Review highlights.
- Communicate with athletes.
- Recommend opportunities.

Permissions

Coaches manage only assigned teams and approved athlete information.

PBOS Responsibilities

Validate eligibility, recruiting, and permissions.

Validation Rules

Roster integrity required.

Coach Operating System standardized.
