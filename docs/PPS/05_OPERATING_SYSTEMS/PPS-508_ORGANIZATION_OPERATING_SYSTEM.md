Purpose

The Organization Operating System enables schools, nonprofits, community organizations, employers, colleges, and partner institutions to coordinate programs, opportunities, and learner engagement through the Playbook Platform.

Objectives

The Organization Operating System shall enable organizations to:

- Manage programs.
- Publish opportunities.
- Coordinate events.
- Enroll participants.
- Track engagement.
- Communicate with approved users.
- Measure organizational outcomes.

Canonical Principles

Organizations participate in the platform but do not own learner identities or Scholar Records.

Organizational data shall remain separate from canonical learner data.

Permissions

Organizations may manage only their own organizational resources and approved participant information.

Relationships

Consumes:

- Experience Platform
- Authorized Scholar Record references

Produces:

- Programs
- Opportunities

- Analytics

PBOS Responsibilities

PBOS shall:

- Validate organizational boundaries.
- Prevent unauthorized learner access.
- Audit organization activity.
- Verify program ownership.

Validation Rules

PBOS shall verify:

- Organizational ownership.
- Participant permissions.
- Resource integrity.
- Analytics consistency.
