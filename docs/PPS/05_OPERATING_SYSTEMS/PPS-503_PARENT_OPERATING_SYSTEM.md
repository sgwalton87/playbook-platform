Purpose

The Parent Operating System empowers parents, guardians, and approved caregivers to actively support a learner's educational, athletic, financial, and personal development while respecting learner privacy and institutional governance.

Objectives

The Parent Operating System shall enable users to:

- Monitor scholar progress.
- Support goal completion.
- Track academic milestones.
- Review financial aid readiness.
- Participate in mentoring.
- Coordinate family responsibilities.
- Communicate with approved educators.
- Celebrate learner achievements.

Canonical Principles

Parents support the Scholar Record but do not own it.

Learners remain the owners of their canonical records.

Access shall be determined by permissions, age, legal requirements, and organizational policy.

Target Users

- Parents
- Guardians
- Approved Caregivers

Optional Intelligence Engines

Primary Workflows

- Review learner progress.
- Approve permissions.
- Register for events.
- Monitor deadlines.
- Participate in family planning.
- Communicate with educators.

Permissions

May:

- View approved learner information.
- Communicate with approved users.
- Register for family events.

May Not:

- Modify Scholar Records.
- Override institutional records.
- Access restricted learner information.

Relationships

Consumes:

- Identity Domain
- Scholar Record
- Experience Platform

Produces:

- Family participation
- Communication records
- Event participation

PBOS Responsibilities

PBOS shall:

- Enforce privacy policies.
- Validate parental permissions.
- Preserve learner ownership.
- Audit access history.

Validation Rules

PBOS shall verify:

- Authorized relationships.
- Required permissions.
- Privacy compliance.
