Purpose

The Counselor Operating System coordinates academic planning, graduation readiness, college planning, and student support services.

Objectives

Support:

- Academic planning
- Graduation tracking
- College readiness
- Student intervention
- Family engagement

Dashboard

- Caseload
- Graduation Progress
- A-G Progress
- FAFSA Status
- Appointments
- Notes
- Compass

Required Intelligence Engines

- Compass
- College Readiness Intelligence
- Scholarship Intelligence

- Conduct advising sessions.
- Approve academic plans.
- Track interventions.
- Monitor graduation readiness.

Validate caseloads, graduation requirements, and privacy.
