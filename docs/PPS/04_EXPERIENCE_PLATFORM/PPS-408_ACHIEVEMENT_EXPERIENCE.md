Purpose

The Achievement Experience governs recognition displayed throughout the platform.

Achievement Types

- Badge
- Certificate
- Milestone
- Award
- Credential
- Honor

Display

Achievements may appear within:

- Feed
- Profile
- Scholar Record
- Resume
- Dashboard
- Public Profile

PBOS Responsibilities

PBOS shall:

- Validate achievement ownership.
- Preserve historical recognition.
- Prevent duplicate awards.

Definition of Done

Achievement Experience standardized.
