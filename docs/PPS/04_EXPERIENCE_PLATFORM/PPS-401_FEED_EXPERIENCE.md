Purpose

The Feed Experience governs how platform activity is shared, discovered, and consumed.

Objectives

The feed shall support:

- Community engagement
- Educational content
- Achievements
- Announcements
- Media
- Discussions
- Organizational updates

Feed Objects

The Feed may contain:

- Posts
- Images
- Videos
- Documents
- Polls
- Announcements
- Shared achievements
- Events
- Courses

Visibility

Feed content shall support:

- Public
- Organization
- Private
- Role-based
- Group-based

Relationships

The Feed references:

- User Profiles
- Scholar Records
- Courses
- Events
- Achievements

PBOS Responsibilities

PBOS shall:

- Validate visibility.
- Verify ownership.
- Preserve feed history.

Definition of Done

Feed experience standardized.
