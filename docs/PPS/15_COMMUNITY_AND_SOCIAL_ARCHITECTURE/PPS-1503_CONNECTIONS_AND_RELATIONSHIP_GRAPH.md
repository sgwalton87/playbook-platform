# Purpose

Establish the constitutional framework governing relationships between individuals, organizations, mentors, educators, employers, institutions, and future entities throughout the Playbook Platform.

---

# Scope

Applies to every connection established through the platform.

Examples include:

- Scholar ↔ Mentor
- Scholar ↔ Coach
- Scholar ↔ Parent
- Scholar ↔ University
- Scholar ↔ Employer
- Mentor ↔ Organization
- Organization ↔ School
- Team ↔ Athlete
- Administrator ↔ Institution

---

# Authority

Relationships shall strengthen educational, professional, athletic, and community outcomes while preserving individual agency.

---

# Constitutional Principles

- Mutual consent.
- Transparency.
- Explainable recommendations.
- Permission-aware relationships.
- Privacy.
- Trust.

---

# Relationship Types

Supported relationships include:

- Following
- Mentorship
- Coaching
- Advising
- Teaching
- Employment
- Alumni
- Team Membership
- Organization Membership
- Family Relationships
- Administrative Oversight

---

# Responsibilities

The relationship graph shall:

- Connect appropriate users.
- Strengthen opportunity discovery.
- Improve recommendations.
- Support collaboration.
- Preserve relationship history.
- Respect privacy boundaries.

---

# Validation Rules

Relationship systems shall:

- Require consent when appropriate.
- Preserve audit history.
- Validate permissions.
- Prevent unauthorized access.
- Support relationship lifecycle management.

---

# Compliance Requirements

Relationship architecture shall integrate with:

- Recommendation engines
- Events
- Messaging
- Organizations
- Learning pathways
- Community governance

---

# Definition of Done

The relationship graph accurately models trusted human connections while protecting privacy, transparency, and constitutional governance.

---

# Future Amendments

- Multi-generational mentorship graphs
- Professional networking intelligence
- Opportunity relationship mapping
- AI-assisted relationship recommendations
