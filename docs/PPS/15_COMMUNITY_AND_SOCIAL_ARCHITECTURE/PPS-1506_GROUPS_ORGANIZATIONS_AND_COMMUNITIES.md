# Purpose

Define the constitutional architecture governing organized communities, institutions, teams, cohorts, clubs, organizations, and collaborative groups throughout the Playbook Platform.

---

# Scope

Applies to:

- Schools
- Universities
- Athletic teams
- Nonprofits
- Employers
- Companies
- Community organizations
- Clubs
- Cohorts
- Classes
- Professional associations
- Alumni networks
- Future organizational structures

---

# Authority

Organizations shall exist to facilitate collaboration, learning, leadership, and opportunity while remaining consistent with constitutional governance.

---

# Constitutional Principles

## Shared Purpose

Every organization exists to advance a clearly defined mission.

---

## Distributed Leadership

Leadership responsibilities should be appropriately delegated while maintaining accountability.

---

## Inclusive Participation

Communities should encourage participation from all qualified members.

---

## Constitutional Governance

Every organization remains subject to the Playbook Constitution.

---

## Healthy Collaboration

Organizational structures should encourage cooperation rather than competition.

---

# Organizational Components

Organizations may contain:

- Members
- Roles
- Permissions
- Teams
- Committees
- Events
- Courses
- Resources
- Announcements
- Discussions
- Reports
- Dashboards

---

# Responsibilities

Organizations shall:

- Support collaboration.
- Share knowledge.
- Develop leaders.
- Manage memberships.
- Protect member information.
- Preserve institutional history.

---

# Validation Rules

Organization systems shall:

- Validate permissions.
- Preserve organizational history.
- Maintain membership records.
- Record administrative actions.
- Protect organizational resources.

---

# Compliance Requirements

Organizational architecture shall integrate with:

- Identity
- Events
- Messaging
- Community governance
- Learning systems
- Security architecture

---

# Implementation Guidance

Organizations should remain flexible enough to support institutions ranging from small mentoring groups to large universities and enterprise partners.

---

# Definition of Done

Organizations effectively coordinate people, knowledge, and opportunities while remaining constitutionally governed.

---

# Future Amendments

Future revisions may include:

- Cross-organization collaboration
- International chapters
- Federation architecture
- Enterprise governance extensions
- Community health metrics
