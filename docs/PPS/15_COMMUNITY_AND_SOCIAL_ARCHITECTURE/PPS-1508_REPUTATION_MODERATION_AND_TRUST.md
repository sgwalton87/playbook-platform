# Purpose

Establish the constitutional architecture governing trust, reputation, moderation, safety, and community integrity throughout the Playbook Platform.

The purpose of moderation is not punishment—it is preserving a healthy learning community where every member can safely grow.

---

# Scope

Applies to:

- Reputation systems
- Community moderation
- Reports
- Appeals
- Content review
- Trust indicators
- Verification
- Community standards
- Administrative enforcement
- Future safety systems

---

# Authority

Community trust shall be earned through authentic participation, integrity, accountability, and positive contribution.

Artificial engagement shall never determine reputation.

---

# Constitutional Principles

## Trust Is Earned

Reputation develops through consistent behavior.

---

## Safety First

Protecting community members takes priority over engagement.

---

## Fair Process

Moderation decisions should be explainable, reviewable, and consistently applied.

---

## Transparency

Users should understand significant moderation decisions affecting them.

---

## Restoration

Whenever appropriate, moderation should prioritize education and restoration before permanent exclusion.

---

# Reputation Components

Reputation may consider:

- Constructive participation
- Educational contributions
- Mentorship
- Leadership
- Community service
- Verified achievements
- Professional conduct
- Positive collaboration

Popularity alone shall never determine reputation.

---

# Moderation Lifecycle

1. Detection
2. Review
3. Investigation
4. Decision
5. Notification
6. Appeal
7. Resolution
8. Historical audit

---

# Responsibilities

Moderation systems shall:

- Protect community members.
- Reduce harmful behavior.
- Preserve evidence.
- Support appeals.
- Maintain consistency.
- Record audit history.

---

# Validation Rules

Moderation shall:

- Record every administrative action.
- Preserve constitutional due process.
- Maintain immutable audit logs.
- Respect privacy requirements.
- Prevent abuse of authority.

---

# Compliance Requirements

Trust systems shall integrate with:

- Security Architecture
- Identity
- Community Governance
- Audit systems
- Recommendation engines

---

# Implementation Guidance

Automation may assist moderation but shall not eliminate meaningful human oversight for significant enforcement actions.

---

# Definition of Done

Community trust is maintained through transparent, fair, consistent, and constitutionally governed moderation.

---

# Future Amendments

Future revisions may include:

- AI moderation assistants
- Community review panels
- Constitutional reputation scoring
- Behavioral wellness analytics
- International compliance frameworks
