# Purpose

Define the constitutional architecture governing digital identity, public profiles, verified accounts, professional presence, and personal representation throughout the Playbook Platform.

---

# Scope

Applies to:

- Scholar profiles
- Scholar-athlete profiles
- Mentor profiles
- Coach profiles
- Parent profiles
- Organization profiles
- University profiles
- Employer profiles
- Team profiles
- Future platform identities

---

# Authority

Profiles shall accurately represent the individual or organization they describe while preserving user privacy and constitutional trust.

---

# Constitutional Principles

- Authentic identity.
- User ownership.
- Privacy by design.
- Professional presentation.
- Explainable verification.
- Portable identity.

---

# Profile Components

Profiles may include:

- Biography
- Education
- Athletics
- Professional experience
- Skills
- Certifications
- Achievements
- Portfolio
- Resume
- Transcript
- Badges
- Community activity
- Recommendations
- Goals

---

# Verification

The platform may verify:

- Email
- School affiliation
- Organization membership
- Professional credentials
- Athletic participation
- Identity documents
- Administrative approval

Verification methods shall remain transparent.

---

# Responsibilities

Identity systems shall:

- Preserve profile integrity.
- Protect sensitive information.
- Support profile portability.
- Prevent impersonation.
- Record verification history.

---

# Validation Rules

Identity systems shall:

- Prevent duplicate identities.
- Maintain audit history.
- Preserve ownership.
- Validate verification status.

---

# Compliance Requirements

Identity architecture shall integrate with:

- Scholar Record
- Authentication
- Community
- Learning
- Recommendation engines

---

# Definition of Done

Every profile accurately represents its owner while maintaining constitutional privacy, trust, and security.

---

# Future Amendments

- Decentralized identity
- International credential verification
- Professional licensing verification
- Government identity integration
