# Purpose

Establish the constitutional architecture governing direct communication, collaboration, messaging, conversations, and digital correspondence across the Playbook Platform.

---

# Scope

Applies to:

- Direct messages
- Group messaging
- Mentor communication
- Instructor communication
- Parent communication
- Organization communication
- Administrative messaging
- AI-assisted communication
- Future communication channels

---

# Authority

Messaging exists to strengthen relationships, accelerate learning, and facilitate collaboration.

Communication systems shall never compromise constitutional privacy, security, or trust.

---

# Constitutional Principles

- Respectful communication.
- Privacy by default.
- Secure delivery.
- User control.
- Explainable automation.
- Healthy communication.

---

# Communication Types

Supported communications include:

- Individual messaging
- Group conversations
- Announcements
- Broadcast messages
- Mentorship conversations
- Academic discussions
- Professional networking
- Event communication
- AI-assisted drafting

---

# Responsibilities

Messaging systems shall:

- Deliver communications reliably.
- Protect confidential conversations.
- Respect notification preferences.
- Support accessibility.
- Preserve message history.
- Enable collaboration.

---

# Validation Rules

Messaging systems shall:

- Authenticate participants.
- Protect message integrity.
- Preserve delivery history.
- Record moderation actions.
- Respect permissions.

---

# Compliance Requirements

Messaging shall integrate with:

- Identity systems
- Community architecture
- Events
- Organizations
- Notification systems
- Security architecture

---

# Implementation Guidance

Communication features should encourage productive, respectful, and educational conversations while reducing unnecessary interruptions and spam.

---

# Definition of Done

Messaging enables secure, accessible, trustworthy communication that strengthens community relationships.

---

# Future Amendments

- Live translation
- AI conversation summaries
- Meeting scheduling
- Voice messaging
- Secure institutional communications
