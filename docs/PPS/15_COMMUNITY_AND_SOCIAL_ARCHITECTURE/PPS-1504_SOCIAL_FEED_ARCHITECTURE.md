# Purpose

Establish the constitutional architecture governing content discovery, social feeds, activity streams, educational updates, and information distribution throughout the Playbook Platform.

The Playbook Feed exists to help people discover opportunities, knowledge, relationships, achievements, and educational resources—not to maximize addictive engagement.

---

# Scope

Applies to:

- Home feeds
- Community feeds
- Organization feeds
- School feeds
- Team feeds
- Course activity
- Achievement feeds
- Event feeds
- Mentorship updates
- Administrative announcements
- Future activity streams

---

# Authority

The feed shall prioritize meaningful educational value over engagement metrics.

Content ranking shall never intentionally amplify outrage, misinformation, harassment, or divisive behavior for increased interaction.

---

# Constitutional Principles

## Educational Value

Learning opportunities shall receive greater priority than entertainment.

---

## Authentic Discovery

Users should discover meaningful people, organizations, events, and opportunities that contribute to their goals.

---

## Explainable Recommendations

Feed recommendations shall be explainable.

Users should understand why important content appears.

---

## Healthy Engagement

The feed shall encourage participation without encouraging compulsive usage.

---

## Community Safety

Unsafe content shall never receive algorithmic amplification.

---

## Transparency

Ranking systems shall remain constitutionally auditable.

---

# Feed Components

The feed architecture supports:

- Posts
- Articles
- Videos
- Photos
- Documents
- Course updates
- Community announcements
- Scholarship opportunities
- Job opportunities
- Internship opportunities
- Athletic recruiting updates
- Mentorship recommendations
- Events
- Achievements
- Certifications
- Badges

---

# Feed Ranking

Feed ranking may consider:

- Relationship strength
- Educational relevance
- Community participation
- User interests
- Role
- Organization membership
- Location (when appropriate)
- Opportunity relevance
- Time sensitivity

Popularity alone shall never determine ranking.

---

# Responsibilities

The feed architecture shall:

- Promote educational opportunity.
- Encourage collaboration.
- Surface trustworthy information.
- Reduce information overload.
- Protect user wellbeing.
- Maintain constitutional transparency.

---

# Validation Rules

Feed systems shall:

- Preserve ranking history.
- Record recommendation sources.
- Support chronological viewing.
- Prevent prohibited amplification.
- Respect user preferences.

---

# Compliance Requirements

The feed architecture shall integrate with:

- Community relationships
- Recommendation engines
- Organizations
- Events
- Learning systems
- Reputation systems

---

# Implementation Guidance

Engineers shall optimize for:

- Educational impact.
- Opportunity discovery.
- Community health.
- Accessibility.
- Explainability.
- Long-term trust.

---

# Definition of Done

Feed experiences consistently surface meaningful educational content while maintaining transparency, safety, and constitutional integrity.

---

# Future Amendments

Future revisions may include:

- AI-assisted educational summarization
- Opportunity forecasting
- Constitutional recommendation controls
- Community wellness analytics
- Multi-platform syndication
