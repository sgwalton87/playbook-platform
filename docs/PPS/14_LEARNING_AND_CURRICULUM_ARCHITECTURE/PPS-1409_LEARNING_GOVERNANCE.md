# Purpose

Establish constitutional governance for every learning experience, curriculum, facilitator, assessment, credential, and educational program delivered through the Playbook Platform.

---

# Constitutional Principles

- Educational excellence.
- Continuous improvement.
- Learner success.
- Academic integrity.
- Evidence-based governance.
- Inclusive learning.

---

# Governance Lifecycle

1. Curriculum Proposal
2. Educational Review
3. Approval
4. Delivery
5. Assessment
6. Certification
7. Evaluation
8. Improvement
9. Retirement

---

# Responsibilities

The governance framework shall:

- Approve curricula.
- Govern facilitators.
- Maintain educational quality.
- Preserve academic standards.
- Measure learner outcomes.
- Guide continuous improvement.

---

# Validation Rules

- Every curriculum is reviewed.
- Every assessment is measurable.
- Every credential is verifiable.
- Every governance decision is auditable.

---

# Definition of Done

Every educational experience is constitutionally governed throughout its complete lifecycle.
