# Purpose

Define the constitutional framework governing assessment, competency validation, and evidence of learning.

---

# Scope

Applies to quizzes, assignments, projects, demonstrations, certifications, peer reviews, mentor evaluations, and future assessment methods.

---

# Authority

Learners shall demonstrate competency through measurable evidence rather than course completion alone.

---

# Constitutional Principles

- Competency-based learning.
- Multiple forms of evidence.
- Authentic assessment.
- Transparent evaluation.
- Continuous feedback.

---

# Assessment Types

- Knowledge Checks
- Practical Exercises
- Projects
- Peer Review
- Mentor Evaluation
- Reflection
- Capstone Demonstrations

---

# Responsibilities

The assessment framework shall:

- Measure competency.
- Preserve evidence.
- Support remediation.
- Track improvement.
- Certify mastery.

---

# Validation Rules

- Preserve assessment history.
- Record evaluator feedback.
- Maintain scoring transparency.
- Support reassessment.

---

# Definition of Done

Every competency is supported by measurable evidence of mastery.
