# Purpose

Establish the constitutional framework governing certifications, credentials, badges, transcripts, and recognition throughout the Playbook Platform.

---

# Constitutional Principles

- Competency earns credentials.
- Evidence precedes recognition.
- Credentials are verifiable.
- Learning is portable.
- Achievement is permanent.

---

# Credential Types

- Certificates
- Digital Badges
- Competency Records
- Learning Transcripts
- Pathway Completion
- Professional Credentials

---

# Responsibilities

The certification architecture shall:

- Verify competency.
- Issue credentials.
- Preserve achievement history.
- Support credential verification.
- Maintain credential integrity.

---

# Validation Rules

- Verify competency evidence.
- Preserve credential lineage.
- Prevent duplicate issuance.
- Record credential lifecycle.

---

# Definition of Done

Every credential is evidence-based, verifiable, secure, and constitutionally governed.
