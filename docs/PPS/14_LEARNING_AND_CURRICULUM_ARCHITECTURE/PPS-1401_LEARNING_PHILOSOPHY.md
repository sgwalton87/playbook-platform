# Purpose

Define the constitutional educational philosophy guiding learning across the Playbook Platform.

---

# Constitutional Principles

- Learning through action.
- Reflection strengthens mastery.
- Progress over perfection.
- Personalized pathways.
- Community learning.
- Evidence of competency.

---

# Responsibilities

The learning philosophy shall guide curriculum, assessment, mentoring, coaching, and certification.

---

# Definition of Done

Every learning experience aligns with the constitutional educational philosophy.
