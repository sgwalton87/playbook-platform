# Purpose

Define the constitutional responsibilities and architecture governing instructors, mentors, coaches, advisors, counselors, and future learning facilitators.

---

# Scope

Applies to every individual or organization providing educational guidance through the Playbook Platform.

---

# Authority

Facilitators shall guide learner growth while preserving learner agency and constitutional educational standards.

---

# Constitutional Principles

- Coaching over directing.
- Empowerment over dependency.
- Constructive feedback.
- Accountability.
- Professional integrity.
- Learner-centered support.

---

# Responsibilities

Facilitators shall:

- Guide learners.
- Review progress.
- Provide feedback.
- Verify competencies.
- Encourage growth.
- Maintain professional standards.

---

# Validation Rules

- Preserve mentoring history.
- Record learner interactions.
- Maintain evaluation transparency.
- Support continuous facilitator development.

---

# Definition of Done

Every facilitator supports learner success through constitutional coaching and educational excellence.
