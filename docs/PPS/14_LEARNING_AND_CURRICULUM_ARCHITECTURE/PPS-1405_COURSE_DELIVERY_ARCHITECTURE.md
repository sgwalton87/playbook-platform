# Purpose

Establish the constitutional architecture governing course delivery across every Playbook learning environment.

---

# Scope

Applies to live instruction, self-paced learning, hybrid learning, cohort-based programs, workshops, mentorship sessions, and future educational experiences.

---

# Authority

Course delivery shall maximize learner engagement while maintaining educational quality and accessibility.

---

# Constitutional Principles

- Flexible delivery.
- Active participation.
- Accessible instruction.
- Continuous engagement.
- Measurable learning.
- Consistent quality.

---

# Responsibilities

The delivery architecture shall:

- Deliver content.
- Track participation.
- Preserve learning progress.
- Support instructors.
- Enable hybrid learning.

---

# Validation Rules

- Preserve completion history.
- Track learner participation.
- Validate accessibility.
- Record instructional improvements.

---

# Definition of Done

Every learning experience is delivered consistently, accessibly, and measurably.
