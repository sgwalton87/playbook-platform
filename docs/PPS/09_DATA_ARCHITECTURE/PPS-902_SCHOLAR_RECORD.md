# Purpose

The Canonical Scholar Record defines the single authoritative representation of an individual learner throughout the Playbook Platform.

Every operating system, intelligence engine, workflow, experience, and recommendation derives information from this record.

---

# Scope

Applies to every scholar regardless of age, institution, geography, operating system, or educational pathway.

---

# Authority

There shall be exactly one Canonical Scholar Record for each scholar.

No duplicate canonical scholar records shall exist.

---

# Definitions

## Scholar Record

The authoritative representation of a learner.

## Derived Profile

A presentation-specific representation generated from the Scholar Record.

---

# Constitutional Principles

- One scholar.
- One record.
- Many experiences.
- Canonical first.
- Derived second.
- Preserve lifelong history.

---

# Constitutional Domains

The Scholar Record includes:

- Identity
- Demographics
- Family
- Education
- Athletics
- Experiences
- Achievements
- Credentials
- Assessments
- Financial Literacy
- Career
- Mentorship
- Scholarships
- College
- Employment
- Entrepreneurship
- Recommendations
- Goals
- Documents
- Timeline
- Permissions

---

# Responsibilities

The Scholar Record shall:

- Serve as the canonical learner profile.
- Supply every operating system.
- Preserve historical truth.
- Support lifelong learning.

---

# Validation Rules

The Scholar Record shall:

- Prevent duplicate records.
- Preserve historical integrity.
- Record provenance.
- Maintain constitutional ownership.

---

# Compliance Requirements

Every platform service shall consume canonical scholar information from this specification.

---

# Implementation Guidance

Presentation layers shall never become the canonical source of scholar information.

---

# Definition of Done

Every scholar has one lifelong constitutional record capable of supporting every Playbook Operating System.

---

# Future Amendments

Future versions may support international education systems, lifelong employment history, digital identity credentials, and decentralized identity.
