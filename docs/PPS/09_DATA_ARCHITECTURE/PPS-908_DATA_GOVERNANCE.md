# Purpose

The Data Governance specification establishes the constitutional framework for stewardship, accountability, quality, compliance, and evolution of canonical data throughout the Playbook Platform.

---

# Scope

Applies to every constitutional data domain, canonical record, steward, policy, quality rule, and governance decision.

---

# Authority

Canonical data shall be governed according to constitutional policies rather than implementation-specific practices.

---

# Definitions

## Data Steward

The constitutional owner responsible for a canonical data domain.

## Governance Policy

A constitutional rule governing data ownership, quality, security, lifecycle, or compliance.

## Data Quality

The degree to which canonical data satisfies constitutional standards.

---

# Constitutional Principles

- Ownership is explicit.
- Governance is transparent.
- Quality is measurable.
- Compliance is continuous.
- Evolution preserves constitutional compatibility.

---

# Governance Domains

- Ownership
- Stewardship
- Quality
- Compliance
- Retention
- Security
- Privacy
- Metadata
- Standards

---

# Responsibilities

The governance framework shall:

- Assign stewardship.
- Define governance policies.
- Measure quality.
- Resolve ownership conflicts.
- Preserve constitutional integrity.

---

# Validation Rules

The governance framework shall:

- Reject undefined ownership.
- Require stewardship.
- Preserve governance history.
- Enforce constitutional policies.

---

# Compliance Requirements

Every canonical data domain shall have documented ownership, stewardship, quality expectations, and governance policies.

---

# Implementation Guidance

Governance processes should evolve through constitutional amendments rather than operational exceptions.

---

# Definition of Done

Every canonical data domain has clearly defined ownership, stewardship, governance policies, and measurable quality standards.

---

# Future Amendments

Future versions may support automated governance agents, enterprise policy inheritance, regulatory profiles, and AI-assisted data stewardship.
