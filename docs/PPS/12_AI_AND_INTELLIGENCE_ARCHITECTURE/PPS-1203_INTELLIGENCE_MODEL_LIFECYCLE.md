# Purpose

Define the constitutional lifecycle governing every intelligence model operating within the Playbook Platform.

---

# Scope

Applies to machine learning models, LLMs, recommendation engines, scoring models, predictive models, ranking algorithms, and future intelligence systems.

---

# Authority

Every intelligence model shall follow a governed lifecycle before serving production users.

---

# Lifecycle

1. Proposal
2. Design
3. Training
4. Validation
5. Certification
6. Deployment
7. Monitoring
8. Improvement
9. Retirement

---

# Constitutional Principles

- Models are replaceable.
- Validation precedes deployment.
- Performance is continuously monitored.
- Models remain explainable.
- Human governance is continuous.

---

# Responsibilities

The lifecycle shall:

- Track versions.
- Preserve lineage.
- Record evaluations.
- Support rollback.
- Maintain audit history.

---

# Validation Rules

- Reject uncertified models.
- Preserve model history.
- Require measurable evaluation.

---

# Definition of Done

Every intelligence model is versioned, certified, monitored, and governed throughout its lifecycle.
