# Purpose

Establish constitutional safeguards that promote fairness, reduce harmful bias, and ensure responsible intelligence throughout the Playbook Platform.

---

# Scope

Applies to every AI model, recommendation engine, predictive system, ranking algorithm, and future intelligence capability.

---

# Authority

Responsible AI is a constitutional requirement for every intelligence capability.

---

# Constitutional Principles

- Fairness by design.
- Transparency.
- Human dignity.
- Bias measurement.
- Continuous improvement.
- Inclusive outcomes.
- Human accountability.

---

# Responsibilities

Responsible AI governance shall:

- Measure bias.
- Monitor fairness.
- Detect unintended outcomes.
- Support remediation.
- Preserve evaluation history.

---

# Validation Rules

- Evaluate fairness before deployment.
- Monitor production behavior.
- Record mitigation activities.
- Preserve evaluation evidence.

---

# Compliance Requirements

Every production intelligence capability shall demonstrate responsible AI practices before certification.

---

# Definition of Done

Every intelligence capability is evaluated for fairness, transparency, accountability, and responsible operation.
