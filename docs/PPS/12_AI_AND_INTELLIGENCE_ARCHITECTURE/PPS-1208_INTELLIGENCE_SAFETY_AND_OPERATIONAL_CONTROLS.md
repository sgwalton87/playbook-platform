# Purpose

Establish constitutional operational safeguards that ensure intelligence systems remain safe, predictable, resilient, and aligned with Playbook governance.

---

# Scope

Applies to all production intelligence systems, assistants, recommendation engines, autonomous workflows, and future AI capabilities.

---

# Authority

Operational safeguards shall exist before any intelligence capability may operate in production.

---

# Constitutional Principles

- Safe by default.
- Human override.
- Fail safely.
- Controlled autonomy.
- Continuous monitoring.
- Operational resilience.

---

# Operational Controls

- Approval Gates
- Confidence Thresholds
- Human Escalation
- Kill Switches
- Rollback Procedures
- Runtime Monitoring
- Incident Logging

---

# Responsibilities

The operational framework shall:

- Monitor behavior.
- Prevent unsafe execution.
- Support emergency shutdown.
- Preserve operational history.
- Enable recovery.

---

# Validation Rules

- Verify safety controls.
- Test rollback procedures.
- Validate escalation paths.
- Preserve operational audit trails.

---

# Compliance Requirements

Every intelligence capability shall satisfy constitutional operational safety requirements before production deployment.

---

# Definition of Done

Every production intelligence capability operates within constitutional operational safety controls.

---

# Future Amendments

Future versions may support autonomous guardrails, adaptive operational controls, and self-healing runtime protections.
