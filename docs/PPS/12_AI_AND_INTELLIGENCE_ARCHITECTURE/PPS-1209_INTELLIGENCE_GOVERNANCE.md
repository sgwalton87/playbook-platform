# Purpose

Establish the constitutional governance framework overseeing every intelligence capability throughout the Playbook Platform.

---

# Scope

Applies to every intelligence engine, AI model, recommendation system, decision-support service, operational workflow, and future AI capability.

---

# Authority

Intelligence governance is the constitutional authority responsible for ensuring all AI capabilities remain aligned with Playbook's mission, governance, transparency, and human-first principles.

---

# Constitutional Principles

- Human agency is preserved.
- Canonical truth remains authoritative.
- Governance is transparent.
- Intelligence is accountable.
- Continuous improvement is required.
- Trust is earned.

---

# Governance Responsibilities

The governance framework shall:

- Approve intelligence capabilities.
- Define constitutional standards.
- Certify production readiness.
- Monitor operational performance.
- Review incidents.
- Govern model retirement.
- Direct continuous improvement.

---

# Governance Lifecycle

1. Proposal
2. Design Review
3. Validation
4. Certification
5. Deployment
6. Monitoring
7. Improvement
8. Retirement

---

# Validation Rules

- Every intelligence capability is versioned.
- Every deployment is certified.
- Every model is explainable.
- Every governance decision is auditable.
- Every retirement preserves historical lineage.

---

# Compliance Requirements

All intelligence systems shall remain constitutional, transparent, explainable, secure, measurable, and continuously governed.

---

# Implementation Guidance

Implementation technologies may evolve while constitutional governance principles remain unchanged.

---

# Definition of Done

Every intelligence capability is governed throughout its complete lifecycle by transparent constitutional oversight.

---

# Future Amendments

Future versions may introduce constitutional AI councils, autonomous governance agents, adaptive policy engines, multi-model orchestration governance, and international AI governance frameworks.
