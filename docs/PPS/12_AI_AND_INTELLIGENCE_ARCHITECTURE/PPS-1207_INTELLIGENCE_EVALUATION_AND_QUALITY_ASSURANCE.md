# Purpose

Define the constitutional framework for evaluating, validating, measuring, and continuously improving intelligence capabilities throughout the Playbook Platform.

---

# Scope

Applies to every recommendation engine, predictive model, AI assistant, ranking system, optimization engine, and future intelligence capability.

---

# Authority

Every intelligence capability shall be evaluated before certification and continuously monitored throughout production.

---

# Constitutional Principles

- Quality is measurable.
- Evaluation is repeatable.
- Performance is transparent.
- Continuous improvement.
- Human verification.

---

# Evaluation Categories

- Accuracy
- Reliability
- Explainability
- Fairness
- Robustness
- User Satisfaction
- Operational Performance

---

# Responsibilities

The evaluation framework shall:

- Define benchmarks.
- Execute evaluations.
- Track performance trends.
- Record quality metrics.
- Support continuous improvement.

---

# Validation Rules

- Reject uncertified intelligence.
- Preserve evaluation history.
- Measure production performance.
- Document quality regressions.

---

# Compliance Requirements

Every intelligence capability shall demonstrate measurable quality before production deployment.

---

# Definition of Done

Every intelligence capability is continuously evaluated, benchmarked, monitored, and improved.

---

# Future Amendments

Future versions may support autonomous evaluation agents, simulation environments, and continuous benchmarking.
