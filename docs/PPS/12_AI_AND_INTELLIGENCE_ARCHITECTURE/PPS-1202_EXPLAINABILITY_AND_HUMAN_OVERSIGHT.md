# Purpose

Govern constitutional explainability, transparency, accountability, and human oversight for every intelligence decision.

---

# Constitutional Principles

- Every recommendation is explainable.
- Inputs are traceable.
- Reasoning is reviewable.
- Human approval is supported.
- Human override is always available.
- AI remains accountable.

---

# Responsibilities

The explainability framework shall:

- Record inputs.
- Record outputs.
- Record confidence.
- Record reasoning.
- Support human review.
- Preserve audit history.

---

# Validation Rules

- Reject opaque recommendations.
- Preserve decision provenance.
- Record human overrides.
- Maintain explainability history.

---

# Compliance Requirements

Every production intelligence capability shall provide meaningful explanations for its outputs.

---

# Definition of Done

Every AI-generated recommendation is transparent, reviewable, explainable, and constitutionally governed.
