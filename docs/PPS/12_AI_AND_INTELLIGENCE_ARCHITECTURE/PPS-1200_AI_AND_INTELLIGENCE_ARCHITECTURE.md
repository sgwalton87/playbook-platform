# Purpose

The AI and Intelligence Architecture establishes the constitutional framework governing every intelligence capability within the Playbook Platform.

Intelligence augments human decision-making but never replaces constitutional truth, human agency, or governance.

---

# Scope

Applies to every recommendation engine, AI assistant, workflow engine, predictive model, scoring engine, optimization service, and future intelligence capability.

---

# Authority

All intelligence systems shall derive insight from constitutional data while preserving explainability, transparency, and human oversight.

---

# Constitutional Principles

- Human-first intelligence.
- Canonical data is authoritative.
- AI never creates constitutional truth.
- Explainability is mandatory.
- Human override always exists.
- Recommendations are distinguishable from facts.
- Every model is auditable.

---

# Architecture

The Intelligence Architecture consists of:

- Context Engine
- Recommendation Engines
- Predictive Models
- Planning Engines
- Decision Support
- Explainability Services
- Human Review
- Model Governance

---

# Responsibilities

The architecture shall:

- Produce recommendations.
- Preserve explainability.
- Support human decision-making.
- Protect constitutional truth.
- Maintain audit history.

---

# Definition of Done

Every intelligence capability operates within constitutional governance while preserving transparency and human control.
