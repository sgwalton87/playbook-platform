# Purpose

Define the constitutional principles governing every intelligence capability within Playbook.

---

# Constitutional Principles

- Assist rather than replace.
- Recommendations are never facts.
- Intelligence supports human judgment.
- Every recommendation is explainable.
- Confidence shall be communicated.
- Models remain replaceable.
- Bias shall be monitored.
- Intelligence continuously improves.

---

# Responsibilities

Every intelligence engine shall:

- Respect constitutional data ownership.
- Preserve transparency.
- Support explainability.
- Record provenance.
- Enable human override.

---

# Validation Rules

- Reject unexplained recommendations.
- Reject unsupported conclusions.
- Preserve recommendation history.

---

# Definition of Done

Every intelligence capability follows constitutional AI principles.
