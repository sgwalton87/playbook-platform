# Purpose

Define the constitutional framework for AI-assisted decision support throughout the Playbook Platform.

---

# Scope

Applies to planning, prioritization, opportunity selection, scheduling, workflows, coaching, mentoring, advising, and future decision-support systems.

---

# Authority

Decision support shall augment—not replace—human judgment.

---

# Constitutional Principles

- Humans decide.
- AI advises.
- Alternatives are presented.
- Tradeoffs are explained.
- Confidence is communicated.

---

# Responsibilities

Decision support shall:

- Present options.
- Explain tradeoffs.
- Estimate outcomes.
- Support planning.
- Preserve decision history.

---

# Validation Rules

- Preserve decision provenance.
- Reject unsupported conclusions.
- Record user decisions.

---

# Definition of Done

Every decision-support capability remains explainable, reviewable, and human-directed.
