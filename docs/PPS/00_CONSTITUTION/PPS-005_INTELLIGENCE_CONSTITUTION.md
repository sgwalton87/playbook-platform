Purpose

This document establishes the constitutional principles governing every intelligence capability within the Playbook Platform.

Intelligence exists to assist users by transforming information into actionable guidance while preserving human agency, transparency, and trust.

Definition

An Intelligence Engine is a platform capability that analyzes canonical data to produce recommendations, insights, predictions, prioritization, summaries, or decision support.

Intelligence shall never replace the canonical record.

It augments understanding.

Constitutional Principles

Principle 1

Human Agency

Humans make decisions.

Artificial intelligence provides guidance.

No intelligence engine shall remove meaningful user choice.

------------------------------------------------------------

Principle 2

Canonical Data

Intelligence engines consume canonical platform data.

They do not become the canonical source.

Generated recommendations shall never overwrite authoritative records without explicit user action.

------------------------------------------------------------

Principle 3

Explainability

Every recommendation shall be explainable.

Users should understand:

- Why the recommendation exists.
- Which information contributed.
- Confidence level.
- Recommended action.
- Expected outcome.

------------------------------------------------------------

Principle 4

Confidence

Every recommendation shall include an internal confidence score.

Confidence informs prioritization but does not determine truth.

------------------------------------------------------------

Principle 5

Transparency

Users shall know when information was:

- Generated
- Suggested
- Predicted
- Summarized
- Prioritized

Artificial intelligence shall never intentionally appear as human judgment.

------------------------------------------------------------

Principle 6

Privacy

Intelligence engines shall operate only on data users are authorized to access.

Recommendations shall never expose restricted information.

------------------------------------------------------------

Principle 7

Fairness

Recommendations shall strive to reduce unnecessary bias.

Platform intelligence should expand opportunity rather than reinforce existing inequities.

------------------------------------------------------------

Principle 8

Continuous Improvement

Engines may improve over time through:

- Better specifications
- Improved models
- User feedback
- Outcome measurement

Constitutional principles remain stable.

------------------------------------------------------------

Principle 9

Safety

Recommendations shall prioritize user wellbeing.

No engine shall intentionally encourage harmful behavior.

------------------------------------------------------------

Principle 10

Platform Alignment

Every intelligence engine shall support the Playbook mission.

Engines exist to help users discover opportunities, make informed decisions, and accomplish meaningful goals.

Constitutional Rules

Intelligence engines inherit platform governance.

Recommendations shall not become canonical facts.

Generated content shall remain distinguishable from user-provided information.

PBOS Responsibilities

PBOS shall:

- Validate intelligence inheritance.
- Verify explainability.
- Preserve canonical ownership.
- Prevent unauthorized data usage.
- Detect constitutional violations.
- Maintain traceability.

Definition of Done

Human agency preserved.

Canonical data protected.

Explainability required.

Confidence supported.

Transparency established.

Platform intelligence governed constitutionally.
