# Letter From The Founder

## The Story Behind The Playbook

## Stephisha Ycoy-Walton

Founder, Playbook Platform

---

To every scholar, athlete, educator, mentor, parent, community leader, builder, and future generation who encounters Playbook,

Before you understand the platform, I want you to understand the story behind it.

Playbook was not created because I wanted to build another application.

It was not created because the world needed another piece of technology.

Playbook was created because my entire life has been shaped by people who became my playbook before a system existed to help people navigate their own journeys.

This platform is an honor to those people.

It is my thank you.

It is my commitment.

It is the system I wish existed for every person trying to discover who they are, where they are going, and how to get there.

---

# Where It Started

I was born in Vallejo, California, to my parents:

**Stephen Walton** and **Cyntheny LaShawn Jones-Walton.**

I was the oldest of two children, with a seven-year age gap between my younger sibling and me.

Growing up in Vallejo shaped my understanding of community, resilience, opportunity, and the importance of having people who believe in you.

I attended schools throughout the Vallejo school district.

I was placed in the GATE program, but that opportunity did not happen because the system automatically recognized my potential.

It happened because my mother advocated for me.

My mother saw something in me before many systems knew what to do with me.

---

# The Student Who Needed Someone To See Her

I was the student who finished assignments early and started talking.

I was curious.

I was energetic.

I wanted to engage.

But because I finished my work and began interacting with others, I was often labeled as the troublemaker.

I was viewed as a distraction.

The reality was that I was a young person with intelligence, energy, and potential who needed guidance and challenge.

That experience has stayed with me.

Because I know there are countless students today who are misunderstood.

Students who are brilliant.

Students who are capable.

Students who simply need someone to recognize their gifts and help guide them.

---

# My First Playbook Was My Mother

My mother was my first mentor.

She was my:

- Advocate
- Academic coach
- Accountability partner
- Support system
- Believer

She fought for opportunities for me.

She pushed me.

She challenged me.

She reminded me of who I was when I could not yet see it myself.

Before Playbook existed, my mother was my Playbook.

---

# Basketball Opened Doors

Basketball became the vehicle that helped me discover my potential.

I was recruited to attend **St. Patrick-St. Vincent High School (SPSV)** in Vallejo, California.

I am forever grateful to the **Faucett family**, especially **Grandpa Faucett** and **Dr. Faucett**, for making that opportunity possible and for mentoring me during that chapter of my life.

I also played club basketball with **Bay Area Magic under Rae Jackson**.

Those relationships changed my trajectory.

The combination of:

- My mother's advocacy
- The Faucett family's mentorship
- Rae Jackson's coaching
- The opportunities created through basketball

helped create the foundation that brought me here.

---

# The Journey Through College Basketball

Through basketball, I was recruited by more than 75 universities, including Ivy League institutions such as:

- Princeton
- Columbia
- Dartmouth
- Cornell

I ultimately chose Oregon State University because I wanted to compete at the highest level and continue the journey alongside friends who were also attending Pac-10 programs.

But life changed.

Before I even arrived, the coaching staff that recruited me was gone.

The plan changed.

The path changed.

And once again, I had to adapt.

I transferred to Portland State University, where **Coach Charity Elliott and Chris Elliott** supported me and helped me continue developing.

When their journey took them to new opportunities in San Diego, my path changed again.

I transitioned back to California and finished my final two years at **Cal Poly Pomona**, earning my Bachelor of Science in Kinesiology.

---

# Learning To Finish What I Started

Before completing my degree, I left to pursue professional basketball overseas.

That experience changed my worldview.

Playing professionally taught me independence, discipline, resilience, and the reality that every athlete eventually faces transition.

But before I finished my academic journey, my mentors and my mother reminded me:

Finish what you started.

I returned and completed my degree.

Because education was always part of the mission.

---

# The Generational Foundation

My story is also a story about education and generational progress.

My grandparents did not have full formal educations.

They worked hard.

They sacrificed.

They built the foundation that allowed the next generation to go further.

My mother, **Cyntheny Jones-Walton**, became a first-generation college graduate.

She changed the trajectory of our family.

In 2012, my mother and I graduated college two years apart.

That moment represented more than degrees.

It represented generations of progress.

I became the first person in my family to earn a master's degree.

I earned my MBA from the **University of Arizona Forbes School of Business**.

Every academic milestone represents the sacrifices and dreams of those who came before me.

---

# The Medical School Moment

After basketball, I returned home.

I was accepted into medical school.

It was a dream becoming reality.

But then I encountered a reality that many talented people face:

Opportunity requires resources.

I did not have the four Cs:

- Cash
- Capital
- Collateral
- Credit

I deferred my acceptance for a year while trying to figure out how to make it possible.

But I could not bridge that gap.

That moment changed the direction of my life.

---

# Becoming A Financial Advisor

That is how I entered financial services.

I realized that financial literacy was not just about money.

It was about access.

It was about understanding systems.

It was about knowing the rules of the game.

I saw that many people were not lacking intelligence, ambition, or work ethic.

They were lacking information.

They were lacking guidance.

They were lacking someone who could help them navigate.

---

# The Person Who Helped Build This Vision

In 2005, I met **Vivica E. Coy Walton**.

From that point forward, she became more than a supporter.

She became a:

- Thought partner
- Collaborative partner
- Implementation partner
- Vision partner

Every step of this journey, Vivica has helped me think bigger, build better, and execute the vision.

Building something meaningful requires people who can see the future with you.

Vivica has been one of those people.

---

# Why Playbook Exists

Playbook exists because I know what it feels like to have potential but need guidance.

I know what it feels like to have dreams but need someone to help navigate the path.

I know what it feels like when one person opening a door can change everything.

So now, I am building the system.

A system that connects:

- Education
- Athletics
- Mentorship
- Career development
- Financial empowerment
- Community
- Opportunity

---

# Building For The Next Generation

Playbook is not only being built for today's scholars.

It is being built for tomorrow's generation.

It is being built for my son, **Karter Ycoy-Walton**.

It is being built for every child who deserves a stronger starting point than the generations before them had.

The goal is not simply to create success for one person.

The goal is to create systems that make success more accessible for generations.

---

# The Future We Are Building

I believe every person deserves access to a playbook.

Not everyone will have the same journey.

Not everyone will choose the same path.

But everyone deserves:

Someone who believes in them.

Someone who helps them navigate.

Someone who connects them to opportunity.

Someone who helps them see what is possible.

---

# Final Words

My life is proof that people change lives.

My mother changed mine.

My father, **Stephen Walton**, helped shape mine.

My family changed mine.

The Faucett family changed mine.

Rae Jackson changed mine.

Coach Charity Elliott and Chris Elliott changed mine.

Vivica E. Coy Walton has helped build mine.

Every mentor, coach, educator, and advocate who invested in me became part of this story.

Playbook is my way of honoring them.

It is my commitment to future generations.

It is the system I wish every scholar, athlete, family, and future leader had from the beginning.

Because talent is everywhere.

Potential is everywhere.

Dreams are everywhere.

Now the playbook should be everywhere too.

With gratitude,

**Stephisha Ycoy-Walton**

Founder  
Playbook Platform
