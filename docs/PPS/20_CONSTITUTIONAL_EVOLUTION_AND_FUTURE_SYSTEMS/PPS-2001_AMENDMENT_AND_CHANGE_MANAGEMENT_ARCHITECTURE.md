# Purpose

Establish the constitutional process for proposing, reviewing, approving, documenting, and implementing changes to the Playbook Platform Specification.

---

# Authority

The Constitution may evolve, but changes must preserve the foundational mission and principles of Playbook.

---

# Constitutional Principles

## Changes Require Intent

Every amendment must clearly explain:

- Why change is needed.
- What problem is solved.
- What impact is expected.

---

## Documentation Creates Trust

All significant changes must be recorded.

---

## Review Protects Integrity

Major changes require appropriate evaluation.

---

## Improvement Requires Learning

Feedback and evidence should inform evolution.

---

# Amendment Lifecycle

## 1. Proposal

Document:

- Requested change
- Motivation
- Expected impact

---

## 2. Review

Evaluate:

- Alignment
- Risk
- Dependencies
- User impact

---

## 3. Approval

Determine:

- Acceptance
- Modification
- Rejection

---

## 4. Implementation

Update:

- Documentation
- Systems
- Processes
- Governance records

---

## 5. Validation

Confirm:

- Correct implementation
- Constitutional alignment
- No unintended harm

---

# Change Categories

## Minor Changes

Examples:

- Clarifications
- Documentation improvements
- Formatting updates

---

## Major Changes

Examples:

- New constitutional principles
- New platform domains
- Governance changes
- Architectural changes

---

# Responsibilities

Change management shall:

- Preserve history.
- Maintain transparency.
- Protect standards.
- Coordinate implementation.

---

# Validation Rules

All amendments shall:

- Include rationale.
- Identify affected systems.
- Document approvals.
- Preserve previous versions.

---

# Definition of Done

Every constitutional change follows a transparent, documented, and accountable process.
