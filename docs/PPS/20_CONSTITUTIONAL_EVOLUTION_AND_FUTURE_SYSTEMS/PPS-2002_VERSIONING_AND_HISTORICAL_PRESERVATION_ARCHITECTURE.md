# Purpose

Establish the constitutional architecture governing documentation history, version management, preservation, and continuity throughout the Playbook Platform.

---

# Authority

Every major evolution of Playbook must preserve institutional memory.

Past decisions provide context for future development.

---

# Constitutional Principles

## History Is Infrastructure

Documentation is not administrative overhead.

It is a core operating system capability.

---

## Changes Must Be Traceable

Future teams must understand:

- What changed.
- Why it changed.
- Who approved it.
- What impact occurred.

---

## Knowledge Must Survive Individuals

The platform must not depend on any single person maintaining context.

---

# Versioning Architecture

Includes:

- Document versions
- Release records
- Change logs
- Decision records
- Historical archives

---

# Preservation Requirements

Systems shall preserve:

- Previous specifications
- Governance decisions
- Architectural changes
- Lessons learned

---

# Responsibilities

Version governance shall:

- Maintain accuracy.
- Preserve history.
- Support audits.
- Enable future understanding.

---

# Validation Rules

Versioning systems shall:

- Maintain unique identifiers.
- Preserve previous states.
- Document changes.
- Prevent accidental history loss.

---

# Definition of Done

Versioning Architecture is complete when every major evolution of Playbook can be understood through preserved historical records.

---

# Future Amendments

Future versions may include:

- Automated historical intelligence
- Decision archaeology systems
- AI documentation preservation
