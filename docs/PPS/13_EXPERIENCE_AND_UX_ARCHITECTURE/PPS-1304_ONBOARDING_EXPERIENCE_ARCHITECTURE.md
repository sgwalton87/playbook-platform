# Purpose

Establish the constitutional architecture governing onboarding for every role within the Playbook Platform.

---

# Scope

Applies to scholars, scholar-athletes, parents, mentors, coaches, counselors, educators, administrators, enterprise users, and future platform roles.

---

# Authority

Every onboarding experience shall collect only the information necessary to establish a successful personalized journey.

---

# Constitutional Principles

- Welcome before configuration.
- Progressive onboarding.
- Role-aware experiences.
- Save progress continuously.
- Never lose user work.
- Explain why information is requested.

---

# Onboarding Lifecycle

1. Welcome
2. Identity
3. Role Selection
4. Personalization
5. Verification
6. Configuration
7. Success

---

# Responsibilities

The onboarding architecture shall:

- Reduce abandonment.
- Personalize experiences.
- Support every role.
- Preserve data integrity.
- Prepare the Canonical Scholar Record.

---

# Validation Rules

- Preserve progress.
- Support resume capability.
- Prevent incomplete required submissions.
- Validate role requirements.

---

# Definition of Done

Every new user successfully reaches their personalized Playbook experience.
