# Purpose

Establish constitutional standards governing notifications, messaging, reminders, alerts, announcements, and communications throughout the Playbook Platform.

---

# Scope

Applies to email, SMS, push notifications, in-app messages, reminders, announcements, AI recommendations, calendar reminders, and future communication channels.

---

# Constitutional Principles

- Timely.
- Relevant.
- Actionable.
- Respectful.
- Configurable.
- Accessible.
- Never spam.

---

# Responsibilities

The communication architecture shall:

- Deliver meaningful notifications.
- Support user preferences.
- Prevent notification fatigue.
- Maintain delivery history.
- Support cross-platform delivery.

---

# Validation Rules

- Respect notification preferences.
- Prevent duplicate delivery.
- Preserve delivery history.
- Support accessibility.

---

# Definition of Done

Every communication is relevant, understandable, accessible, and constitutionally governed.
