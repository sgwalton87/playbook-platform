# Purpose

Establish constitutional accessibility and inclusive design standards ensuring the Playbook Platform is usable by every learner regardless of ability, language, device, or background.

---

# Scope

Applies to every interface, interaction, workflow, document, notification, media asset, and future platform capability.

---

# Authority

Accessibility is a constitutional requirement and shall never be treated as an optional enhancement.

---

# Constitutional Principles

- Accessibility by default.
- Inclusive design.
- Universal usability.
- Multiple interaction methods.
- Clear communication.
- Respect for diverse learners.

---

# Responsibilities

The accessibility framework shall:

- Support assistive technologies.
- Ensure keyboard accessibility.
- Provide sufficient contrast.
- Support captions and transcripts.
- Validate accessible navigation.
- Promote inclusive language.

---

# Validation Rules

- Verify accessibility compliance.
- Prevent inaccessible workflows.
- Validate screen-reader compatibility.
- Preserve accessibility testing history.

---

# Definition of Done

Every platform experience is inclusive, accessible, and constitutionally compliant.
