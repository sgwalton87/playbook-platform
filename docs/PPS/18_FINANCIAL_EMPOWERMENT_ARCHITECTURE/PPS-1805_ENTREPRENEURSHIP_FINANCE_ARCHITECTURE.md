# Purpose

Establish the constitutional architecture governing entrepreneurship finance education, business ownership readiness, startup financial capability, and economic empowerment throughout the Playbook Platform.

---

# Scope

Applies to:

- Student entrepreneurs
- Founders
- Small business owners
- Social enterprises
- Startup programs
- Entrepreneurship courses
- Business mentors

---

# Authority

Entrepreneurship finance systems shall help individuals understand the financial responsibilities of building organizations while encouraging responsible innovation.

---

# Constitutional Principles

## Ideas Require Structure

Entrepreneurship requires more than creativity.

It requires:

- Planning
- Financial understanding
- Execution
- Measurement

---

## Ownership Requires Responsibility

Business ownership includes accountability to customers, employees, communities, and stakeholders.

---

## Learning Through Building

Entrepreneurship education should include practical application.

---

## Sustainable Growth

Healthy businesses prioritize sustainability over unrealistic expansion.

---

# Entrepreneurship Finance Domains

Includes:

- Business models
- Revenue
- Expenses
- Pricing
- Cash flow
- Funding
- Financial statements
- Budgeting
- Business planning

---

# Responsibilities

The entrepreneurship finance system shall:

- Teach business fundamentals.
- Support founders.
- Connect resources.
- Encourage responsible planning.
- Track entrepreneurial growth.

---

# Validation Rules

Systems shall:

- Preserve founder ownership.
- Avoid guaranteeing business success.
- Provide educational context.
- Maintain accurate financial information.

---

# Compliance Requirements

Integrates with:

- Opportunity Architecture
- Learning Architecture
- Community Architecture
- Financial Empowerment Architecture

---

# Implementation Guidance

Entrepreneurship systems should empower creators from idea development through sustainable operation.

---

# Definition of Done

Entrepreneurs have access to financial knowledge, tools, relationships, and resources necessary to make informed business decisions.
