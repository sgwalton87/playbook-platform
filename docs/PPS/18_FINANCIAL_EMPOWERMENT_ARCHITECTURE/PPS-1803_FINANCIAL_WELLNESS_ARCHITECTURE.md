# Purpose

Establish the constitutional architecture governing financial wellness, financial stability, healthy financial behaviors, and lifelong financial resilience throughout the Playbook Platform.

Financial wellness represents the ability to understand, manage, and improve one's financial life while pursuing personal, educational, professional, and community goals.

---

# Scope

Applies to:

- Scholars
- Families
- College students
- Athletes
- Professionals
- Entrepreneurs
- Alumni
- Financial mentors
- Community partners

---

# Authority

Financial wellness systems shall support informed financial habits while recognizing that every person's financial circumstances are unique.

The platform shall provide education and resources without judgment.

---

# Constitutional Principles

## Financial Wellness Is Holistic

Financial health includes:

- Knowledge
- Habits
- Stability
- Confidence
- Planning
- Access to resources

---

## Progress Over Perfection

Financial growth occurs through continuous improvement.

---

## Prevention Creates Security

Early education and planning reduce future challenges.

---

## Financial Confidence Matters

Understanding creates empowerment.

---

# Financial Wellness Domains

Includes:

## Cash Flow Management

Supporting:

- Income awareness
- Spending awareness
- Budgeting
- Planning

---

## Emergency Preparedness

Supporting:

- Emergency savings
- Risk awareness
- Financial resilience

---

## Credit Health

Supporting:

- Credit education
- Responsible borrowing
- Credit management

---

## Financial Decision-Making

Supporting:

- Comparing options
- Understanding tradeoffs
- Long-term thinking

---

# Responsibilities

The financial wellness system shall:

- Provide education.
- Identify learning needs.
- Recommend resources.
- Encourage healthy habits.
- Track financial learning progress.

---

# Validation Rules

Systems shall:

- Protect financial privacy.
- Avoid financial judgment.
- Maintain educational accuracy.
- Explain recommendations.

---

# Compliance Requirements

Financial wellness integrates with:

- Financial Literacy
- Learning Architecture
- Journey Architecture
- Opportunity Architecture

---

# Implementation Guidance

Financial wellness experiences should meet users where they are and provide realistic next steps.

---

# Definition of Done

Every member has access to financial wellness education and resources that support informed, confident financial decision-making.
