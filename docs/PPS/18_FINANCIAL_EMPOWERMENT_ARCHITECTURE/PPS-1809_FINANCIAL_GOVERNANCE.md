# Purpose

Establish the constitutional governance framework overseeing financial education, financial resources, financial partnerships, and financial empowerment systems throughout the Playbook Platform.

---

# Scope

Applies to:

- Financial education
- Financial resources
- Financial partners
- Advisors
- Mentors
- Financial tools
- Future financial systems

---

# Authority

Financial governance exists to protect users, maintain educational integrity, and ensure financial systems operate according to constitutional values.

---

# Constitutional Principles

## Trust Above Transactions

Financial empowerment shall prioritize user benefit over commercial incentives.

---

## Transparency

Users should understand:

- Information sources
- Relationships
- Recommendations
- Limitations

---

## Protection

Financial systems must protect users from:

- Fraud
- Misleading claims
- Exploitation
- Conflicts of interest

---

## Human Oversight

Important financial decisions require appropriate human judgment.

---

# Governance Responsibilities

Financial Governance shall:

- Establish standards.
- Review partnerships.
- Monitor resources.
- Protect users.
- Maintain compliance.
- Evaluate outcomes.

---

# Governance Lifecycle

1. Proposal
2. Review
3. Verification
4. Approval
5. Implementation
6. Monitoring
7. Renewal or Retirement

---

# Validation Rules

Financial governance systems shall:

- Maintain audit history.
- Document decisions.
- Preserve transparency.
- Monitor partner compliance.

---

# Compliance Requirements

Integrates with:

- Security Governance
- Community Governance
- Opportunity Governance
- Constitutional Governance

---

# Implementation Guidance

Financial empowerment must remain centered on education and user agency.

Technology may support decisions but shall never replace appropriate professional judgment.

---

# Definition of Done

Financial Governance is complete when financial systems operate transparently, ethically, and in alignment with Playbook's mission of empowerment.

---

# Future Amendments

Future versions may include:

- Global financial education standards
- Financial ethics councils
- Community financial governance
- Advanced compliance systems
