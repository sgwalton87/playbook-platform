# Purpose

Define the constitutional principles governing financial education, decision-making, resource access, and wealth-building guidance throughout the Playbook Platform.

---

# Authority

Financial empowerment exists to increase confidence, knowledge, and opportunity while respecting individual circumstances and choices.

---

# Constitutional Principles

## Knowledge Creates Agency

People make stronger decisions when they understand their options.

---

## Financial Education Should Begin Early

Financial capability should develop throughout childhood, adolescence, adulthood, and beyond.

---

## Small Decisions Create Large Outcomes

Financial habits compound over time.

---

## Ownership Matters

Building assets and understanding ownership creates long-term opportunity.

---

## Everyone Deserves Access

Financial knowledge should not be limited by background or resources.

---

# Financial Values

Every financial experience should encourage:

- Responsibility
- Confidence
- Patience
- Understanding
- Planning
- Growth

---

# Responsibilities

Financial systems shall:

- Teach foundational concepts.
- Encourage informed decisions.
- Connect users with resources.
- Promote responsible behavior.
- Support lifelong learning.

---

# Validation Rules

Financial experiences shall:

- Avoid unrealistic promises.
- Provide appropriate context.
- Explain risks.
- Maintain educational integrity.

---

# Compliance Requirements

All financial education experiences shall align with:

- Learning Governance
- Security Governance
- Opportunity Governance

---

# Definition of Done

Financial principles are implemented when users can confidently understand and navigate financial decisions.

---

# Future Amendments

Future revisions may include:

- Global financial literacy standards
- Family wealth education systems
- Community financial empowerment models
