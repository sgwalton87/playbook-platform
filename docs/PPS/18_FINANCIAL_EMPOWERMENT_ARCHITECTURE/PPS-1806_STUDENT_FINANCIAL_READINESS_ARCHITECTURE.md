# Purpose

Establish the constitutional architecture governing financial preparation for students transitioning through education, career, and independence pathways.

The Student Financial Readiness Architecture ensures learners understand the financial decisions connected to education, career development, and personal independence.

---

# Scope

Applies to:

- High school students
- College students
- Scholar-athletes
- First-generation students
- Families
- Adult learners
- Career transition participants

---

# Authority

Financial readiness is a foundational component of educational success.

Students should have access to financial knowledge before making significant educational and career decisions.

---

# Constitutional Principles

## Financial Decisions Shape Pathways

Education and career decisions often have financial implications.

---

## Preparation Reduces Barriers

Understanding costs, resources, and options improves decision-making.

---

## Students Need Context

Financial choices should be evaluated alongside:

- Goals
- Career pathways
- Personal circumstances
- Available resources

---

## Families Are Partners

Support networks should have access to appropriate education and resources.

---

# Financial Readiness Domains

Includes:

- College costs
- Financial aid awareness
- Budgeting
- Banking
- Credit education
- Student employment
- Career earnings
- Debt awareness
- Financial planning

---

# Responsibilities

The student financial readiness system shall:

- Provide education.
- Explain financial concepts.
- Connect resources.
- Support planning.
- Encourage informed choices.

---

# Validation Rules

Systems shall:

- Provide accurate information.
- Protect personal financial data.
- Maintain educational standards.
- Avoid making financial decisions for users.

---

# Compliance Requirements

Integrates with:

- College Transition Architecture
- Financial Literacy
- Opportunity Architecture
- Learning Architecture

---

# Implementation Guidance

Student financial readiness should begin before major financial decisions occur and continue throughout educational transitions.

---

# Definition of Done

Students have the knowledge and resources needed to make informed financial decisions throughout their educational journey.

---

# Future Amendments

Future versions may include:

- Personalized financial readiness scoring
- Financial simulations
- Family financial education pathways
- Institutional financial integrations
