# Purpose

Establish the constitutional architecture governing trusted financial mentors, educators, advisors, and professional support networks throughout the Playbook Platform.

---

# Scope

Applies to:

- Financial educators
- Mentors
- Advisors
- Coaches
- Community leaders
- Licensed professionals
- Institutional partners

---

# Authority

Financial guidance relationships must prioritize trust, education, transparency, and appropriate professional boundaries.

---

# Constitutional Principles

## Relationships Matter

Financial empowerment improves when people have access to trusted guidance.

---

## Education and Advice Are Different

The platform shall distinguish:

- Financial education
- General information
- Professional financial advice

---

## Trust Must Be Earned

Mentors and advisors should demonstrate:

- Expertise
- Integrity
- Transparency
- Accountability

---

## User Choice Remains Primary

Users choose their trusted support relationships.

---

# Advisor Ecosystem Components

Includes:

- Mentor profiles
- Professional verification
- Matching systems
- Educational resources
- Appointment pathways
- Relationship tracking

---

# Responsibilities

The advisor ecosystem shall:

- Connect users with support.
- Maintain professional standards.
- Preserve relationship history.
- Support financial learning.
- Encourage informed decisions.

---

# Validation Rules

Systems shall:

- Verify credentials where applicable.
- Maintain transparency.
- Protect user information.
- Record appropriate relationship activity.

---

# Compliance Requirements

Integrates with:

- Identity Architecture
- Community Architecture
- Security Architecture
- Financial Governance

---

# Implementation Guidance

Advisor systems should strengthen human relationships rather than replace professional judgment with automation.

---

# Definition of Done

Users can access trusted financial education and support relationships within a safe, transparent ecosystem.

---

# Future Amendments

Future versions may include:

- National advisor networks
- Financial mentorship communities
- Professional education pathways
- AI-assisted advisor matching
