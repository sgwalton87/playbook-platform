Purpose

The Athlete Abroad Operating System supports scholar-athletes and professional athletes pursuing educational, athletic, and professional opportunities outside of their home country.

Objectives

The Athlete Abroad Operating System shall enable users to:

- Prepare for international competition.
- Manage overseas contracts.
- Coordinate travel requirements.
- Navigate immigration processes.
- Build financial readiness.
- Prepare for cultural transition.
- Maintain long-term career planning.

Canonical Principles

International experiences supplement the Scholar Record but never replace it.

Athlete Abroad shall coordinate with the Scholar-Athlete Operating System.

Target Users

Primary

- International Scholar-Athletes
- Professional Athletes Abroad

Activation Criteria

Activated when:

- Athlete accepts an overseas opportunity.
- Athlete registers for international competition.
- Organization assigns international status.

Primary Workflows

Permissions

Organizations manage only approved international programs.
