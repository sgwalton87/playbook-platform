Purpose

The Scholarship Journey Operating System provides the canonical operating environment for discovering, preparing for, applying to, earning, managing, renewing, and reporting scholarship opportunities throughout a learner's educational journey.

Rather than functioning as a scholarship search tool, the Scholarship Journey Operating System continuously coordinates every activity, recommendation, workflow, deadline, document, mentor interaction, financial aid milestone, and award associated with scholarship attainment.

Mission

Reduce financial barriers to education by intelligently guiding every learner toward scholarship opportunities aligned with their academic profile, leadership experiences, career aspirations, financial need, talents, identity, and life journey.

Vision

Every qualified learner should know:

- Which scholarships they qualify for.
- Why they qualify.
- What they must improve.
- What actions should be completed next.
- How much scholarship funding remains available.
- How close they are to achieving their educational funding goals.

Objectives

The Scholarship Journey Operating System shall:

- Build a complete scholarship readiness profile.
- Continuously evaluate scholarship eligibility.
- Match learners with relevant opportunities.
- Coordinate scholarship preparation.
- Manage application workflows.
- Track deadlines.
- Coordinate recommendation letters.
- Generate application insights.
- Track awards.
- Support scholarship renewals.
- Measure lifetime scholarship attainment.

Canonical Principles

Scholar Record First

Scholarship eligibility shall always reference the canonical Scholar Record.

------------------------------------------------------------

Opportunity Matching

Recommendations shall be generated from verified learner data rather than manual searching whenever possible.

------------------------------------------------------------

Continuous Readiness

Scholarship readiness shall be evaluated continuously rather than only during application periods.

------------------------------------------------------------

Learner Ownership

Learners retain ownership of all submitted application materials.

------------------------------------------------------------

One Journey

Every scholarship application belongs to one lifelong Scholarship Journey.
