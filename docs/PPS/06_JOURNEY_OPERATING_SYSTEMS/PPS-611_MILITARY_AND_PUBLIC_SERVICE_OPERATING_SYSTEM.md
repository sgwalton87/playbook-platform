Purpose

The Military and Public Service Operating System provides the canonical operating environment for learners pursuing careers dedicated to military service, public safety, emergency response, government, nonprofit leadership, civic engagement, education, public health, international service, and community impact.

The Military and Public Service Operating System coordinates exploration, eligibility, preparation, leadership development, service pathways, benefits planning, professional advancement, and lifelong civic engagement.

Mission

Prepare every learner who feels called to serve by providing the knowledge, mentorship, structure, and opportunities necessary to build an ethical, impactful, and sustainable career in service to others.

Vision

Public service is one of society's highest callings.

Every learner should understand:

- The many pathways into service.
- The qualifications required.
- The responsibilities they will assume.
- The leadership expected of them.
- The long-term opportunities available after service.

Objectives

The Military and Public Service Operating System shall:

- Introduce service pathways.
- Coordinate career exploration.
- Track eligibility requirements.
- Support commissioning and application processes.
- Develop leadership capacity.
- Connect learners with mentors.
- Prepare learners for service benefits.
- Promote civic engagement.
- Encourage ethical decision-making.
- Support lifelong public leadership.

Canonical Principles

Service Before Self

Leadership begins with serving others responsibly and ethically.

------------------------------------------------------------

Integrity Above All

Integrity, accountability, and professionalism shall guide every recommendation and workflow.

------------------------------------------------------------

Leadership Through Action

Leadership is demonstrated through consistent action, responsibility, and service.

------------------------------------------------------------

Preparation Creates Confidence

Learners shall be prepared before entering demanding service environments.

------------------------------------------------------------

Lifelong Civic Engagement

Public service extends beyond a single career and includes ongoing contributions to communities and society.

------------------------------------------------------------

Learner Ownership

Learners retain ownership of their career decisions, service history, and professional records.
