Purpose

The Graduate School Operating System provides the canonical operating environment for learners pursuing advanced academic, professional, and research degrees. It coordinates graduate school exploration, admissions, testing, funding, research preparation, faculty engagement, application management, enrollment, and long-term academic success.

Mission

Prepare every learner to successfully pursue graduate and professional education through personalized planning, evidence-based guidance, and coordinated institutional support.

Vision

Graduate education should never feel mysterious or inaccessible.

Every qualified learner should understand:

- Which graduate pathways align with their goals.
- How competitive they are.
- What experiences strengthen admission.
- How to finance advanced education.
- How to thrive after acceptance.

Objectives

The Graduate School Operating System shall:

- Explore graduate pathways.
- Assess admissions readiness.
- Coordinate prerequisite planning.
- Track entrance examinations.
- Manage faculty engagement.
- Coordinate recommendation letters.
- Build graduate portfolios.
- Track funding opportunities.
- Manage admissions workflows.
- Support enrollment and transition.

Canonical Principles

Preparation Before Application

Preparation begins years before submitting an application.

------------------------------------------------------------

Evidence-Based Readiness

Admissions recommendations shall rely upon verified learner achievements.

------------------------------------------------------------

Research Matters

Research, scholarship, leadership, and service shall be considered alongside academic performance.

------------------------------------------------------------

One Academic Journey

Graduate education extends the lifelong learning journey already represented within the Scholar Record.

------------------------------------------------------------

Learner Ownership

Learners retain ownership of all application materials, research portfolios, and academic plans.
