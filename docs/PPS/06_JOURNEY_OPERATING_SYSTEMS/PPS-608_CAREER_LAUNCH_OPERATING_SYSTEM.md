Purpose

The Career Launch Operating System provides the canonical operating environment for preparing learners to successfully transition from education into internships, apprenticeships, military service, entrepreneurship, graduate education, public service, and meaningful careers.

The Career Launch Operating System coordinates career exploration, workforce readiness, experiential learning, professional networking, job placement, career planning, and long-term professional development throughout a learner's career journey.

Mission

Empower every learner to confidently discover, prepare for, obtain, and thrive within meaningful career opportunities while continuously developing professionally throughout life.

Vision

Every learner should graduate with more than a diploma or degree.

Every learner should graduate with:

- Career clarity.
- Professional experience.
- A competitive resume.
- A strong professional network.
- Financial confidence.
- Leadership experience.
- The skills necessary to thrive throughout an evolving workforce.

Objectives

The Career Launch Operating System shall:

- Help learners discover career pathways.
- Connect education to workforce opportunities.
- Coordinate internships and apprenticeships.
- Build professional resumes.
- Develop interview readiness.
- Facilitate employer connections.
- Strengthen networking.
- Track career milestones.
- Support employment transitions.
- Promote lifelong career development.

Canonical Principles

Career Readiness Begins Early

Career exploration shall begin well before graduation.

------------------------------------------------------------

Experience Matters

Experiential learning shall be valued alongside academic achievement.

------------------------------------------------------------

Career is a Journey

Career development continues throughout a learner's lifetime.

------------------------------------------------------------

Evidence Over Assumptions

Career recommendations shall utilize verified Scholar Record data rather than generalized assumptions.

------------------------------------------------------------

Learner Ownership

Learners own their professional portfolio, achievements, and career planning decisions.

The Career Launch Operating System activates when:

- A learner selects career planning.

- A learner registers for an internship.
- A learner creates a professional resume.
- An organization enrolls a learner in workforce programming.
