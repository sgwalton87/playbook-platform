Purpose

This document establishes the constitutional standards governing analytics, observability, operational monitoring, and platform measurement throughout the Playbook Platform.

Every platform capability shall be measurable, observable, and auditable throughout its lifecycle.

Objectives

Platform observability shall ensure:

- Operational visibility
- Performance monitoring
- Reliability measurement
- User experience insights
- Platform health
- Continuous improvement
- Evidence-based decision making

Observability Principles

Measure by Design

Every major platform capability shall define measurable outcomes before implementation.

Success metrics shall be specified alongside functional requirements.

------------------------------------------------------------

Operational Visibility

Platform operators shall be able to determine the health of systems through observable signals.

Core observability signals include:

- Logs
- Metrics
- Traces
- Health checks

------------------------------------------------------------

Business Analytics

The platform shall support measurement of meaningful outcomes, including:

- User engagement
- Goal completion
- Experience progression
- Course completion
- Badge attainment
- Scholarship outcomes
- Career outcomes
- Platform adoption

Future specifications may define additional business metrics.

------------------------------------------------------------

Performance Monitoring

Platform performance shall be continuously measurable.

Recommended measurements include:

- Response time
- Throughput
- Availability
- Error rates
- Resource utilization

Performance targets shall be defined by implementation specifications.

------------------------------------------------------------

Reliability

Platform services shall expose health information sufficient to determine operational status.

Critical failures shall be detectable through monitoring.

------------------------------------------------------------

Artificial Intelligence Observability

Every intelligence engine shall support measurement of:

- Recommendation volume
- Recommendation acceptance
- Recommendation rejection
- Confidence distribution
- Outcome effectiveness

Observability shall improve future engine quality without replacing human oversight.

------------------------------------------------------------

Privacy

Analytics shall respect platform privacy policies.

Sensitive personal information shall not be collected beyond authorized purposes.

Analytics implementations shall comply with applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

------------------------------------------------------------

Continuous Improvement

Analytics shall support iterative improvement of:

- Platform capabilities
- Operating Systems
- Intelligence engines
- User experiences
- Documentation

Measurements inform improvement but do not replace constitutional governance.

Reporting

Platform reporting should support multiple audiences, including:

- Users
- Administrators
- Platform operators
- Product teams
- Engineering teams
- Executive leadership

Reporting requirements shall be defined by future specifications.

PBOS Responsibilities

PBOS shall:

- Validate required metrics are defined.
- Verify observability requirements.
- Detect undocumented operational dependencies.
- Confirm health monitoring exists for release-blocking services.
- Report missing measurement requirements.

Constitutional Rules

Every major capability shall be measurable.

Every release-blocking service shall be observable.

Analytics shall support continuous improvement while respecting privacy and security.

Definition of Done

Observability standards established.

Analytics principles documented.

Operational measurement requirements defined.

Platform monitoring governed constitutionally.
