# Purpose

Establish the constitutional architecture governing career transitions, professional growth, reinvention, and movement between life pathways.

---

# Scope

Applies to:

- New professionals
- Career changers
- Entrepreneurs
- Former athletes
- Returning workers
- Professionals seeking advancement
- Alumni

---

# Authority

Career transitions are recognized as natural stages of growth.

The platform shall support individuals as they adapt, learn, and pursue new opportunities.

---

# Constitutional Principles

## Change Is Growth

Transitions represent opportunities for development.

---

## Skills Are Transferable

Past experiences create future value.

---

## Identity Evolves

A person's professional identity can expand and change.

---

## Preparation Reduces Uncertainty

Successful transitions require planning and support.

---

# Career Transition Lifecycle

1. Reflection
2. Goal Definition
3. Skill Assessment
4. Learning
5. Opportunity Discovery
6. Application
7. Transition
8. Advancement

---

# Transition Categories

Includes:

- Education to career
- Athletics to career
- Career change
- Entrepreneurship
- Leadership transition
- Retirement transition

---

# Responsibilities

The career transition system shall:

- Identify transferable skills.
- Recommend learning opportunities.
- Connect users with networks.
- Support career planning.
- Preserve professional history.

---

# Validation Rules

Systems shall:

- Maintain accurate experience records.
- Avoid limiting future recommendations.
- Explain suggested pathways.
- Protect professional information.

---

# Compliance Requirements

Integrates with:

- Resume Intelligence
- Opportunity Architecture
- Learning Architecture
- Community Architecture

---

# Implementation Guidance

Career transition systems should focus on translating experiences into future opportunities.

---

# Definition of Done

Every individual can navigate career change with clarity, preparation, support, and access to opportunity.

---

# Future Amendments

Future versions may include:

- AI career transition coaching
- Workforce forecasting
- Skill translation engines
- Global professional mobility
