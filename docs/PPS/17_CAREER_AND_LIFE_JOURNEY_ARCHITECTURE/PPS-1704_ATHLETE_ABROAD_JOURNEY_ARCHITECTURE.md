# Purpose

Establish the constitutional architecture supporting athletes who pursue international athletic careers, including professional opportunities abroad, cultural adaptation, financial planning, and eventual transitions.

---

# Scope

Applies to:

- Professional athletes abroad
- International athletes
- Coaches
- Agents
- Athletic organizations
- International partners

---

# Authority

International athletic experiences are recognized as meaningful professional and personal development journeys.

The platform shall support athletes before, during, and after international competition.

---

# Constitutional Principles

## Global Opportunity

Athletes should have access to international pathways when aligned with their goals.

---

## Cultural Awareness

International experiences require preparation beyond athletic performance.

---

## Safety and Support

Athletes require reliable support networks while abroad.

---

## Transition Preparation

International careers eventually change.

Preparation must begin early.

---

# Athlete Abroad Journey Stages

## Preparation

Includes:

- Opportunity evaluation
- Contract understanding
- Financial preparation
- Cultural readiness

---

## International Experience

Includes:

- Performance tracking
- Community connection
- Personal development
- Professional growth

---

## Transition Home

Includes:

- Career planning
- Network activation
- Skill translation
- Alumni engagement

---

# Responsibilities

The athlete abroad system shall:

- Support international readiness.
- Preserve career history.
- Connect athletes with resources.
- Encourage financial literacy.
- Support post-athletic transitions.

---

# Validation Rules

Systems shall:

- Verify opportunities.
- Protect athlete information.
- Maintain accurate timelines.
- Support international privacy requirements.

---

# Compliance Requirements

Integrates with:

- Opportunity Architecture
- Career Architecture
- Community Architecture
- Financial Empowerment Architecture

---

# Implementation Guidance

The system should help athletes translate international experiences into lifelong assets.

---

# Definition of Done

Athletes abroad have structured support before, during, and after international athletic careers.

---

# Future Amendments

Future versions may include:

- International athlete networks
- Global contract intelligence
- International relocation support
- Global alumni communities
