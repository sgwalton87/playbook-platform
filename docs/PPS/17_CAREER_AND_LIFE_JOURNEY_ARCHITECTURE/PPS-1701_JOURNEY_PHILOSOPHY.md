# Purpose

Define the constitutional philosophy governing how Playbook understands, supports, and guides human journeys.

---

# Authority

Playbook recognizes that success is not a destination but an evolving journey shaped by experiences, relationships, learning, opportunities, and decisions.

---

# Constitutional Principles

## Everyone Has a Journey

Every person begins from a different starting point.

---

## Context Matters

Guidance should consider:

- Background
- Goals
- Experiences
- Strengths
- Challenges
- Aspirations

---

## Progress Is Personal

Success should be measured through meaningful growth.

---

## Reflection Creates Wisdom

Past experiences provide insight for future decisions.

---

## Support Networks Matter

People succeed through relationships.

---

# Responsibilities

Journey systems shall:

- Encourage reflection.
- Celebrate milestones.
- Support transitions.
- Identify next steps.
- Preserve growth history.

---

# Validation Rules

Journey experiences shall:

- Respect individual goals.
- Avoid assumptions.
- Preserve personal agency.
- Maintain transparency.

---

# Definition of Done

Every journey experience reflects a human-centered philosophy focused on growth, opportunity, and lifelong development.
