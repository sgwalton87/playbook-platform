# Purpose

Establish the constitutional governance framework overseeing every journey experience throughout the Playbook Platform.

Journey Governance ensures that every pathway, transition, milestone, recommendation, and support system remains aligned with the mission of empowering individuals throughout their lives.

---

# Scope

Applies to:

- Scholar journeys
- Scholar-athlete journeys
- Athlete transitions
- College transitions
- Career transitions
- Entrepreneurship journeys
- Alumni journeys
- Lifelong learning pathways
- Future journey systems

---

# Authority

The Playbook Platform shall support human development without prescribing a single definition of success.

Users maintain ownership of their goals, choices, and future direction.

---

# Constitutional Principles

## Human Agency

The platform guides but does not control.

---

## Continuous Growth

Every journey remains capable of change, improvement, and reinvention.

---

## Context-Aware Support

Guidance should consider:

- Personal goals
- Experiences
- Relationships
- Skills
- Opportunities
- Circumstances

---

## Transparent Intelligence

AI and recommendation systems must explain journey guidance.

---

## Long-Term Stewardship

The platform should support individuals beyond short-term milestones.

---

# Governance Responsibilities

Journey Governance shall:

- Maintain journey standards.
- Protect user agency.
- Review journey frameworks.
- Ensure ethical recommendations.
- Preserve historical continuity.
- Monitor journey effectiveness.
- Approve future journey models.

---

# Journey Governance Lifecycle

## 1. Journey Design

Define:

- Purpose
- Users
- Milestones
- Success indicators
- Support systems

---

## 2. Validation

Evaluate:

- Accessibility
- User experience
- Data requirements
- Safety considerations

---

## 3. Deployment

Release journey experiences through approved PBOS processes.

---

## 4. Measurement

Review:

- Engagement
- Progress
- Outcomes
- User feedback

---

## 5. Improvement

Continuously refine journey experiences.

---

# Validation Rules

Journey governance systems shall:

- Preserve user ownership.
- Maintain accurate journey history.
- Validate milestone definitions.
- Document recommendations.
- Preserve audit records.
- Prevent harmful assumptions.

---

# Compliance Requirements

Journey Governance shall integrate with:

- Constitutional Governance
- Community Governance
- Opportunity Governance
- Learning Governance
- Intelligence Governance
- Data Governance

---

# Implementation Guidance

Engineering teams shall avoid creating rigid pathways that assume every person follows the same timeline.

The platform should create navigation systems, not predetermined lives.

---

# Definition of Done

Journey Governance is complete when every Playbook journey experience is:

- Human-centered.
- Transparent.
- Adaptable.
- Measurable.
- Ethical.
- Constitutionally compliant.

---

# Future Amendments

Future versions may include:

- Global journey standards

- Multi-generational journey systems
- Life planning intelligence
- Digital legacy pathways
