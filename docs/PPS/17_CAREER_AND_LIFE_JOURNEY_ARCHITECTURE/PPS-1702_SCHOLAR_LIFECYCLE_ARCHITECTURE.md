# Purpose

Establish the constitutional architecture governing the complete scholar journey from early development through lifelong achievement.

---

# Scope

Applies to:

- K-12 scholars
- College students
- Adult learners
- Returning learners
- Alumni scholars

---

# Authority

The scholar journey shall support academic growth, personal development, opportunity access, and lifelong learning.

---

# Constitutional Principles

## Scholars Are Whole People

Academic progress exists alongside identity, relationships, interests, and aspirations.

---

## Preparation Creates Possibility

The platform shall help scholars understand and pursue future pathways.

---

## Every Experience Matters

Activities, leadership, service, athletics, and personal growth contribute to the complete record.

---

# Scholar Journey Stages

1. Exploration
2. Preparation
3. Application
4. Transition
5. Achievement
6. Leadership
7. Alumni Contribution

---

# Responsibilities

The scholar lifecycle system shall:

- Track milestones.
- Support academic planning.
- Connect opportunities.
- Preserve achievements.
- Encourage growth.

---

# Validation Rules

Scholar journey systems shall:

- Maintain record accuracy.
- Preserve learner ownership.
- Support changing goals.
- Maintain historical continuity.

---

# Definition of Done

Every scholar has a continuous pathway from exploration through lifelong contribution.
