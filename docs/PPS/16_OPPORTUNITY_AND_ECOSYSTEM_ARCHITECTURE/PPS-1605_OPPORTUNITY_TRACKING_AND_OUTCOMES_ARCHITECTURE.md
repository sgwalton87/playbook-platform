# Purpose

Establish the constitutional architecture governing opportunity progress tracking, outcomes measurement, historical records, and long-term impact analysis.

---

# Scope

Applies to:

- Applications
- Offers
- Acceptances
- Scholarships
- Employment
- Internships
- Recruiting
- Fellowships
- Programs
- Entrepreneurship milestones

---

# Authority

Opportunity tracking exists to support users and measure ecosystem impact while preserving privacy and individual ownership.

---

# Constitutional Principles

## Progress Visibility

Users should understand where they are in every opportunity journey.

---

## Historical Continuity

Past opportunities contribute to future growth.

---

## Outcome Learning

Successes and setbacks should improve future guidance.

---

## Privacy Protection

Outcome data must remain appropriately protected.

---

# Opportunity Lifecycle Tracking

1. Discovered
2. Reviewed
3. Preparing
4. Applied
5. Submitted
6. Interviewing
7. Accepted
8. Declined
9. Completed
10. Archived

---

# Responsibilities

The tracking architecture shall:

- Monitor progress.
- Preserve history.
- Support reminders.
- Measure outcomes.
- Improve recommendations.

---

# Validation Rules

Tracking systems shall:

- Preserve status history.
- Validate timeline information.
- Protect sensitive outcomes.
- Maintain accurate records.

---

# Compliance Requirements

Opportunity tracking shall integrate with:

- Scholar Record
- Analytics Architecture
- Recommendation Systems
- Notifications
- Community Systems

---

# Implementation Guidance

Outcome tracking should measure meaningful growth rather than only final acceptance.

A declined opportunity may still produce valuable learning, relationships, and future preparation.

---

# Definition of Done

Every opportunity journey can be understood, supported, and improved through transparent lifecycle tracking.

---

# Future Amendments

Future versions may include:

- Opportunity success forecasting
- Alumni outcome networks
- Career pathway analytics
- Ecosystem impact measurement
