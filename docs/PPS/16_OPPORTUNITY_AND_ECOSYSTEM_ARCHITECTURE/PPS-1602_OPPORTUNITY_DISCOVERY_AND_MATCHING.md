# Purpose

Establish the constitutional framework governing how Playbook discovers, evaluates, ranks, and recommends opportunities to users.

---

# Scope

Applies to:

- Scholarships
- Internships
- Jobs
- Athletic opportunities
- Programs
- Fellowships
- Grants
- Mentorship opportunities
- Educational pathways
- Entrepreneurial opportunities

---

# Authority

Matching systems shall assist users in discovering relevant opportunities while preserving transparency and personal decision-making.

---

# Constitutional Principles

## Explainable Matching

Users should understand why opportunities appear.

---

## Personalized Discovery

Recommendations should reflect:

- Goals
- Experiences
- Skills
- Interests
- Progress
- Preferences

---

## Fair Opportunity Exposure

Matching systems shall avoid unfair exclusion.

---

## Continuous Improvement

Opportunity recommendations should improve through feedback.

---

# Matching Architecture

The matching system consists of:

- Opportunity Database
- User Profile Data
- Eligibility Engine
- Recommendation Engine
- Ranking System
- Feedback Loop
- Explainability Layer

---

# Matching Inputs

Possible inputs include:

- Academic interests
- Career goals
- Athletic profile
- Skills
- Certifications
- Location
- Availability
- Experience
- Preferences
- Community involvement

---

# Responsibilities

The matching architecture shall:

- Identify relevant opportunities.
- Explain recommendations.
- Highlight requirements.
- Suggest preparation steps.
- Track outcomes.

---

# Validation Rules

Matching systems shall:

- Prevent unsupported recommendations.
- Maintain recommendation provenance.
- Preserve fairness testing.
- Allow user feedback.

---

# Compliance Requirements

Opportunity matching shall comply with:

- AI Governance
- Privacy Standards
- Data Governance
- Community Trust Standards

---

# Implementation Guidance

Matching should prioritize meaningful alignment rather than maximizing the number of recommendations shown.

---

# Definition of Done

Opportunity discovery successfully connects users with relevant, verified pathways while preserving transparency and agency.

---

# Future Amendments

Future versions may include:

- AI opportunity assistants
- Predictive pathway modeling
- Real-time opportunity alerts
- Global opportunity matching
