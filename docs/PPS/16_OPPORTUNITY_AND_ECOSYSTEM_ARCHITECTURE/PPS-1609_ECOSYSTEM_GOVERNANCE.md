# Purpose

Establish the constitutional governance framework overseeing opportunity providers, partnerships, opportunity quality, ecosystem relationships, and long-term impact throughout the Playbook Platform.

---

# Scope

Applies to:

- Universities
- Employers
- Scholarship providers
- Organizations
- Athletic programs
- Community partners
- Opportunity providers
- Ecosystem administrators

---

# Authority

Every opportunity relationship must maintain alignment with Playbook values, safety standards, and constitutional requirements.

---

# Constitutional Principles

- Trustworthy partnerships.
- Verified opportunities.
- Shared mission.
- Transparency.
- Accountability.
- Continuous improvement.

---

# Governance Responsibilities

The ecosystem governance framework shall:

- Validate partners.
- Monitor opportunity quality.
- Maintain standards.
- Review relationships.
- Measure outcomes.
- Protect users.

---

# Governance Lifecycle

1. Partner Evaluation
2. Verification
3. Integration
4. Monitoring
5. Outcome Review
6. Renewal
7. Retirement

---

# Validation Rules

Governance systems shall:

- Maintain partner records.
- Verify opportunities.
- Track outcomes.
- Preserve audit history.
- Document decisions.

---

# Compliance Requirements

Opportunity governance shall integrate with:

- Security Governance
- Community Governance
- Identity Systems
- Recommendation Systems

---

# Implementation Guidance

Partnership growth should prioritize mission alignment and user impact over volume of partnerships.

---

# Definition of Done

The opportunity ecosystem operates as a trusted network connecting users with verified pathways while maintaining constitutional oversight.

---

# Future Amendments

Future versions may include:

- Global ecosystem federation
- Partner marketplaces
- Impact scoring
- Automated partner intelligence
