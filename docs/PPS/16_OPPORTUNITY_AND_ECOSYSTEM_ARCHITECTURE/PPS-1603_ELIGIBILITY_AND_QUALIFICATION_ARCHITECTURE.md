# Purpose

Establish the constitutional architecture governing how Playbook evaluates opportunity eligibility, qualification requirements, readiness indicators, and preparation pathways.

The Eligibility Architecture ensures users understand not only whether they qualify for an opportunity, but what steps they can take to become competitive.

---

# Scope

Applies to:

- Scholarships
- College admissions
- Athletic recruiting
- Internships
- Fellowships
- Employment opportunities
- Grants
- Programs
- Certifications
- Competitions
- Entrepreneurship opportunities

---

# Authority

Eligibility systems shall provide guidance and transparency without making final decisions on behalf of external institutions.

External organizations retain authority over final selection decisions.

---

# Constitutional Principles

## Transparency

Users shall understand requirements and qualifications.

---

## Preparation Over Exclusion

The system should identify pathways toward readiness rather than simply identify barriers.

---

## Explainable Evaluation

Users should understand why they do or do not appear qualified.

---

## Growth Orientation

Missing qualifications represent opportunities for development.

---

## Respect External Authority

Playbook assists preparation but does not replace institutional decision-making.

---

# Eligibility Architecture

The eligibility framework consists of:

- Requirement Registry
- User Profile Data
- Qualification Rules
- Readiness Assessment
- Gap Analysis
- Preparation Recommendations
- Verification Records

---

# Qualification Categories

May include:

- Academic requirements
- Athletic requirements
- Experience requirements
- Skill requirements
- Geographic requirements
- Financial requirements
- Application requirements
- Deadline requirements

---

# Responsibilities

The eligibility system shall:

- Identify requirements.
- Compare user readiness.
- Highlight gaps.
- Recommend preparation actions.
- Preserve evaluation history.

---

# Validation Rules

Eligibility systems shall:

- Preserve source requirements.
- Identify requirement origin.
- Avoid unsupported conclusions.
- Maintain evaluation transparency.

---

# Compliance Requirements

Eligibility systems shall integrate with:

- Canonical Scholar Record
- Learning Architecture
- Recommendation Architecture
- Opportunity Registry

---

# Implementation Guidance

Eligibility engines should avoid binary thinking.

A user who does not currently qualify should receive a roadmap toward qualification whenever possible.

---

# Definition of Done

Every opportunity evaluation clearly communicates requirements, readiness, gaps, and possible next steps.

---

# Future Amendments

Future versions may include:

- Predictive readiness modeling
- Dynamic qualification pathways
- Institutional requirement synchronization
- Global eligibility standards
