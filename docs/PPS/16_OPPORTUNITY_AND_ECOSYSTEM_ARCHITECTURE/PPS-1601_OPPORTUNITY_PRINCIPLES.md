# Purpose

Define the constitutional principles governing how opportunities are discovered, evaluated, recommended, accessed, and measured throughout the Playbook Platform.

---

# Authority

Opportunity systems exist to expand access, reduce information barriers, and connect people with pathways aligned to their goals.

---

# Constitutional Principles

## Access Before Advantage

Every person deserves the ability to discover opportunities regardless of existing networks.

---

## Readiness Matters

Opportunity matching should consider preparation, not only eligibility.

---

## Context Matters

The best opportunity is not always the largest opportunity.

Matching should consider:

- Goals
- Location
- Timing
- Interests
- Skills
- Circumstances
- Aspirations

---

## Verified Over Viral

The platform prioritizes trustworthy opportunities over popularity.

---

## Preparation Creates Success

The platform should help users become competitive applicants.

---

## Long-Term Growth

Opportunity systems should support lifelong advancement, not one-time transactions.

---

# Opportunity Values

Every opportunity experience should promote:

- Access
- Preparation
- Growth
- Connection
- Achievement
- Reflection

---

# Responsibilities

Opportunity systems shall:

- Reduce information gaps.
- Explain requirements.
- Support preparation.
- Encourage exploration.
- Preserve user choice.

---

# Validation Rules

Opportunity recommendations shall:

- Include supporting information.
- Identify eligibility.
- Explain relevance.
- Preserve transparency.

---

# Compliance Requirements

All opportunity systems shall operate according to fairness, transparency, and user-centered design.

---

# Definition of Done

Opportunity principles are implemented when every recommendation supports informed, empowered decision-making.

---

# Future Amendments

Future revisions may include:

- Opportunity equity measurement
- Community opportunity guarantees
- Global access standards
- Opportunity impact scoring
