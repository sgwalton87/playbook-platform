# Purpose

Establish the constitutional architecture governing career exploration, workforce opportunities, internships, employment pathways, and professional ecosystem connections throughout the Playbook Platform.

---

# Scope

Applies to:

- Career exploration
- Internships
- Apprenticeships
- Jobs
- Fellowships
- Employer partnerships
- Professional networking
- Workforce development programs
- Entrepreneurship pathways

---

# Authority

Career systems shall help users understand, prepare for, and pursue meaningful professional pathways while preserving individual goals and agency.

---

# Constitutional Principles

## Purpose-Driven Careers

Career development begins with understanding personal strengths, values, and aspirations.

---

## Preparation Before Placement

Users should develop skills and evidence before entering professional opportunities.

---

## Relationship-Based Growth

Professional success is strengthened through mentorship and networks.

---

## Lifelong Development

Career journeys continue throughout life.

---

# Career Architecture

The ecosystem includes:

- Career Exploration
- Skill Mapping
- Resume Intelligence
- Portfolio Development
- Internship Matching
- Employer Connections
- Career Tracking
- Alumni Networks

---

# Responsibilities

Career systems shall:

- Connect users with opportunities.
- Identify skill development.
- Support preparation.
- Track career progress.
- Strengthen employer relationships.

---

# Validation Rules

Systems shall:

- Verify employers.
- Preserve opportunity details.
- Explain recommendations.
- Protect user information.

---

# Compliance Requirements

Career systems shall integrate with:

- Learning Architecture
- Resume Intelligence
- Community Architecture
- Opportunity Matching

---

# Implementation Guidance

Career technology should focus on helping people build meaningful futures rather than simply matching keywords.

---

# Definition of Done

Every user has access to career discovery, preparation, relationships, and measurable workforce pathways.

---

# Future Amendments

Future versions may include:

- AI career coaching
- Workforce forecasting
- Employer talent pipelines
- Global career marketplaces
