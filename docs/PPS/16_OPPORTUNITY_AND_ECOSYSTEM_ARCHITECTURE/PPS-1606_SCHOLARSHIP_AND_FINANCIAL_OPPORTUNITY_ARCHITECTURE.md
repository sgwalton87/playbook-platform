# Purpose

Establish the constitutional architecture governing scholarships, grants, financial aid opportunities, educational funding pathways, and financial resource discovery throughout the Playbook Platform.

The purpose of this architecture is to reduce financial barriers by connecting learners with verified resources while helping them understand preparation, eligibility, deadlines, and application requirements.

---

# Scope

Applies to:

- Scholarships
- Grants
- Fellowships
- Financial aid programs
- Tuition assistance
- Educational funding
- Community resources
- Employer education benefits
- Athletic scholarships
- Institutional financial opportunities

---

# Authority

Financial opportunity systems shall provide access, education, and guidance while preserving user choice and respecting the authority of funding organizations.

Playbook does not guarantee awards or replace official financial aid decisions.

---

# Constitutional Principles

## Financial Access Is Educational Access

Financial barriers should not prevent capable learners from pursuing opportunity.

---

## Transparency

Users should understand:

- Eligibility
- Deadlines
- Requirements
- Application steps
- Funding source

---

## Preparation Creates Opportunity

Users should receive guidance to strengthen applications and readiness.

---

## Verified Resources

Financial opportunities must originate from trustworthy sources.

---

## Empowerment Through Knowledge

Users should understand financial options and decisions.

---

# Scholarship Architecture

The scholarship ecosystem includes:

- Opportunity Registry
- Eligibility Matching
- Deadline Tracking
- Application Support
- Financial Education
- Award Tracking
- Resource Library

---

# Responsibilities

The scholarship architecture shall:

- Discover opportunities.
- Verify sources.
- Match users.
- Explain requirements.
- Track applications.
- Support financial literacy.

---

# Validation Rules

Systems shall:

- Verify opportunity legitimacy.
- Maintain accurate deadlines.
- Preserve eligibility requirements.
- Protect financial information.
- Avoid misleading claims.

---

# Compliance Requirements

Financial opportunity systems shall integrate with:

- Scholar Record
- Financial Literacy Architecture
- Recommendation Engines
- Application Support
- Notifications

---

# Implementation Guidance

Financial opportunity systems should prioritize education alongside discovery.

A successful experience teaches users how to navigate financial systems, not merely submit applications.

---

# Definition of Done

Every learner can discover, understand, prepare for, and track financial opportunities through a transparent and trusted system.

---

# Future Amendments

Future versions may include:

- Scholarship application automation
- Institutional financial aid integrations
- AI financial opportunity advisors
- Global education funding networks
