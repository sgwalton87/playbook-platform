# Goals and Milestones

## Purpose

Preserve learner-owned goals and progress milestones.

## Supported Goals

Includes:

- academic;
- athletic;
- career;
- entrepreneurship;
- personal development;
- community.

## Governance

Goals remain person-owned.

PBOS provides guidance but does not determine life outcomes.

## Definition of Done

Goal and milestone domain established.
