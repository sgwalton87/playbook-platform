# Career and College Profile

## Purpose

The Career and College Profile supports lifelong educational and professional development.

## Supported Information

Includes:

- interests;
- goals;
- career exploration;
- college pathways;
- professional experiences;
- entrepreneurship.

## Governance

PBOS provides guidance and organization.

PBOS does not make employment, admissions, or career decisions.

## Definition of Done

Career and college profile established.
