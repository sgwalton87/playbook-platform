# Canonical Scholar Record

## Purpose

The Canonical Scholar Record establishes the authoritative lifelong learner record within Playbook.

The Scholar Record preserves the learner's educational journey, accomplishments, experiences, goals, credentials, growth, and development.

## Ownership

The Scholar owns their record.

PBOS operates as steward.

Institutions, organizations, mentors, coaches, and partners contribute authorized information but do not own the learner record.

## Record Principles

The Scholar Record shall:

- maintain one canonical learner identity;
- prevent duplicate records;
- preserve longitudinal history;
- support all Operating Systems;
- support intelligence engines;
- maintain provenance and evidence.

## Supported Information Domains

The Scholar Record connects:

- Academic Profile;
- Athletics Profile;
- Career Profile;
- Achievements;
- Credentials;
- Portfolio;
- Goals;
- Milestones;
- Privacy Preferences.

## Governance

The Scholar Record cannot be sold, transferred, ranked, or controlled by external organizations.

## Definition of Done

Canonical Scholar Record authority established.
