# Privacy and Sharing

## Purpose

Govern learner ownership, privacy, consent, and authorized sharing.

## Principles

The learner controls:

- visibility;
- sharing;
- permissions;
- connections.

## Governance

PBOS defaults information to private.

External access requires authorization and consent.

## Definition of Done

Privacy and sharing authority established.
