# Purpose

The Engine Governance specification establishes the constitutional rules governing the evolution, operation, oversight, and accountability of the PBOS Engine.

---

# Scope

Applies to every PBOS Engine module, constitutional amendment, operational policy, governance decision, and autonomous capability.

---

# Authority

Engine Governance is the highest operational authority for the PBOS Engine after the Playbook Constitution.

---

# Definitions

## Governance

The constitutional framework that directs and constrains PBOS behavior.

## Governance Policy

A formally adopted operational rule.

## Constitutional Amendment

An approved modification to the Playbook Platform Specification.

---

# Constitutional Principles

- The Constitution is supreme.
- Governance is transparent.
- Governance decisions are auditable.
- Human authority is preserved.
- PBOS continuously improves while maintaining constitutional compatibility.

---

# Architecture

Engine Governance consists of:

- Governance Registry
- Constitutional Rule Registry
- Policy Manager
- Amendment Manager
- Compliance Monitor

---

# Governance Lifecycle

1. Propose
2. Review
3. Validate
4. Approve
5. Publish
6. Monitor
7. Amend

---

# Responsibilities

Engine Governance shall:

- Preserve constitutional integrity.
- Manage governance policies.
- Review amendments.
- Monitor compliance.
- Record governance history.
- Coordinate constitutional evolution.

---

# Interfaces

Coordinates with every PBOS Engine module, Runtime component, and Constitutional specification.

---

# Validation Rules

Engine Governance shall:

- Reject unconstitutional changes.
- Preserve amendment history.
- Require formal approval.
- Maintain governance traceability.

---

# Compliance Requirements

Governance activities shall remain transparent, auditable, reproducible, and constitutionally compliant.

---

# Implementation Guidance

Governance policies should evolve through documented constitutional amendments rather than ad hoc operational changes.

---

# Definition of Done

Engine Governance is complete when the PBOS Engine operates under transparent, auditable, constitutionally enforced governance.

---

# Future Amendments

Future versions may introduce distributed governance, delegated governance authorities, constitutional voting systems, and enterprise governance profiles.
