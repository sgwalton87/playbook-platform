# Purpose

Define the constitutional protection of human identities, organizational identities, service identities, and digital credentials.

---

# Constitutional Principles

- One identity.
- Verified identity.
- Federated identity.
- Recoverable identity.
- Auditable identity.

---

# Responsibilities

The Identity Security architecture shall:

- Verify identities.
- Protect credentials.
- Prevent impersonation.
- Support recovery.
- Preserve audit history.

---

# Validation Rules

- Reject unverifiable identities.
- Require secure credential management.
- Preserve identity provenance.

---

# Definition of Done

Every platform identity is securely managed throughout its lifecycle.
