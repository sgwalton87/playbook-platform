# Purpose

The Partner and Enterprise Integration specification governs constitutional collaboration between the Playbook Platform and enterprise partners.

---

# Scope

Applies to school districts, universities, nonprofits, employers, corporations, government agencies, community organizations, enterprise customers, and strategic partners.

---

# Authority

Enterprise integrations shall respect constitutional governance, organizational boundaries, and canonical ownership.

---

# Definitions

## Enterprise Partner

An organization authorized to integrate with PBOS.

## Partner Workspace

An isolated environment supporting enterprise collaboration.

---

# Constitutional Principles

- Organizations never own scholar truth.
- Organizational access is permission-based.
- Data sharing is explicit.
- Organizational boundaries are enforced.
- Enterprise integrations remain auditable.

---

# Architecture

The enterprise integration architecture consists of:

- Organization Registry
- Partner Gateway
- Workspace Manager
- Permission Engine
- Audit Services

---

# Responsibilities

The enterprise architecture shall:

- Manage partner organizations.
- Enforce organizational permissions.
- Support multi-tenant collaboration.
- Protect scholar privacy.
- Preserve governance.

---

# Validation Rules

- Reject unauthorized organizations.
- Validate organizational identity.
- Preserve tenant isolation.
- Maintain audit history.

---

# Compliance Requirements

Enterprise integrations shall satisfy constitutional governance, privacy, security, and interoperability requirements.

---

# Implementation Guidance

Enterprise features should scale without changing constitutional ownership or scholar protections.

---

# Definition of Done

Enterprise organizations collaborate securely while constitutional ownership remains unchanged.

---

# Future Amendments

Future versions may support federated organizations, cross-institution collaboration, delegated administration, and international partnerships.
