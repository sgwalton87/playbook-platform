# Purpose

The Educational Systems Integration specification governs constitutional interaction between the Playbook Platform and educational institutions, academic systems, and educational service providers.

---

# Scope

Applies to student information systems (SIS), learning management systems (LMS), FAFSA, UC A-G, College Board, ACT, NCAA, NAIA, universities, scholarship providers, transcript systems, and future educational platforms.

---

# Authority

Educational integrations shall enrich the Canonical Scholar Record without replacing constitutional ownership.

---

# Definitions

## Educational System

An external platform managing educational information.

## Academic Integration

A governed exchange of educational information.

---

# Constitutional Principles

- Scholar Record remains canonical.
- Educational evidence is traceable.
- Institutional boundaries are respected.
- Academic integrity is preserved.
- Student privacy is protected.

---

# Architecture

The educational integration architecture consists of:

- SIS Connectors
- LMS Connectors
- Transcript Services
- Assessment Services
- Scholarship Services
- Academic Validation

---

# Responsibilities

The educational integration layer shall:

- Synchronize verified educational data.
- Validate institutional records.
- Preserve provenance.
- Protect student privacy.
- Support lifelong educational history.

---

# Validation Rules

- Reject unverified academic records.
- Preserve institutional attribution.
- Prevent duplicate educational records.
- Maintain educational provenance.

---

# Compliance Requirements

Educational integrations shall satisfy constitutional privacy, security, and educational governance requirements.

---

# Implementation Guidance

Institutional systems may evolve independently while preserving constitutional scholar ownership.

---

# Definition of Done

Educational systems integrate reliably while preserving lifelong canonical scholar records.

---

# Future Amendments

Future versions may support international education standards, digital credentials, competency frameworks, and lifelong learning ecosystems.
