# Purpose

The Integration Governance specification establishes the constitutional framework governing the approval, operation, monitoring, evolution, certification, and retirement of every Playbook Platform integration.

---

# Scope

Applies to every API, connector, webhook, messaging system, enterprise integration, educational integration, external service, and future integration technology.

---

# Authority

Every integration shall operate under constitutional governance throughout its lifecycle.

---

# Definitions

## Integration Governance

The constitutional oversight of all integrations.

## Integration Steward

The constitutional owner responsible for an integration.

## Integration Lifecycle

The complete progression from proposal through retirement.

---

# Constitutional Principles

- Governance is transparent.
- Integrations remain replaceable.
- Contracts govern behavior.
- Ownership is explicit.
- Every integration is auditable.

---

# Governance Lifecycle

1. Proposal
2. Review
3. Validation
4. Certification
5. Deployment
6. Monitoring
7. Versioning
8. Deprecation
9. Retirement

---

# Responsibilities

The governance framework shall:

- Approve integrations.
- Maintain integration standards.
- Govern version evolution.
- Preserve interoperability.
- Monitor operational health.
- Maintain constitutional compliance.

---

# Validation Rules

- Reject undocumented integrations.
- Reject incompatible contracts.
- Preserve governance history.
- Require constitutional certification.

---

# Compliance Requirements

Every integration shall remain secure, interoperable, auditable, versioned, and constitutionally governed.

---

# Implementation Guidance

Operational processes may evolve without changing constitutional governance principles.

---

# Definition of Done

Every integration operates under transparent constitutional governance throughout its lifecycle.

---

# Future Amendments

Future versions may introduce autonomous governance agents, policy engines, and distributed partner governance.
