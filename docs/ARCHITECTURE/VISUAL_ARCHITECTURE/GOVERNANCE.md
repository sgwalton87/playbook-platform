# Playbook Visual Architecture Governance

## Purpose

This document establishes the constitutional governance model for all Visual Architecture within the Playbook Platform.

Visual Architecture shall be governed with the same rigor as source code.

Every blueprint is a governed architectural asset.

---

# Mission

Ensure that every Master Blueprint evolves through transparent governance, deterministic review, constitutional inheritance, and controlled certification.

Architecture shall be intentional.

Architecture shall never drift.

---

# Governance Principles

Visual Architecture shall be:

- Constitutional
- Traceable
- Reviewable
- Deterministic
- Versioned
- Auditable
- Backward Compatible where practical
- AI Readable

---

# Architectural Authority

Authority flows downward.

Constitutional Architecture

↓

Visual Architecture

↓

Golden Screen Library

↓

Component Architecture

↓

Screen Architecture

↓

Engineering Implementation

Lower layers may extend higher layers.

Lower layers shall not redefine higher-layer authority.

---

# Blueprint Ownership

Every Master Blueprint shall identify:

- Constitutional Owner
- Business Owner
- Product Owner
- Experience Owner
- Engineering Owner
- Design System Owner

Ownership shall be explicit.

---

# Architectural Decisions

Major architectural decisions shall be documented through Architecture Decision Records (ADRs).

Each ADR shall include:

- Decision
- Context
- Alternatives Considered
- Rationale
- Consequences
- Approval Date
- Approvers
- Superseded Decisions

No significant architectural change shall occur without an ADR.

---

# Versioning

Blueprints shall use semantic versioning.

Major

Breaking architectural changes.

Minor

Backward-compatible architectural enhancements.

Patch

Documentation corrections and clarifications.

---

# Change Categories

Changes are classified as:

- Editorial
- Clarification
- Enhancement
- Structural
- Constitutional

Constitutional changes require PBOS approval.

---

# Certification Lifecycle

Every blueprint progresses through:

Concept

↓

Architectural Draft

↓

Architecturally Complete

↓

Engineering Complete

↓

PBOS Certified

↓

Golden Certified

↓

Reference Masterpiece (Rare)

Each stage requires documented evidence.

---

# Review Requirements

Golden Certification requires multidisciplinary review from:

- Product Architecture
- UX Architecture
- Information Architecture
- Accessibility
- Engineering
- Security
- Performance
- PBOS Governance

---

# Architectural Exceptions

Exceptions shall be:

- Documented
- Time bounded
- Risk assessed
- Approved
- Traceable

Temporary exceptions shall not become permanent architecture.

---

# Deprecation Policy

Blueprints may be deprecated when:

- Replaced by superior architecture
- Constitutional conflicts emerge
- Platform evolution requires retirement

Deprecated blueprints remain archived for traceability.

---

# AI Governance

AI implementation agents shall:

- Preserve constitutional intent
- Reference canonical blueprints
- Avoid architectural invention
- Produce traceable implementation decisions
- Escalate ambiguity instead of guessing

---

# PBOS Responsibilities

PBOS shall validate:

- Blueprint inheritance
- Version consistency
- Required artifacts
- Certification status
- Traceability
- Architectural integrity

PBOS shall fail closed when governance requirements are violated.

---

# Success Criteria

The Playbook Visual Architecture shall remain a trusted, governed, and evolving architectural system that produces deterministic user experiences through transparent governance, constitutional authority, and measurable quality.
