# Playbook Company History

## Chapter 1 — Origin

Playbook began as a mission to help Scholars tell fuller stories about who they are and what they can become.

## Chapter 2 — Platform Foundation

The early platform included authentication, profiles, courses, certificates, badges, dashboards, and community features.

## Chapter 3 — The Record

The introduction of the Playbook Record™ shifted the company from building pages to building lifelong achievement infrastructure.

## Chapter 4 — The Operating System

The Playbook Graph™, Trust Layer™, Event Bus™, Engine Layer™, and Repository Layer™ established the foundation for Playbook OS™.
