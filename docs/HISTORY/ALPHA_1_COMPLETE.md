## Overview

Alpha 1.0 represents the completion of the foundational operating system for Playbook.

The goal of Alpha was never to build a collection of pages.

The goal was to build an Intelligence Operating System capable of transforming educational records into trusted guidance, opportunities, and lifelong learner intelligence.

After months of engineering, architecture, and documentation work, that foundation is now complete.

---

# Mission

Build an Education Intelligence Operating System that allows every learner to own, understand, and grow their complete learning story.
