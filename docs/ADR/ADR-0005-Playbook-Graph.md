# ADR-0005 — Playbook Graph™

## Status

Accepted

## Decision

Playbook will use a graph-style data model connecting records, achievements, evidence, trust, opportunities, timelines, and AI guidance.

## Why

Every meaningful action should strengthen multiple parts of the platform.

## Alternatives Considered

- Flat tables
- Feature-specific data silos

## Consequences

Playbook becomes compounding architecture.
